M114 RETRACTION -- measurement space contamination
==================================================

Every render-domain number produced by probes r10-r23 in this directory was
measured on TIFFs rendered with `--srgb --auto-exposure`. M113 (commit aa6e7ec)
established that this space contains two cross-channel operators that forge the
very quantity being measured:

  1. camera_to_srgb is non-diagonal. Output red carries camera GREEN at 27.4%
     of its own weight, BOTH AT ZERO DISPLACEMENT -- a synthetic zero-lag
     inter-channel signal sitting on top of the displacement being measured.
     Full leak table, each channel as a fraction of its own diagonal term,
     re-derived by M114 from the crate source and the capture's own metadata:
         green -> red   27.42%      red  -> green   7.73%
         blue  -> green 25.10%      green-> blue   24.36%
         blue  -> red   11.46%      red  -> blue    0.36%
     An earlier revision of this file quoted 10.4% and 20.3%, taken from a
     corpus value that computed rownorm(inv(A@S)). color.rs:191-208 computes
     inv(rownorm(A@S)) -- normalise THEN invert. The two do not commute. The
     wrong ordering reproduces 10.44% / 20.32% exactly, which identifies the
     mechanism rather than merely the discrepancy.
  2. Malvar-He-Cutler's gradient correction assumes R-G is smoother than either
     channel (demosaic.rs:482). A lateral-CA fringe IS R-G roughness at an edge.

main.rs:116 documents --srgb as "For looking at, NOT for measuring." M114
ignored a warning written into the tool.

RETRACTED
---------
* The "application factor" of ~1.20 (readings 1.130-1.286 across three stacks)
  and the H3 "application-path over-delivery" conclusion built on it. M113's
  transfer check reads applied/eps_r = 1.3073 in sRGB and 0.9399/0.9813 in the
  clean binned space. The factor was the colour matrix, not the code.
* The steer to M107 to hunt a quad-grid vs full-resolution radius convention.
  There is no anomaly left for it to explain.
* Fit-quality factors 0.903 / 1.040 / 0.847 (r23) and all gains derived from
  them: 1.161 / 1.279 / 0.957, and the earlier 1.241 / 1.052.
* All earlier M114 gain estimates: r10e 1.117-1.344, r10d 1.284, r11 1.225-1.416,
  consolidated "~1.28", and the proposed ~0.78 scalar.
* The blue claim that 6/7 and 4/7 rings clear the tangential noise floor, and
  that hp|B-Y| exceeds hp|R-Y| after correction. The sRGB matrix mixes blue into
  output green at 25.10% of green's own weight and green into output blue at
  24.36%, which is ample to invalidate both readings.
  CORRECTED SUPERLATIVE: an earlier revision of this file called blue "the WORST
  contaminated channel in this space", on the strength of a 34.8% figure from
  the mis-ordered corpus matrix. With the correct matrix the largest single leak
  is green -> red at 27.42%, which EXCEEDS blue -> green at 25.10%. Blue is
  heavily contaminated but it is not the worst. The retraction stands; the
  superlative did not, and it rested on a number M114 did not re-derive.
* Recovered translation |t.u| = 0.375 / 0.250 / 0.450 px (r23 Y3). The pairing
  METHOD is sound and space-independent; the numbers are not.

WHY THE CONTROLS DID NOT CATCH IT
---------------------------------
Bars W3 and X3 required the application factor to be SCENE-INDEPENDENT and it
passed at 3.4% and 7.2% spread. That was read as evidence the factor was a real
property of the code. A FIXED COLOUR MATRIX IS ALSO SCENE-INDEPENDENT.
Scene-independence cannot distinguish "property of the system under test" from
"property of my own transform", because both are constant across scenes. The
bar was green and proved nothing.

The declared constant eps_r = -704.5 ppm was present in these scripts the whole
time, as NOMINAL. applied/declared was computed on every ring. A systematic
departure from 1.0 was attributed to the system under test when it was a
calibration failure of the measurement chain.

WHAT SURVIVES
-------------
Space-independent, unaffected:
* The byte-identical null across six binaries and five commits (hashes).
* The decile decomposition being a regression-to-the-mean instrument (a
  statistical argument about binning on the outcome variable).
* W3's keep-sign bar having negative power: noise 93.0%, a strictly damaging
  wrong-sign correction 84.8%, the real correction 75.1%. All placebos traverse
  the identical transform, so the ORDERING is robust.
* support_r_max is read at exactly one site, optics.rs:853, inside a test.
* cfa_site_channel mapping, verified from RAW metadata (claim 4).
* Gear matching, verified from EXIF (claim 6).
* The solve-cache-key call graph (claim 7).
* The tangential placebo's QUALITATIVE result and the 1:1 crops: the placebo
  moves chroma hard the WRONG way while the correction moves it right, and the
  corrected dots keep their size and lose their colour. Percentages in that
  probe are space-contaminated; the sign and ordering are not.

Corroborated by M113 in the clean space:
* Scene-to-scene variation in native CA. M114 measured a bee-3-cpl to 119MSDCF
  ratio of 1.227 (22.7%) in the contaminated space; M113 measures 529.9 vs
  654.9 ppm on bee-3-cpl vs couch-fibers-cpl, 23.6%, in the clean space. A
  common-mode contaminant partly cancels in a between-scene ratio.
