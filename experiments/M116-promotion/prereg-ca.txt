0. PREMISES READ OUT OF THE SOURCE AT THE PINNED SHA
----------------------------------------------------
Discipline 808 says derive a bar's premises from the code, not from a binary's
`--help` and not from a headline figure in a report. ORCH's message #5 makes
that a standing instruction after two successive briefs rested on unqualified
headline numbers. So every premise below was read at the pinned SHA before this
file was written, and every corpus number quoted was opened at its own source
section rather than taken from the brief that cited it.

S1. The switch is `STREHL_FIX_CA` (`optics.rs`, `CA_ENV`). It takes a PROFILE
    KEY, not a truthy value: `merge.rs:3027` records that truthy values are
    refused deliberately, and `CaConfigError::UnknownProfile` refuses an
    unknown key loudly and lists the known ones. So `STREHL_FIX_CA=1` does
    NOT enable the correction — it is an error. The value must be
    `a7c2-fe100gm-2xtc-x4cpl`. An arm that set `1` would measure OFF while
    believing it measured ON; that is exactly the M97q F2 failure mode and it
    is designed out here rather than discovered later.

S2. Exactly one profile exists: `PROFILES: [&OpticalProfile; 1]`. A second
    constant, `A7C2_FE100GM_2XTC_X4CPL_RADIAL`, is defined but is NOT in
    `PROFILES` and so is not selectable by key.

S3. The correction is applied inside `align_cmd::warp_frame_in_place`
    (`align_cmd.rs:1272-1291`) via `OpticalProfile::compose`, per CFA site
    channel, at GATHER time. `merge_cmd.rs:968` calls that function, so the
    correction IS reachable from `stack`. This is the arm `outlier` and
    `robust` did not have.

S4. `active_profile()` is re-read per call, by design (`align_cmd.rs:279`), so
    the environment variable takes effect per invocation with no caching.

S5. Because CA composes ON TOP of the similarity transform inside
    `warp_frame_in_place`, supplying a frozen `STREHL_WARPMAP` freezes
    registration bit-identically while still allowing CA to vary. Registration
    therefore cannot confound this A/B — the two arms differ in the CA
    composition and in nothing else.

S6. Corpus premises, each opened at its own source section, not at the brief:
    - M110 §16.4 measures edge-localised chroma excess falling 61.0% on
      `bee-3-cpl` and 58.7% on `flat-on-ruler-cpl`. VERIFIED by reading
      M110-sheet.md:1243-1244 directly.
    - M110 §16.4 also records that half-maximum width is UNCHANGED or
      marginally wider: "it gets fainter, not thinner". Discipline 59 requires
      amplitude and extent be reported separately. Honoured by CB7.
    - M110 discipline 57: overshoot is the CONJUNCTION flip-AND-growth. A
      sign-flip bar penalises the correction for succeeding. Honoured by CB8.
    - M110 fact 56: the fitted support ends at r = 0.788; beyond it the
      correction extrapolates. Discipline 58 requires testing for a
      discontinuity AT the boundary. Honoured by reporting the in-support and
      extrapolated bands separately.

S7. The profile key names a complete optical path: A7C II + FE 100 GM + 2x TC
    + X4 CPL. The product parses no camera identity, so it will apply the
    profile to any stack. I audited every subject's EXIF before choosing any
    (`ca_gear_audit.py`). RESULT: all 23 subjects carrying raws report
    `ILCE-7CM2` and `FE 100mm F2.8 Macro GM OSS + 2X Teleconverter` at 200 mm.
    Body, lens and teleconverter are therefore CONFIRMED FROM METADATA for
    every subject.
    THE CPL IS NOT CONFIRMABLE. A filter leaves no EXIF trace. The only
    evidence that the X4 CPL was fitted is the owner's `-cpl` filename
    convention. This is the weakest link in the applicability chain and it is
    disclosed here rather than discovered afterwards. CONSEQUENCE: every
    subject in this campaign is a `-cpl` subject. No claim is made for the
    non-CPL stacks (`foam`, `coffee-bean`, `wrapped-text-*`, the `*MSDCF`
    directories), and the report must say the CPL rests on a filename.


1. WHY THIS CAMPAIGN EXISTS
---------------------------
Three campaigns have now returned negative. Photometric and soft gate failed
all 12 B2 bars; the occlusion halo failed its selectivity bar backwards at
R = 3.054; the dose ladder found two silent no-ops and a subject-dependent
detail cost. Nothing has yet been promoted.

CA is the last instrument with an independent positive measurement behind it.
It is switched OFF. If it survives, it is the answer to "those stacks don't
look too good yet". If it does not, then the honest finding of M116 is that
the default path is already the best configuration the binary can produce, and
that is a publishable result in its own right.

I am an INDEPENDENT check on M110, not a restatement of it. I therefore
reimplement the metric rather than reuse M110's, and I test two subjects M110
NEVER USED. Transfer to an unseen subject is the claim that matters: both of
M110's subjects are subjects its coefficients were measured against.

3. METRIC
---------
Chroma is measured as |R-G| and |B-G|.

NOT |R-Y| and NOT |B-Y|, which is what M110 §16.4 used. Reason, and it is a
correction to the instrument rather than a preference: Y = 0.2126R + 0.7152G +
0.0722B, so |B-Y| expands to |0.9278B - 0.2126R - 0.7152G| and carries an
EXPLICIT R TERM. For a correction whose largest coefficient is on red, a
"control" channel containing R leaks the treatment in proportion to the effect
and is quietest exactly where it is most needed. |B-G| has no R term. This is
the defect measured on the CA work itself (M110 fact 41 / discipline 48), and
repeating it here would be inexcusable.

E(band) = median |R-G| over the pixels in that band, restricted to strong
edges (top 2% of luminance gradient), which is where lateral CA lives.

Edge-localised chroma excess, following M110 §16.4's construction but on the
corrected channel pair:
    X = E(band 0) - E(band 5)          [band 5 is the far-band baseline]
    dX% = 100 * (X_off - X_on) / X_off

Radius bands, normalised r = distance from frame centre / half-diagonal:
    CENTRE       r < 0.15     the trap; CA is radial and must vanish here
    IN-SUPPORT   0.55 <= r <= 0.788    inside the fitted support (fact 56)
    EXTRAPOLATED r > 0.788             beyond it (discipline 58)

Fine-structure retention, as in campaign 3:
    D = log10( highpass amplitude ON / highpass amplitude OFF )
    measured on oriented content (structure-tensor coherence >= 0.6).
    D < 0 means the correction destroyed high-frequency content.


4. BARS
-------
Bars are fixed here. A bar I can widen after seeing the data is not a bar.
Controls are decided and reported BEFORE any positive result, as in §3 of the
report.

CB1  NULL / determinism.
     A-OFF rendered twice, cold private caches, must be byte-identical
     (SHA-256). If it is not, the renderer is non-deterministic and every
     difference below is confounded; I say so first and loudly and quantify
     the run-to-run spread before reporting anything else.
     PASS = identical digests on all four subjects.

CB2  PLACEBO.
     A-PLACEBO vs A-OFF: dX% must be EXACTLY 0.000 and the digests identical.
     If the metric moves on the placebo it is measuring my harness.
     PASS = |dX%| < 0.001 on all four subjects.

CB3  PLANT / responsiveness.  (fact 1548: a null with no plant is a dead probe)
     A null is worthless unless the instrument can express a difference on the
     same wiring. I synthesise a known lateral CA into the A-OFF linear render
     — resample R radially outward by +0.5 px about the frame centre — and run
     the identical scorer.
     PASS = the planted arm reads dX% <= -20% (i.e. the metric registers the
     planted aberration as a large WORSENING). If it cannot see a planted
     0.5 px radial red shift, the metric is dead and CB5 is void.
     PREDICTION: passes. If it fails, the campaign stops here.

CB4  TRAP.  (discipline 797: a detector needs an arm it must NOT fire on)
     Lateral CA is radial: `displacement_at(ch, radius)` -> 0 as r -> 0. So in
     the CENTRE band the correction moves nothing and dX% MUST read ~zero.
     PASS = |dX%(centre)| <= 3.0 on all four subjects.
     If the centre fires, the metric is reading a global recolour or an
     exposure/normalisation difference, NOT lateral CA, and CB5 is void
     regardless of how good the outer numbers look.
     This is the bar most likely to catch me being wrong.

CB5  PRIMARY PROMOTION BAR — transfer.
     dX% >= +25.0 in the IN-SUPPORT band on at least 3 of the 4 subjects,
     AND on at least one of the two TRANSFER subjects.
     Set at 25%, well below M110's 61.0/58.7, because those two figures come
     from subjects the coefficients were measured against and I must not
     import them as an expectation. The bar tests transfer, not replication.

CB6  DO-NO-HARM — fine structure.  Deciding bar.
     The correction RESAMPLES. Campaign 3 measured a resampling treatment
     destroying 50.1% of high-pass amplitude on one subject while improving
     its target metric on another, so this is a live risk and not a formality.
     PASS = D >= -0.02 dex (<= 4.7% high-pass loss) on EVERY subject.
     PROMOTION RULE: CB6 failing on any subject means KEEP-OFF, even if CB5
     passes everywhere. A colour fix that eats hair detail is not a win; the
     owner shoots insects.

CB7  EXTENT HONESTY.  Not a promotion bar — a caption bar.
     Report half-maximum width of the chroma profile, off vs on.
     PREDICTION, registered: unchanged, |dw| <= 0.5 px. M110 §16.4 says the
     fringe gets fainter, not thinner. If dw is materially negative I have
     contradicted M110 and must report that as the headline.
     The crops MUST NOT be captioned as sharpening either way.

CB8  OVERSHOOT.
     Rate of (chroma sign flip AND magnitude growth), gated to >= 1 display
     level, in the IN-SUPPORT band.
     PASS = <= 20%. M110 measured 9-19% by the same conjunction.
     Sign flips alone are NOT counted: driving a fringe through neutral toward
     grey is the correction succeeding (M110 discipline 57).

CB9  BOUNDARY.
     Report dX% separately for IN-SUPPORT and EXTRAPOLATED. Descriptive.
     PREDICTION: no discontinuity at r = 0.788 (M110 fact 53 disconfirmed
     radius-dependent over-correction). Recorded so it can be wrong.

6. WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG
---------------------------
- CB4 fires. The metric is not measuring lateral CA and I report a harness
  failure, not a quality result.
- CB3 fails. The metric cannot see a planted 0.5 px red shift, so its zeros
  mean nothing.
- CB5 passes on the two reproduction subjects and fails on both transfer
  subjects. That would mean the coefficients are fitted to their own subjects
  and do not generalise — which is the single most likely negative outcome
  and the one worth the campaign.
- CB6 fails. The correction buys colour with detail. Given campaign 3 found
  exactly that trade on a resampling treatment, this is a real possibility.
- dX% comes out large and NEGATIVE. The correction makes chroma worse on the
  owner's stacks, M110's numbers do not survive an independent instrument, and
  that leads the report.

I expect, honestly: CB1-CB4 pass, CB5 passes on the reproduction subjects,
and the transfer subjects are the coin toss. I am recording that expectation
so the report can convict me of it.
