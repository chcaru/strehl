WHY THIS ADDENDUM EXISTS: THE REGISTERED INSTRUMENT FAILED ITS OWN PLANT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
prereg-ca.txt registered a chroma-excess metric

    X = median|R-G|(edge band 0) - median|R-G|(far band 5)
    dX% = 100 * (X_off - X_on) / X_off

and registered CB3, a plant: displace R radially outward by 0.5 px and require
the metric to read dX% <= -20%. I predicted it would pass, and wrote "if it
fails, the campaign stops here."

IT FAILED.

    flat-on-ruler-cpl   plant reads  -16.45%   (bar: <= -20%)   FAIL
    bee-3-cpl           plant reads +104.05%   -- wrong SIGN entirely

The diagnosis, and it condemns the metric rather than the correction:

    bee-3-cpl, in-support band:  X_off = -133.0 DN

X IS NEGATIVE. The construction assumes chroma peaks at edges and decays to a
clean far-field baseline. On a bee that is false: the subject is strongly and
largely coloured, so median |R-G| five pixels from an edge is dominated by
SUBJECT COLOUR, not by fringe, and it exceeds the value at the edge. A
percentage reduction of a negative baseline is not a reduction of anything.

CONSEQUENCE, applied without negotiation: every dX% figure from campaign 4 is
VOID, including the ones that flattered the correction — bee's +35.34% and
flat-on-ruler's +66.79%. They are not reported as results. CB4 (the centre
trap) also failed, at -4.05% on bee against a +/-3.0 bar, and it is void by the
same defect: it is a ratio of the same broken quantity.

This is the fact-1548 failure mode caught in the direction it is supposed to be
caught in — by the plant, before a positive was published, rather than after.


THE REPLACEMENT, AND WHY IT CANNOT FAIL THE SAME WAY
----------------------------------------------------
Lateral chromatic aberration IS a displacement: the red image is formed at a
slightly different magnification from the green one, so red is displaced
radially with respect to green, by an amount proportional to radius. That is
what `OpticalProfile::displacement_at` returns and it is what the correction
undoes.

So measure the displacement, not a colour proxy for it.

    For each annulus of normalised radius r, in each of 8 azimuthal sectors,
    take a window of the R plane and the G plane and compute the sub-pixel
    shift of R relative to G by phase correlation at 1/100 px resolution.
    Project that shift onto the RADIAL direction of the sector.
    RD(r) = median over sectors of the radial R-vs-G displacement, in px.

Properties that matter, and each is why it replaces the proxy:
  - It cannot go negative-baseline. It is a signed displacement with a true
    zero, not a difference of two medians of an unsigned quantity.
  - It is immune to subject colour. Phase correlation keys on STRUCTURE. A
    large flat yellow region carries no registration information and cannot
    masquerade as a fringe.
  - Its units are the same units the profile is written in, so the prediction
    is quantitative rather than directional: the correction should drive RD to
    zero, and by a known amount.
  - It has a free, exact trap: at r = 0 the radial term vanishes by
    construction, so RD(centre) must read ~0 both arms.
  - It has a free, exact plant: displace R by a known 0.5 px and the
    instrument must recover 0.5 px, not merely "something".

Already established, before this addendum was written, and carried forward:
  - The applied profile is TRANSLATION-FREE. `profile_from_setting`
    (optics.rs:456-458) resolves the key `a7c2-fe100gm-2xtc-x4cpl` to
    `A7C2_FE100GM_2XTC_X4CPL_RADIAL`, whose `translation` is (0,0). The
    non-zero (0.6294, -0.4240) in the named constant is recorded as provenance
    and NOT applied.
  - Measured, independently and before that line was found: the red plane's
    displacement at the frame centre is 0.0224 px, against 0.7589 px if the
    translation were applied. The measurement corrected my source reading and
    the source then confirmed the measurement.
  - Green does not move at all: 0.0000 px on both subjects scored.


BARS — fixed here, before the instrument has produced a number
---------------------------------------------------------------
CD1  PLANT / calibration.  Deciding.
     Take the OFF render, displace R radially outward by a known +0.50 px at
     the corner, and score. The instrument must RECOVER that displacement.
     PASS = measured change in RD at the outermost annulus is within
     +/-0.10 px of the planted +0.50 px, on every subject scored.
     This is stronger than campaign 4's CB3, which only required "some large
     movement in the right direction". A calibrated instrument must return the
     number, not the sign.

CD2  TRAP.  Deciding.  (discipline 797)
     |RD(centre annulus)| <= 0.10 px in BOTH arms, on every subject.
     The applied profile is radial-only and vanishes at r = 0, so a correction
     that moves the centre is not correcting lateral CA.
     Pinned to a value fixed by the source (the radial term is exactly zero at
     r=0), not chosen from the data.

CD3  NULL.  Deciding.
     Green must not move: |GD| <= 0.02 px in every annulus, both arms.
     Green is the profile's reference channel and has no coefficient. If green
     moves, the gather is doing something the profile does not describe.

CD4  PRIMARY.  Does the correction reduce lateral CA?
     PASS = |RD_on(r)| < |RD_off(r)| in the in-support band (0.55 <= r <=
     0.788) on at least 3 of the 4 subjects, AND on at least one TRANSFER
     subject (tweezers-cpl, flower-bud-with-background-fern-cpl), which M110
     never used.

CD5  MAGNITUDE.  How much, and does it match the profile?
     Report the fraction of lateral CA removed,
         Q(r) = 1 - |RD_on(r)| / |RD_off(r)|,
     per annulus. DESCRIPTIVE, no bar, because I have no independent prior for
     it: M99's +6.88% is retracted (fact 1551) and M110's 61.0/58.7 are a
     DIFFERENT quantity (chroma amplitude, not displacement) and must not be
     read as a prediction of this one.
     Registered prediction, so it can be wrong: Q > 0.5 in the in-support band.

CD6  OVERSHOOT.
     Report the sign of RD_on. If RD_off > 0 and RD_on < 0 the correction has
     pushed red past green — over-correction, which M107/M114 dispute at
     1.06-1.25x. DESCRIPTIVE; I do not adjudicate their dispute, I only report
     what this instrument reads.

CD7  DO-NO-HARM carries over unchanged from CB6, which did NOT depend on the
     broken quantity: D = log10(highpass ON / OFF) on oriented content,
     PASS = D >= -0.02 dex on every subject, scored on R, B and luminance and
     decided on the WORST of the three.
     Campaign 4 already measured D_worst = -0.00169 (bee) and -0.00101
     (flat-on-ruler). Those stand; they are not ratios of X.

WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG
------------------------
- CD1 fails. The instrument cannot recover a planted displacement and I have
  no business reporting displacements. The campaign then ends with "no
  instrument", which is a legitimate and publishable outcome.
- CD4 passes on the two reproduction subjects and fails on both transfer
  subjects: the coefficients are fitted to their own subjects.
- Q comes out small, or RD_on flips sign: the correction is real but badly
  scaled, which would corroborate M107/M114's over-correction reading.
