WHY THIS ADDENDUM EXISTS
------------------------
ORCH's objection, in full and in its own terms: the correction works by
FORBIDDING frames within DELTA of the local focus frame inside the yoke, so the
treated neighbourhood is necessarily reconstructed from more defocused frames.
The treatment therefore blurs the exact region in which the halo is scored, and
any halo statistic that is a brightness excess near a silhouette falls under
blur alone. A binary on/off A/B cannot separate the two. The remedy ORCH
specifies is a dose ladder plus a fine-structure curve at every rung, compared
between ADJACENT RUNGS rather than against off.

I accept the objection. It is correct, it is not speculation, and the same
confound was mispriced tonight in the CA work by a control that under-blurred
by 93% on oriented content. This addendum registers the ladder.


0. PREMISES READ FROM SOURCE AT THE PINNED SHA (discipline 808)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Every statement in this section was read out of
`crates/strehl-cli/src/merge_cmd/halo.rs` at the pinned SHA via `git show`,
NOT from `--help` and NOT from the working tree. Line numbers are that blob's.

  S1. The knob is `STREHL_FIX_HALO_DELTA` (line 71). It exists. It is read by
      `delta_in_use()` at lines 109-114.

  S2. *** THE ZERO RUNG SPECIFIED IN THE LADDER IS UNREACHABLE THROUGH THIS
      KNOB. *** `delta_in_use()` is

          std::env::var(DELTA_ENV).ok()
              .and_then(|v| v.trim().parse::<u16>().ok())
              .filter(|d| *d > 0)
              .unwrap_or(DELTA)

      `.filter(|d| *d > 0)` discards 0, so `STREHL_FIX_HALO_DELTA=0` falls
      through to `unwrap_or(DELTA)` and renders THE SHIPPED DEFAULT OF 40, with
      no warning and no diagnostic. A ladder run as `0/10/20/40/80` would
      therefore have measured 40 twice and reported a flat bottom rung that is
      an artefact of a filter clause. The same silent fallback swallows any
      unparseable value, so a typo in the variable name's VALUE also renders 40.

      This matters beyond bookkeeping. `allows(s,k)` is
      `|k - kf[s]| >= delta` (line 445), so delta = 0 admits every frame and is
      exactly the no-op that builds the region and forbids nothing -- i.e. the
      one configuration that would isolate ORCH's confound perfectly. It exists
      in the arithmetic and is gated off by one `.filter()`. The bottom rung of
      this ladder is therefore delta = 1, which forbids only the focus frame
      itself. It is the minimum reachable dose, not zero dose.

  S3. *** THE TREATED REGION IS INVARIANT ACROSS THE LADDER. *** In
      `Plan::build` (lines 379-437) `treated` is filled by
      `if !mask[i] && d2[i] <= yoke2 && near[i] != u32::MAX`. `delta` appears
      nowhere in that predicate; it is stored on the Plan and consulted only
      later, in `allows()`. `YOKE` is a `pub const` of 40.0 with no environment
      override (line 75). `mask` comes from `foreground_mask(luma, ...)` where
      `luma` is pass one's elected winner luma, and the halo fix is pass two, so
      pass one is identical across rungs.

      Consequence: every rung treats the SAME pixel set and differs ONLY in
      dose. This is the clean contrast ORCH asked for -- constant region,
      varying dose -- and it is a property of the code rather than a hope. It
      is checkable: the tool logs the treated count, so LB6 below tests it.

  S4. `DELTA = 40` (line 86) and the docstring's provenance for 87.9% / 84.6%
      and for the delta-80 softening claim are as ORCH quoted them.


1. ARMS
-------
Two subjects carry the ladder. Both are M87's OWN stacks, deliberately: the
softening claim and the removal claim are both about these, so this is the
strongest available test of ORCH's confound on the evidence that justifies the
instrument.

  bee-3-cpl    -- oriented fine structure: hairs, claws, antennae. This is the
                  stack the "visibly softens hairs and claws" claim is about,
                  and oriented content is where the analogous CA control failed
                  worst. It is the fine-structure subject.
  flower-bud-with-background-fern-cpl
               -- M87's second stack, a genuine occluding edge, and the stack on
                  which addendum 2 found the correction carving mush out of the
                  subject. It is the damage subject.

Rungs, `STREHL_FIX_HALO=1` throughout, `STREHL_FIX_HALO_DELTA` as named:

  L1   delta = 1     minimum reachable dose (see S2)
  L5   delta = 5
  L10  delta = 10
  L20  delta = 20
  H1   delta = 40    ALREADY RENDERED, reused unchanged
  H2   delta = 80    ALREADY RENDERED, reused unchanged
  L160 delta = 160   above the documented softening dose

  H0   FIX_HALO unset -- off. Present as an anchor ONLY. Per ORCH it is NOT the
       blur reference and no bar below is decided against it.
  LZ   STREHL_FIX_HALO_DELTA=0, bee only -- the dead-rung probe for LB1.

All arms: `--limit 100`, `--gate 4`, linear (NO `--srgb`; sRGB is for looking
at, not measuring), `STREHL_WARPMAP` pointing at the frozen per-subject warp
sidecar so registration is bit-identical across rungs by construction and the
warp cache is bypassed entirely.


2. THE TWO CURVES, AT EVERY RUNG
--------------------------------
Reported together at every rung. One without the other is not interpretable.

  HALO(rung)   the halo index of addendum 2 section 3, unchanged, computed over
               the treated set. Reported as dHALO% against H0 for continuity
               with addendum 2, AND as the adjacent-rung difference, which is
               what the bars below are decided on.

  D(rung)      fine-structure retention: log10 of the ratio of high-pass
               amplitude to H0, over the treated pixels. Negative = detail
               lost. Its untreated control is the same statistic over pixels
               eroded by HP_BOX+2 away from any treated pixel; that control read
               exactly +0.00000 on all four subjects in addendum 2 and must
               continue to.

  D_or(rung)   fine-structure retention restricted to ORIENTED content: treated
               pixels whose local structure tensor has coherence
               (l1-l2)/(l1+l2) >= 0.6 at H0. This is the hairs-and-claws
               statistic ORCH asked for, and the case the CA control failed on.
               Selected on H0, which is not a compared arm.

  X(rung)      the exchange rate, dHALO% divided by -D. How much halo the rung
               buys per unit of detail it spends. See LB4.


3. BARS
-------
Each bar states its contract, its direction, and what number decides it.

LB1  DEAD RUNG (measured, not argued).
     `STREHL_FIX_HALO_DELTA=0` renders BYTE-IDENTICAL to delta 40 on bee.
     PASS = SHA-256 identical. This converts the source read S2 into a
     measurement. If it FAILS, S2 is wrong and I retract it.

LB2  BLUR IS MONOTONE IN DOSE.
     D(rung) is non-increasing across L1 -> L5 -> L10 -> L20 -> H1 -> H2 -> L160
     on bee, allowing each step a tolerance of +0.005 dex for noise.
     PASS = at most one step violates it. FAIL = two or more do.
     *** I PREDICT THIS FAILS. *** Addendum 2 measured D at delta 80 as -0.184
     against -0.237 at delta 40 on bee -- LESS detail loss at the LARGER dose.
     Registering it anyway because if it fails, the premise that dose maps to
     blur monotonically is false, and then the ladder cannot separate blur from
     halo removal by monotonicity and ORCH's remedy does not work as specified.
     That is the outcome most worth knowing and I will not soften the bar to
     avoid it.

LB3  HALO CHANGE IS MONOTONE IN DOSE.
     dHALO%(rung) monotone across the same sequence, same one-violation
     allowance, on both subjects independently.
     PASS = at most one violating step per subject.

LB4  THE EXCHANGE RATE IS NOT CONSTANT.
     If a single mechanism -- blur -- drives both curves, then halo change and
     detail loss are two readings of one quantity and X(rung) is flat. If halo
     removal is doing something blur is not, X must vary with dose.
     Decide on the spread of X over the rungs where both curves are outside
     their controls: PASS (two mechanisms) = max(X)/min(X) >= 2.0.
     FAIL (one mechanism, consistent with ORCH's confound) = < 2.0.
     *** I PREDICT THIS IS UNRESOLVED OR FAILS. *** Addendum 2 already found the
     halo index moving the WRONG WAY at every dose on three of four subjects, so
     X's numerator may not have a usable sign. If X cannot be formed, LB4 is
     VOID and reported as void, not as a pass.
     This bar is an ADJACENT-RUNG, WITHIN-MECHANISM test: every rung builds the
     same region by S3, so no rung-to-rung comparison is contaminated by the
     region-construction difference that separates H0 from the rest.

LB5  VACUITY (fact 1548, and it caught a dead probe in addendum 2).
     Each of HALO, D and D_or must, on at least one rung, differ from its own
     untreated control by more than 10x the control's magnitude. A statistic
     that cannot move is VOID, and any bar decided on it is VOID, not PASS.
     No "null" result may be reported for a statistic that fails LB5.

LB6  REGION INVARIANCE (tests premise S3).
     The tool's reported treated-pixel count is identical across ALL rungs on a
     given subject. PASS = identical. If it FAILS, S3 is wrong, rungs differ in
     region as well as dose, and LB2/LB3/LB4 are all confounded and must be
     reported as confounded.

LB7  VISIBLE COST AT THE SHIPPED DOSE.
     Not a numeric bar: a named deliverable. A 1:1 crop on bee at oriented fine
     structure, H0 vs delta 40, captioned with the rung, published whether or
     not it shows a cost. Per ORCH: if delta 40 costs visible detail, show it.


4. PROMOTION RULE
-----------------
Unchanged from prereg-halo.txt section 5: HB2 already failed at R = 3.054 and
that decided KEEP-OFF. This addendum CANNOT overturn that; it can only explain
it. Specifically:
  - LB4 PASS would show halo removal is not merely blur, which would make the
    instrument worth repairing rather than discarding, but would not by itself
    promote anything.
  - LB4 FAIL would show the 87.9% is priced in blur, which strengthens KEEP-OFF.
  - LB2 FAIL would show the dose ladder does not separate the two at all, in
    which case I report that ORCH's remedy is insufficient on this instrument
    and say why, rather than reporting a separation I did not achieve.
