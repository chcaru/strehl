1. THE QUESTION
---------------
ORCH pre-registered a prediction: turning on STREHL_PHOTOMETRIC / --photometric
should move the patchwork invariant from the shipped 1.114x toward 1.0, and
should help most on stacks with the largest inter-frame level drift.

The mechanism ORCH gives: the merge gathers different frames in different
regions; M24 measured 2.88 / 6.84 / 8.93 / 11.70 percent peak-to-peak drift
across four stacks; if neighbouring regions elect frames differing by that much
in level, the region boundaries become visible steps. Photometric normalisation
removes the level difference, so the steps should shrink.

This campaign answers that on the owner's real capture, end to end.


2. PRIOR ART THAT CONTRADICTS THE PREDICTION — DECLARED BEFORE I RUN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
I am registering this in advance so that a HARM result cannot be read as me
having found what I already expected to find, and so that a HELP result cannot
be read as me having quietly dropped inconvenient prior work.

  - M120 section 36.2 measured --photometric on this exact stack (bee-3-cpl)
    and found HARM: ratio 1.0946, i.e. +9.5 percent worse, on 12 rule-chosen
    windows. Not a median dragged by outliers: 12 of 12 windows harmed, range
    1.066 to 1.112.
  - M122 measured B12 = 1.0638 independently, a different statistic with a
    different window rule, same direction.
  - M120 section 36.4 measured bee-3-cpl's own drift at 14.03 percent
    peak-to-peak — larger than any figure in the corpus, larger than all four
    of M24's stacks. So bee-3-cpl is precisely the stack where ORCH's mechanism
    predicts the largest gain, and it is the stack where the shipped
    implementation was measured to lose.
  - M120 section 36.5 states plainly that its comparison is a ratio-of-ratios
    across two builds, that this is its main limitation, and that the clean
    experiment was not run for want of budget.

So my honest prior is HARM, against ORCH. The bars in section 5 are therefore
written symmetrically, and the HELP bar is no harder to clear than the HARM bar.

I also note the one respect in which M120 leaves the door open: section 36.3
item 3 measured --photometric at -0.9 percent (a slight HELP) on a pure
background ROI and +9.5 percent (HARM) on the rule-chosen windows. The sign
flips with ROI choice. ORCH's instrument uses its own window rule, which is
neither of those two, so the outcome here is genuinely not determined by M120.


3. WHY THIS RUN IS THE EXPERIMENT M120 ASKED FOR AND COULD NOT AFFORD
----------------------------------------------------------------------
M120 section 36.5 names the settling experiment: render the stack once with
--index-map, again with --photometric, and score both on identical pixels
within one frozen build. That removes the ratio-of-ratios and the cross-build
step in one move.

That is exactly the shape of this campaign. If the result here disagrees with
M120's, mine is the stronger design and M120's should be down-weighted; if it
agrees, M120's limitation was not load-bearing. Either way I will say which.

5. WHY STREHL_ALIGN=0, AND THE REGISTRATION DEFECT THAT FORCES IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
ORCH's instrument scores a merge against M93's single frames at identical
(x, y). M116's existing arms (A0..A4) carry a frozen warp map and CANNOT be
scored that way. Measured, by integer-only phase correlation:

  - M116's arms sit (287, -154) px from the M93 singles. Both A0 and A1 sit at
    the same offset, confirming my arms share one frozen warp.
  - Scoring M116's A0 — nominally the same shipped configuration ORCH scored at
    1.114x — against the M93 singles returns median 0.090 and HOLDS in all 12
    windows. That 12x gap is a registration artefact, not a treatment effect.
    Reporting it as a win would have been a spectacular false positive.
  - M120 reached the same refusal independently by a different route:
    NCC(A0, M93-gate4) = 0.7136, and it declined to mix M93 pixels with M116
    pixels.

M93's armM4-gate4-binlin and all 23 armF-f*.tif are logged `alignment : OFF`.
So rendering P0/P1/P2 with STREHL_ALIGN=0 puts them on that same unwarped grid,
and ORCH's instrument becomes valid on them UNMODIFIED. That is the entire
reason for this constraint.

Registered in advance: I will verify P0 reproduces M93's armM4 before I quote
any absolute ratio. If it does not, absolute positioning against ORCH's 1.114
baseline is withdrawn and only the P0-vs-P1 contrast is reported.


6. METRIC
---------
ORCH's instrument, unmodified, at experiments/ORCH-drift-check/
patchwork_invariant.py:

    python patchwork_invariant.py <merged.tif> <tag> <window-reference.tif>

Primary readouts, reported for EVERY arm as ORCH asked:
  (a) median ratio of merged fine-scale structure to the fine-scale structure
      of the NOISIEST single input frame over the same pixels;
  (b) the violation count — windows of 12 reading above 1.0.

The window reference is P0-default for every arm without exception. Passing a
different reference per arm makes each arm choose its own most-background
windows, which is not an A/B.

Published baselines this must be positioned against:
  gate 0 (a negative control, merge.rs:3027)  2.134x   12 of 12 violating
  gate 4 (the shipped default, merge.rs:298)  1.114x    7 of 12 violating

Secondary, reported but not bar-bearing: per-window ratios, so that a median
cannot hide a split, and the unanimity check M120 used (how many of 12 move in
the reported direction).


7. BARS — FIXED NOW, NOT NARROWABLE LATER
------------------------------------------
Let d = median_ratio(P1) - median_ratio(P0), and v = violations(P1) -
violations(P0).

  PROMOTE  ("HELP")  requires  d <= -0.02  AND  v <= 0.
  REJECT   ("HARM")  requires  d >= +0.02  OR   v > 0 with d > 0.
  NULL                otherwise — no detectable effect, no promotion.

0.02 is chosen as roughly one fifth of the distance the shipped gate already
travels from the gate-0 negative control (2.134 -> 1.114), i.e. an effect small
enough to be worth acting on but large enough not to be a rounding artefact. It
is fixed here so it cannot be moved after the numbers exist.

Additional registered claim, stated so it can fail: ORCH predicts photometric
helps MOST where drift is largest. bee-3-cpl carries the largest drift in the
corpus (14.03 percent, M120 section 36.4). So if photometric does not help
HERE, the drift-magnitude form of the mechanism is refuted, not merely
unsupported. I will say so if that is what happens.


8. CONTROLS
-----------
NULL / byte-freeze. P2-placebo must be byte-identical to P0-default. If it is
not, the renderer is not deterministic, and I will quantify the run-to-run
spread FIRST and treat any effect smaller than that spread as null, loudly,

TRAP T-A (discipline 831 — the null that matters is a real instance of the
population minus the mechanism under test). A single unmerged input frame in
the same unwarped grid, armF-f173.tif, scored as though it were a merge. A
single frame contains no merge and therefore CANNOT contain the merge defect.
Registered expectation: the ratio must read at or below 1.0. ORCH's earlier
kurtosis detector failed exactly this test — it fired on 3 of 12 windows of a
real unmerged photograph, harder than it fired on the merge. If ORCH's
invariant fails T-A too, the whole campaign's numbers are void and I report
that instead of a promotion recommendation.

TRAP T-B (identity). P0 scored against itself as the window reference must give
exactly the same numbers on a re-run. A metric that moves when nothing moved is
measuring the harness.

COMMISSIONING. ORCH's instrument commissions itself in-run against an iid null,
a ramp null and a planted Voronoi mosaic, and exits 4 if the plant does not
fire. I will not suppress or bypass that, and I will record that it passed on
every arm. A green from an uncommissioned instrument is not a green.

READER. Every TIFF is read with tifffile and asserted uint16 on the line after
loading (fact 1615). PIL.Image.open silently returns uint8 for 16-bit RGB.
ORCH's loader already asserts this; I will not add a second reader.
