4. ARMS
-------

Every arm of a subject uses the SAME frozen warp sidecar, solved once per
subject by `strehl align --limit 100 --out C:\dev\strehl-out\M116\warp\<S>-l100.txt`
and thereafter loaded read-only via STREHL_WARPMAP. Registration is therefore
identical across arms by construction and cannot be a confound.

Common to every arm below: --gate 4 (the shipped default), --limit 100,
STREHL_WARPMAP=<the frozen sidecar>, STREHL_ALIGN unset (alignment ON),
STREHL_FIX_CA unset, STREHL_FIX_HALO unset, STREHL_SMOOTH unset, threads
default. Output is the DEFAULT file (binned, linear, camera-native), because
`strehl stack --help` states in terms that the --srgb file is not to be
measured.

  A0  default            no instrument flags
  A1  +photometric       --photometric
  A2  +softgate-gauss    STREHL_SOFT_GATE=gaussian
  A3  +softgate-exp      STREHL_SOFT_GATE=exponential
  A4  +photo+soft-gauss  --photometric and STREHL_SOFT_GATE=gaussian

  M0  mask arm           --gate 0   (pure selection). NOT compared. Its only job
                         is to supply the flat and detail masks and the two crop
                         windows, so that no mask is derived from an arm under
                         test. This is the circularity M111 charged against its
                         own headline and retracted for.

  P1  placebo            byte-for-byte the A0 configuration, re-run with a
                         different --out path and a different STREHL_CACHE_DIR.
                         With STREHL_WARPMAP set, STREHL_CACHE_DIR cannot reach
                         a pixel. MUST read exactly zero.
  N1  null               the A0 invocation repeated verbatim. MUST be
                         byte-identical to A0.
  T1  trap (diagnostic)  A0 plus STREHL_DUMP_NEFF=<path>, documented byte-inert
                         debug output. MUST read exactly zero.
  T2  trap (instrument)  A0 plus STREHL_SOFT_GATE=0, documented by merge.rs:442
                         as "shipped top-hat". MUST read exactly zero. This is
                         the trap aimed at the soft gate specifically: if the
                         soft-gate arm's improvement also appears when the soft
                         gate is switched off by a value naming the hard gate,
                         the improvement is not the soft gate.

  B0  single-frame       the M85 baseline, see section 6.

The lateral-CA correction is NOT an arm. STREHL_FIX_CA is unset on every run.
It is under active dispute and belongs to M107 and M114.

The outlier / robustness mask is NOT an arm, because it is not reachable: at
this SHA the only reference to strehl_core::outlier outside the module itself

audit, not by a render.


5. METRICS
----------

All computed on the DEFAULT (binned, linear, camera-native) 16-bit RGB output,
green channel, which the merge defines as (G1+G2)/2. f64 throughout.

  hp(L)  = L - box5(L), a 5x5 box high-pass.

  Masks, both taken from M0 and from M0 alone, and therefore identical across
  every compared arm:
    structure(x,y) = local sd over a 33x33 window of M0's green plane after 4x
                     box-downsampling (downsampled first so the ranking is
                     driven by scene structure and not by M0's pixel noise),
                     nearest-upsampled back to the plane grid.
    Mflat = the lowest  20% of structure    (the rule-9 flat control)
    Mdet  = the highest 20% of structure

  N = log10( sd of hp(L) over Mflat )     LOWER is better.
      FOOLED BY: any blur. A merge that simply softens the image lowers N. N is
      therefore inadmissible alone and is only ever read beside D.

  D = log10( sd of hp(L) over Mdet )      HIGHER is better.
      FOOLED BY: ringing, sharpening, and manufactured detail. Rule 18 and
      M113's finding that a high-band statistic rises with ringing both apply.
      D is admissible as a test AGAINST COLLAPSE and is NOT evidence of quality
      on its own.

  Q = D - N   (dex)                       HIGHER is better.
      A log ratio, so dimensionless and geometric: there is no absolute DN
      tolerance anywhere in it (the mechanism behind the waxy-blob artefact,
      fact 1470).
      FOOLED BY: an operator that adds detail-band ringing while smoothing flat
      regions raises Q twice over. Q is therefore reported beside the 1:1 crops
      and never instead of them.

  Reported alongside, descriptively, not as bars: mean n_eff as printed by the
  binary; the fitted photometric gain spread on the arms that fit one; SHA-256
  and byte count of every output file (rule 47).

  Delta convention: DELTA-X(arm) = X(arm) - X(A0), on the same subject, same
  masks, same grid.


6. THE M85 BASELINE — IS THE MERGE SHARPER THAN THE BEST SINGLE FRAME?
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Asked as a separate sub-experiment because an aligned merge and a single frame
do NOT share a pixel grid: fit_to_cover rescales the merged content by a factor
k about the frame centre while leaving the grid dimensions alone, so comparing
across it would require a resampling that adds blur to the quantity being
measured. Resampling is therefore refused.

Instead both sides are rendered with STREHL_ALIGN=0, which removes the warp and
the cover fit entirely and puts the merge and the single frame on one identical,
pixel-exact 3516x2344 grid with no interpolation anywhere.

  B0  the sharpest single source frame, copied out of the read-only library into
      C:\dev\strehl-out\M116\single\<S>\ and rendered alone with STREHL_ALIGN=0.
      "Sharpest" is defined as the frame index that wins the most pixels in A0's
      election map (--index-map, documented as byte-inert). That is a frame
      CHOICE made by the pipeline; it is not a metric and does not enter any
      score.
  B1  the same 100 frames merged at --gate 4 with STREHL_ALIGN=0.

  Masks for this pair come from a third arm on the same unaligned grid: the same
  100 frames at --gate 0, STREHL_ALIGN=0.

  REGISTERED, and this is the one I lead the report with if it fires:
  If D(B1) - D(B0) < -0.010 dex on 2 or more of the 3 subjects, the report leads
  with "the default merge is softer than the best single source frame", above
  every instrument verdict.

  Stated limitation, registered now: this comparison has alignment switched off,
  so it does not describe the shipped default path, which aligns. It bounds the
  merge-versus-single-frame question with zero resampling confound and nothing
  more.


7. BARS
-------

For an instrument to be recommended PROMOTE on a subject, all four must hold.

  B1  NO DETAIL LOSS       DELTA-D >= -0.010 dex
  B2  A REAL GAIN          DELTA-Q >= +0.010 dex
  B3  ABOVE THE FLOOR      |DELTA-Q| >= 3 x max(|DELTA-Q| over P1, T1, T2)
                           and |DELTA-Q| >= 0.010 dex
  B4  NO VISUAL REGRESSION the 1:1 crops show no new artefact

0.010 dex is 2.33% in linear sd. It is set here, before any datum, at the level
below which the project should not be moving a shipped default.

Per-instrument verdict, over the three subjects:
  PROMOTE       passes B1-B4 on >= 2 of 3 subjects AND DELTA-Q > -0.010 on all 3
  KEEP-OFF      fails B2 on >= 2 of 3 subjects, OR fails B1 on any subject
  INCONCLUSIVE  anything else

Milestone-level criteria:
  SUCCESS  every instrument receives a verdict backed by a number, whether or
           not any instrument promotes. "None of these measurably improves the
           render" is a successful outcome and will be reported as the headline
           if that is what the data say.
  FAILURE  the null N1 is not byte-identical to A0 and the run-to-run spread
           cannot be quantified. In that case every difference below is
           confounded, and the report says so first, loudly, and quantifies the
           spread before reporting a single positive result.


8. CONTROLS THAT MUST NOT FIRE
------------------------------

  P1 placebo    DELTA-Q must be exactly 0.000000 and the output SHA-256 must
                equal A0's.
  N1 null       output SHA-256 must equal A0's.
  T1 trap       output SHA-256 must equal A0's. A byte-inert diagnostic that
                moves the image would refute AGENTS.md rule 26 for this path.
  T2 trap       output SHA-256 must equal A0's, AND DELTA-Q exactly 0.000000.

  Additionally, a predictor with a direction: the photometric arm's effect
  should scale with the fitted per-frame gain spread. If a subject's fitted
  gains span ~0 and A1 nevertheless moves Q by more than the B3 floor, the
  photometric reading on that subject is not the photometric correction and is
  reported as unexplained rather than as an improvement.


9. IMAGES — THE DELIVERABLE THE OWNER LOOKS AT FIRST
-----------------------------------------------------

Written to C:\dev\strehl-out\M116\, named <subject>__<arm>__<crop>.png.

  Full renders   one per subject per arm, --srgb at a FIXED exposure per subject.
                 The exposure is solved ONCE, by --auto-exposure on A0, and then
                 passed as a literal --exposure=N to every arm of that subject
                 including B0, so that the arms differ in the merge and not in
                 the gain applied to it. The value is recorded.
  1:1 crops      two per subject per arm, 512x512 on the plane grid (1024x1024
                 on the --srgb --demosaic grid), at IDENTICAL pixel coordinates
                 across all arms of a subject:
                   crop DETAIL — the 512x512 window of highest mean structure
                   crop FLAT   — the 512x512 window of lowest mean structure
                 Both windows are located on M0, the non-compared mask arm, so
                 the choice of what to look at is not made by an arm under test.
  Contact sheet  one per subject, all arms side by side at 1:1 for each crop,
                 labelled with arm, commit SHA and the metric values.
