---------------------------------------------------------

Per discipline 808 the premises below come from the SOURCE at the pinned SHA,

Established facts, quoted from that source:

  (a) The correction is REACHABLE at this SHA. `merge_cmd.rs:2057` reads
      `if halo::enabled() && opts.out.is_some()`. It is a real third pass.
      It is gated by STREHL_FIX_HALO; STREHL_FIX_HALO_DELTA overrides DELTA.
      Defaults: enabled()=false on Err(_); DELTA=40; YOKE=40.0 binned px.

  (b) The module declares itself COSMETIC AND ANTI-FIDELITY, verbatim:
      "**This is a cosmetic filter. It makes the image measurably more wrong.**"
      Against synthetic truth at delta=40 it reports halo metric -79.8% with
      RMSE against truth +254.6%.

  (c) THE CRITICAL PREMISE. The module states it is NOT depth-selective:
      "On a region with **no defect at all** the correction still 'improves'
      the halo metric by `63.9%` while raising the error against truth by
      `558.4%`. It is not depth-selective: it moves `85.4%` of treated pixels
      at a *planted, non-occluding* disc in the bee's own background against
      `82.1%` at the real occluding edge. It fires on silhouettes, not on
      occlusions, and it cannot tell them apart."

  (d) Therefore the number ORCH asked me to reproduce -- the 87.9% halo
      removal on bee-3-cpl -- is a reading of a metric that the SAME MODULE
      documents as reading -63.9% where there is no defect. Reproducing 87.9%
      would therefore NOT be evidence for promotion. A metric that moves that
      far on a no-defect region is not, on its own, a quality metric.
      This drives the bar structure below: REMOVAL IS NOT THE DECIDING BAR,
      SELECTIVITY IS.

  (e) M87's numbers were all taken at gate 0. The module states the noise cost
      inside the yoke against a GATED merge is "an additional, unmeasured
      cost". The product default is gated. So the shipped configuration has
      never been measured, and that gap is what a promotion decision needs.

3. METRICS
----------

All measured on the LINEAR binned output (never --srgb; its own --help says
"For looking at, NOT for measuring"). --srgb is used only for the crops shown
to the owner, and every such image is labelled as such.

  L        per-pixel green-channel luma of the linear render.
  FG       foreground mask: Otsu threshold on L, same two-class rule the
           module uses, then a 3px morphological close/open, components under
           2000 px dropped.
  d(p)     Euclidean distance in binned px from pixel p to the nearest FG px.

  HALO INDEX
      H = mean over background pixels with 2 <= d <= 40 of  ( L(p)/L_far - 1 )
      where L_far = median L over background pixels with 60 <= d <= 100.
      A RATIO, deliberately scale-invariant: an absolute DN tolerance is what
      produced the "waxy blob" (fact 1470), and a ratio also makes the index
      immune to the exposure constant.

  dHALO%  = 100 * (H_on - H_off) / H_off.     Negative = halo removed.

  SELECTIVITY RATIO
      R = dHALO%(flower-bud-alone) / dHALO%(flower-bud-with-background-fern)
      R near 0  => the correction acts only where there is an occlusion.
      R near 1  => it acts on any silhouette and cannot tell them apart.

  Q = D - N   the same band-ratio detail statistic as the main campaign,
      evaluated on a FINE-STRUCTURE window chosen on the H0 render (never on
      an arm under test) at hairs/claws on bee-3-cpl.

  YOKE NOISE  ratio of local std inside the yoke band (2<=d<=40) to local std
      outside it (60<=d<=100), background pixels only, on- vs off-arm.


4. BARS. These are the contract. They are not adjustable after data exists.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

HB1  REPRODUCTION. On bee-3-cpl, dHALO% at delta 40 is <= -50%.
     (M87 claims -87.9% with its own instrument at gate 0; I use a looser bar
     because my index is independently defined and mine is gated. If HB1
     FAILS, M87's headline does not reproduce and that leads the report.)

HB2  SELECTIVITY -- THE DECIDING BAR. R <= 0.50.
     That is: the correction must remove at most half as much "halo" from the
     stack with no occluding background as from the stack with one.
     PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION, from premise (c): the module's own synthetic
     numbers give 63.9/79.8 = 0.80, so I EXPECT HB2 TO FAIL. Registering the
     expected failure in advance is the point: if it fails as predicted, the
     87.9% removal figure cannot support promotion no matter how well HB1
     passes, because the instrument cannot distinguish a defect from an edge.

HB3  FINE STRUCTURE. At delta 40, on the bee fine-structure window,
     dQ >= -0.010 (the same tolerance as main-campaign B1).
     PLANT (fact 1548): delta 80 MUST fail this bar. The module says delta 80
     "visibly softens hairs and claws". If delta 80 does NOT read a loss, the
     Q statistic is a dead probe on this wiring and HB3's pass is vacuous and
     must be reported as vacuous rather than as a pass.

HB4  YOKE NOISE. The yoke/outside noise ratio rises by <= 20% from H0 to H1.
     This is the cost the module flags as unmeasured against a gated merge.

HB5  TRAP. H3 (STREHL_FIX_HALO=0) is byte-identical to H0 on all subjects.

HB6  CONTAINMENT. Every pixel with d > 40 (outside YOKE) is bit-identical
     between H0 and H1. The correction must not reach beyond its stated yoke.
     A failure here means the implementation exceeds its documented reach.

HB7  VISUAL. I inspect the 1:1 crops myself and state, in the report, whether
     the fringe is visibly reduced and whether fine structure is visibly
     softened, separately, per subject.

-----------------

PROMOTE the halo correction to default-ON only if ALL of:

    OFFER-AS-CHOICE is NOT promotion and does not flip a default.

6. WHAT WOULD MAKE ME WRONG
---------------------------

If dHALO% on flower-bud-alone-cpl reads near zero while bee and fern read
strongly negative, then the correction IS occlusion-selective on real data,
the module's synthetic no-defect row does not transfer, HB2 passes, and the
promotion case is live. I would then be obliged to report that M87's own
caution is too harsh, and to say so as plainly as I would report the reverse.
