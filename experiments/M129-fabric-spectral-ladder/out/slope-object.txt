  REGISTERED PREDICTION WAS: the spread will NOT collapse, because the
  observed aperture dependence is smaller than diffraction alone predicts,
  so removing the full MTF steepening overshoots and inverts the order.
  PREDICTION CONFIRMED: the spread did not collapse. Removing the MTF
  overshoots, which means a second aperture-dependent effect is
  present with the OPPOSITE sign to diffraction. T1 is the registered
  candidate: wider apertures have shallower depth of field, so more of
  the ROI is defocused, and defocus steepens the spectrum. That effect
  is largest at f/5.6 and smallest at f/18 -- exactly the sign needed.
