  BUT THE KNEE BAND IS THE WRONG PLACE TO READ THE NUISANCE FROM. Its
  SNR is non-monotone in aperture because it mixes two effects: the
  noise floor, which is a constant-exposure artefact, and the roll-off
  moving into the band, which is the measurement. The 'low' band lies
  below every candidate cutoff and so isolates the artefact.

  READ THIS AS FOLLOWS, AND MIND THE UNITS, WHICH DIFFER BETWEEN THE TWO
  MEASUREMENTS AND ARE EASY TO CONFLATE.
    xspec.py indexed its bias surface by AMPLITUDE snr, sigma_signal over
    sigma_noise, and measured about 11.4 points of bias per e-fold of it.
    Every ratio printed above is a POWER ratio, scene power over floor
    power. Amplitude = sqrt(power), so the same slope is 5.7 points per
    e-fold of power ratio.

    low-band power ratio across the primary arm    1.72x  ->   3.1 points differential
    knee-band power ratio, contaminated, for contrast  5.69x  ->   9.9 points
    The H2-H3 separation is 28.07 points.

  AND THIS IS AN EXTRAPOLATION, NOT A MEASUREMENT. xspec.py's surface
  spans amplitude SNR 1 to 15; the low band here sits at amplitude 31 to
  74, far above it, on a texture whose spectral slope was not the one
  planted. The slope is quoted to size the problem, not to correct it.
  The commissioning battery measures the bias where the ladder actually
  sits, and its numbers supersede these.

AMENDMENT 5 EQUALISATION, computed on the 'low' band

  Adding white noise of variance v raises the floor by v in this
  normalisation and leaves the scene untouched, so the ratio can be
  driven down to any target below the current one, never up.

  THE BAND MATTERS AND THE OBVIOUS CHOICE IS WRONG. Equalising on the
  knee band equalises away the effect under test: f/18's knee-band SNR
  is low partly because its cutoff lies INSIDE that band, which is the
  signal, not a nuisance. Degrading f/11 and f/14 until they match it
  would be matching them to f/18's optics rather than to its noise.
  The 'low' band lies below every candidate cutoff at every aperture, so
  its SNR is texture contrast over noise floor with the roll-off
  excluded by construction -- the part of the SNR that is an artefact of
  constant exposure rather than a consequence of the optics.
