CROSSING BIAS AGAINST TEXTURE SPECTRAL SLOPE

planted f_c 0.1273 sensor cyc/px (24.9 cy/mm), 16 replicates per slope
low-band SNR held at 2000 by adding noise, so the only
variable is the slope. The fabric measures alpha = 3.126.

PREDICTION, MADE BEFORE RUNNING: bias becomes MORE NEGATIVE as alpha
grows, because a steeper spectrum reaches the floor at a lower frequency.

    alpha   low SNR    bias %      sd    n
    2.000      1994     -0.44    1.05   16
    2.200      2010     -1.53    2.01   16
    2.600      2005     -3.97    1.44   16
    3.000      1988     -3.43    1.88   16
    3.126      1993     -4.24    1.05   16
    3.400      2003     -4.24    1.05   16
    3.800      1978     -5.06    1.44   16

  bias moves -2.35 points per unit of alpha
  at alpha 2.2  (what every synthetic test so far planted): -1.53%
  at alpha 3.126 (what the fabric actually is)            : -4.24%
  difference -2.71 points

  PREDICTION CONFIRMED: the bias is more negative on a steeper texture.

  CONSEQUENCE FOR THE LANE. Every bias figure this lane has quoted was
  measured at alpha 2.2 and the subject is alpha 3.126, so all of them
  are wrong by about -2.7 points in the pessimistic direction. The
  commissioning battery must sweep alpha over at least 2.8 to 3.5 -- the
  fabric's measured mean plus and minus one ROI-to-ROI sd -- or its bias
  correction is an extrapolation in the one variable that was never swept.
