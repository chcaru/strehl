  consistency observation, NOT a blind prediction (see the header)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  measured ratio of waves per frame, f/14 : f/18 = 1.048
  a constant physical step would give (N18/N14)^2 = 1.480
     using N_eff = 30.05 at f/14 and 36.56 at f/18
  a step scaled to the diffraction depth of field would give 1.000,
  because that depth is itself proportional to N_eff^2, so the
  wavefront error per step is the same at every aperture
  the measurement is 0.048 from 1.000 and 0.432 from 1.480, so it favours depth-of-field scaled stepping
  which is the standard way to bracket a macro stack, and it means the
  two stacks carry comparable defocus per frame rather than f/14
  carrying half again as much

what is retracted
============================================================================
  defocus_b.py's reading of its own output is withdrawn. It found
  fitted B peaking near best focus at 12 of 12 cells and concluded the
  estimator had failed off-focus, because a diffraction-limited fit
  should not be trusted on a defocused frame. The premise was sound and
  the conclusion was wrong: the peak is the estimator's genuine defocus
  response, measured here on synthetic data where the answer is known.
  Its control C1 still stands as failed and its P8-P10 are still not
  claimed, because P8 asked for a curvature sign that is now known to
  be the wrong way round and cannot be reinterpreted after the fact.

  What replaces them is stronger than what they asked for. P10 wanted
  the gap to close after a defocus correction. V4 shows no defocus
  correction of any size can close it, because defocus moves B the
  wrong way.
