P3 IS FALSIFIED and that stands on the record. I predicted the bias
would inflate B by under 5%, reasoning from Amendment 7a's applicable
bias near 1.8%. It inflates B by up to 13.02%, seven times my estimate.
The reason is visible in the harness: the bias grows with the cutoff,
and Amendment 7a's figure was a single number for a quantity that is
not one number.

THE REGISTERED FALSIFIER IS NOT TRIGGERED. It was P4 failing, or the
B error exceeding 14%. P4 passed and the error reached 13.02%.
The lane continues, but not on the analysis it was going to use:
inverting the estimator and reading B off the answer spends nearly
half the gap on a correction I cannot pin down, and leaves H3 with
2.3 sigma. The repair is to stop inverting. Push each
hypothesis FORWARD through the estimator, bias and all, and compare
predicted cutoffs against measured ones in the space where the
measurement actually lives. A common bias then cancels by
construction instead of being subtracted by hand. That is a change
to the registered analysis and it needs an amendment before use.
