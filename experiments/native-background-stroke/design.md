**Signed contrast is descriptive.** For every original, gauge and green phase, report S=mean(stroke black-subtracted DN), B=mean(clean DN), C=S-B. Negative C means the selected stroke box is darker. Also disclose C/B only for finite B>0; otherwise mark the ratio unavailable. Keep S,B,C beside that ratio and both green phases separate. The ratio is not a fitted exposure correction, calibrated attenuation, sigma or confidence interval. A broad stroke box dilutes a narrow mark, the clean box is 190 native pixels higher, and a real illumination/scene gradient can produce C without a stroke. Scene-gauge sampling also changes source location and smooths noise. These are reasons contrast cannot choose the coordinate hypothesis by itself.

Record exposure time, ISO and aperture only if the existing loader/metadata already supplies them; otherwise record unavailable. Do not infer unchanged exposure from filenames or fit illumination from the selected mark. A changed gain, black level, illumination or focus-dependent brightness can alter absolute contrast. The clean denominator reduces a common multiplicative nuisance but cannot remove spatial gradients or additive offsets. No private noise model, temporal-sigma estimate, polynomial background fit or new EXIF reader is allocated.

**One compact contact sheet.** Five columns in original-index order, with sensor and scene views clearly labelled and both green phases retained. Use the same documented linear display limits across all raw cells; a single pooled display stretch is permissible solely for visibility. Record its endpoints and clipping, and do not fit a separate gain/contrast per frame or gauge. Mark the frozen stroke/clean boxes and retain the 2D context. Bilinear scene views and unchanged sensor samples have different interpolation smoothing; grain sharpness cannot decide persistence. Lightroom context is supplementary, independently labelled and never placed on the RAW lattice or RAW display-DN scale.

Inspect the two endpoints, vertical placement and any asymmetric neighbouring structure through all five views. Stronger persistence of that 2D structure in sensor coordinates supports a sensor-coordinate hypothesis; stronger persistence in scene coordinates supports captured scene structure under the held geometry. Mere row-profile or average-contrast stability does neither: the recorded movement is mostly along the stroke. An effectively infinite horizontal mark, cropped/missing endpoints, uniform patches, changing defocus/illumination, disagreement between phases, mixed persistence or plausible map residuals require ambiguity. Absence in these five inputs cannot establish fusion manufacture; 100 originals remain uninspected.
