M16: harness, gates and plant characterisation

The M16 driver, plus the two gates and the two descriptive scans that had to
run before any arm.

Harness. `experiments/m16-orientation-fpn/` includes M13's `edge`, `measures`,
`planted` and `tile` modules BY PATH, unmodified, so the render, `E_excess`,
the Sobel/Tenengrad operator and the green-luma extraction are M13's exactly.
M16 adds only a sweep driver and five treatment knobs (`--texture`,
`--destripe`, `--fpn-sd/-axis/-stage`, `--transpose`, `--score-region`), all of
which touch ONLY the focus operator's input. `merged` is still taken from the
untreated frames and `ideal`/`oracle`/`single_sigma` are untouched, so an arm
changes which frame is elected and nothing else.

A `--margin` override lets an arm sweep a subset of M15's widths while keeping
M15's tile, and therefore M15's scene: the edge sits at `tile_width / 2`, so
shrinking the margin would move the boundary relative to the texture and
silently make a different experiment.

Gate 1 (`m16_gate1.py`, written independently of `m15_analyse.py`): reproduces
SYNTHESIS fact 83 from M15's committed CSVs. All twelve published numbers
recovered exactly -- horizontal 10/10 narrower median -2.5, vertical 6/10 wider
+1.5, bar 6/10 wider +1.0, ALL median 0.0. Reference statistic A_ref = -3.5
windows.

Gate 2 (in `m16_analyse.py`): arm A0 compared against M15's committed
`edge-*.csv` on all 33 shared columns as strings. 8640 rows, 0 absent, 0
mismatched. A consistency check, not independent evidence (discipline 34/20).

`fpnscan`: row/column marginal structure and gradient anisotropy of the actual
texture tiles and of the synthetic control field.

`darkfpn`: decomposes the sensor's fixed pattern into row, column and residual
parts from D:\macro-dark-frames, using a master bias against a split-half
difference as the null. M4 measured the total FPN sigma and never decomposed
it; M5 section 3.1 asserts a row/column form as an argued root cause. This
measures the split directly, at all 11 ISO groups.

Unit tests: 17 pass, including that a row-constant field has exactly zero
Sobel-x response (H3's mechanism, asserted rather than assumed), that
`synthetic_texture_seeded` is bit-identical to M13's at M13's hard-coded seed,
and that `destripe_mean` preserves the plane mean.
