# Independent plan for one exploratory native chip observation

Root explicitly commissions this separate observation after the generated
screen failed. That failure, its 98% far-component protection, the original
two-band focus-front failure and their saved receipts remain unchanged. This
is an observational exception to the generated preregistration's closure, not
a passed gate, a new threshold or a general reconstruction qualification.
Generated canonical-consumer quality remains untested; the complete actual
comparison is the purpose of this bounded observation. No new fixture family,
source selection, width, degree, condition limit or quality-score sweep follows.

Hold all 299 originals, source order, reference149, delivered 6500x4332 crop
[133,89,3250,2166], map, saved model, fine width and native quad-center rule
exactly as in chip-focus-radiance. Consume the actual current gathered CFA
planes, including current CA and geometry, rather than the developed-RGB proxy.
Use the unchanged quadratic-intercept operator, common signed weights for all
four planes, and original f32 arithmetic. DC changes explicitly. No input-range
clamp, sharpening, signed-weight truncation or Gaussian/baseline fallback may
conceal an invalid candidate.

Before any candidate output, require every actual pixel to pass the original
finite/rank/two-sided/condition checks, plus the prospective geometric noise
limits: sum(h²)<=0.75 and sum(h²)/sum(g²)<=2.0, with finite positive Gaussian
gain. Both Gaussian side fractions must be >=0.01 and the dimensionless
infinity-norm condition <=1e4. A single failing site refuses the entire
candidate; retain its reason and the successful comparator, but no candidate
TIFF or plane cache. Report global extrema and the all-site decision. These
are equal-independent-source geometric noise gains, not a measurement of
camera noise or proof under correlated/heteroscedastic gathered noise.

The complete canonical comparator must match current production RGB16 and ICC.
Capture its actual development settings, verify a pinned baseline rewrite,
then use exactly those settings for the candidate. Shared settings do not imply
the same per-pixel tone/gamut response after radiance changes. The prior complete
Gaussian radiance image may be retained as an explanatory third reference only
with the same input/model/gather and writer identity; it is already a rejected
candidate and cannot substitute for current production in the quality decision.
Retain input/source hashes, current gather evidence, complete plane-cache hashes,
the numerical receipt, full masters and complete image views. No source files
or originals are modified by this observation.

Independent review examines both whole images in common orientation, then all
25 fixed native pairs from chip-stock-selectivity/targets.json: one rail tile
and four tiles in each of upper-colour, central-stipple, lower-array, lower-colour,
surface-scratches and upper-left-contact-grid. No new crop search or recentering.
The retained Gaussian pairs may explain recovery or residual blur, but the
canonical comparison decides whether there is useful overall progress.

The candidate fails photographic acceptance if broad spread improves by
weakening narrow contact subdivisions, dotted/stippled interiors, cyan cell
separators, thin rail crossings or scratch branches. Inspect the upper-left
border control explicitly because the front extrapolates over the entire field.
Check for new bright/dark ringing, reversed edge contrast, coloured fringes and
patch transitions from signed reconstruction. Smoothness, saturation, darker
shadows or higher gradient magnitude cannot establish quality. A sharper-looking
image must still preserve recognizable source-supported structure. No global
photographic noise claim follows from these textured views.

The front predicts tile contrast in toned, CA-off RGB and need not predict
per-pixel zero defocus in the native consumer. Shared Gaussian response width,
local relief, multiple depths in one footprint, focus breathing/placement errors,
sampling and illumination drift remain unresolved. The quadratic model can
reduce temporal averaging bias while amplifying errors in any of these. Its
successful known-surface tests do not establish that averaging bias caused the
previous chip's detail loss. Improvement or failure in this one whole image
must therefore be reported without a causal or cross-corpus claim.

If useful progress is reported to the owner, show the complete stack output,
not just a favorable section. A failed candidate remains a retained comparison,
with no production/default promotion or rescue tuning. Root owns execution and
the subsequent program decision; this reviewer owns the independent image read.
