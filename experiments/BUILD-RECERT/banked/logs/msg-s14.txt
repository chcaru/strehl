The fourth link in ORCH's chain -- "averages more, therefore softens" -- is
FALSE, and it was the only one nobody had measured. STREHL_GATE_FLOOR=white
restores the old behaviour on one binary. Potency first: 91.87% of 98,898,048
samples differ. Then the print, on ABSOLUTE quantities (structure is p99.5 with
the band's own noise removed, so it cannot rise merely because an image got
quieter): turning the fix OFF costs 24.4% of structure in the 5-13 px band AND
raises the noise floor 36%. Worse on both axes, in every band. Averaging more
does not soften because averaging destroys signal only where there IS signal --
a too-low floor lets noise-dominated coefficients into a selection with
exponent 8, where the loudest frame is the noisiest one.
