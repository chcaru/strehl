BUILD-OUTPUT: the registration guard was refusing on noise, and hiding the worst evidence

flatexp.py's registration guard refused three of the first sixteen subjects --
dry-erase-marker-head-cpl, flower-bud-alone-cpl, flowers-cpl -- on an SSD argmin
taken at ONE site with a +/-4 px search. None of them was shifted. Each arm's
own log reports the same sidecar, the same reference frame and the same N placed
/ 0 refused; the outputs have identical shape; and a selection exponent
reweights pyramid coefficients on a fixed grid, so there is no mechanism by
which it could translate an image. What the argmin was reading was the CONTENT
DIFFERENCE between the arms, which is the quantity the experiment exists to
measure.

The cost of that defect was not neutral. With the guard fixed, the two subjects
it had been discarding turn out to carry the LARGEST sharpness losses in the
corpus -- dry-erase-marker-head-cpl at 27.5% softer and flower-bud-alone-cpl at
35.4% softer, against a corpus median of 19.2%. The broken guard was suppressing
the strongest evidence against the arm under test.

THE THRESHOLD IS MEASURED, NOT CHOSEN (regsens2.py). Three real masters were
self-shifted, so the shift is the only difference between the two sides, and the
published ratios were read off at each planted offset:

    shift   max|blotch-1|   max|sharp-1|   max|grain-1|
        1        0.424%         0.106%         0.088%
        8        0.839%         0.472%         0.346%
       16        2.639%         1.333%         1.174%
       32        5.620%         3.509%         2.530%

The effect under test moves `sharp` by 15-22%. A 1 px argmin -- all the evidence
there ever was -- perturbs the answer by 0.106%, a four-hundredth of the effect.
8 px is the last row that is negligible against it, so TOLERATED_SHIFT = 8.

TWO CANDIDATE REPLACEMENTS WERE BUILT AND BOTH WERE KILLED BY THEIR OWN CONTROLS.
Neither ships, and both are recorded because a rejected instrument is a result:

  * Normalised cross-correlation fails AGENTS.md rule 5 -- not subject-invariant,
    so it cannot carry an absolute threshold. NCC(0) = 0.889 is a 4 px shift on
    coffee-bubbles and a 64 px shift on dry-erase-marker-head-cpl. It reports how
    much fine content a subject has, not how far it moved.
  * Low-passing into the base band, on the banner's claim that the base is a
    plain mean across frames in both arms, does not respond to a planted shift at
    all (baseband.py): on flower-bud-alone-cpl the low-passed excess reads 41.2%
    unshifted, 43.3% at +1 px, 41.5% at +4 px and 33.0% at +32 px. A hard
    low-pass removes the structure that localises SSD.

THREE DEFECTS IN THE REPLACEMENT, EACH CAUGHT BY COMMISSIONING AND NOT BY
READING:

  1. The rewrite searched +/-4 px against a tolerance of 8 and passed a PLANTED
     32 px SHIFT as NEUTRAL -- the same saturation defect one layer down, because
     an argmin pinned at the boundary of its own search reports 4, and 4 <= 8.
     SEARCH_RADIUS must exceed TOLERATED_SHIFT.
  2. Well depth was measured at the ORIGIN rather than at the ARGMIN. On a pair
     that really is displaced the origin is nowhere near the minimum, the surface
     there is flat, and the site was declared UNINFORMATIVE -- so both planted
     shifts passed. Whether a surface has a well AT THE ORIGIN is a different
     question from whether it has one AT ALL.
  3. An exact match at the argmin (SSD == 0) was mapped to float("inf") and then
     gated on np.isfinite, which classified the DEEPEST POSSIBLE WELL as
     uninformative. Every planted shift is a np.roll of an exact copy, so every
     one of them went through that door. Real masters never produce a zero SSD,
     so this was invisible on real data and only a synthetic could expose it.

WHAT SHIPS: every site is probed, not one. A global displacement is the same at
every site and noise is not, so the per-axis MEDIAN argmin across sites is the
primary clause; a site whose surface holds a genuine well may also refuse on its
own. Both clauses are conditioned on, and the registration BASIS is partitioned
in the output and asserted to sum, so a subject admitted because no site could
speak never prints as the same glyph as one admitted because a site spoke and
agreed.

Commissioning is 8 arms, 8 PASS / 0 FAIL, and now brackets the threshold from
both sides: 2 px must be TOLERATED (the false-refusal control, without which a
guard that refuses everything is indistinguishable from a correct one), 12 px
must refuse via the interior path, 32 px must refuse via the saturated path.
