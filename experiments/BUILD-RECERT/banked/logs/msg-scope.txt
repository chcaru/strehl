verifier scope: a section slice that runs to EOF decays as the document grows

verify_s13.py sliced from its own heading to end-of-file, which was correct only
while section 13 was last. Committing section 14 put six more
`| `leaf-2-cpl` |` rows inside that slice and three anchors that had been unique
became ambiguous: 50/53 RED, with ANCHOR failures rather than value failures.

Nothing about section 13 changed. The verifier's scope did.

Both verifiers now bound the section ABOVE as well as below, at the next
top-level heading, so section 15 cannot do the same thing to section 14. The
ANCHOR guard is what surfaced this -- a checker that had merely searched for a
value would have gone on reading GREEN against whichever row it found first.

Re-run after the fix: s13 53/53 GREEN 11/11 caught, s14 67/67 GREEN 11/11
caught.
