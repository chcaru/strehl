## Model-input convention

- Model keypoints are the existing coarse Q8 coordinates divided by 256, float32.
- Scale is size_q8 / 256, in coarse detector pixels, retaining OpenCV's diameter convention.
- Orientation is (angle_degrees_q8 / 256) * pi / 180, in radians.
- Scale/orientation arithmetic is evaluated in binary64 in this stated order and rounded once to float32.
