and passed through the same AREA8 path. Both representation receipts must equal
the cached receipts. Expanded RGBs are deleted before extraction. The two coarse
RGBs are shared unchanged across the three arms. The source contains no real image
population, real pair loop, TIFF writer, RAW decoder, graph solve or product call.
The authority chain reads old metadata; it does not execute old image stages.

The explicit-false arm must reproduce the complete cached feature record: coarse
and lifted coordinates, counts, descriptor/key hashes, fixture and representation.
