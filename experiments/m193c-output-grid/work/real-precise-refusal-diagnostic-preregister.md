For the selected seed, every actually visited returned refinement, and the map actually returned by choose, retain all saved rows in original order as compact arrays: ordinal, signed predicted-minus-reference x numerator, signed y numerator, squared residual numerator, and membership within the unchanged two-native radius. The exact coordinate denominator is `256 * 2^32`; the squared denominator is its square. The misleading historical helper name residual_squared_q40 does not redefine these units. Failed fits have no fabricated candidate. No residual volume is retained for the other exhaustive hypotheses.

For all rows, calibration rows and held-out rows separately, report the fixed CDF at native radii 1/4,1/2,1,2,4,8,16,32; nearest-rank min, median, 90th, 95th and maximum quantiles; two-native membership; endpoint bounding boxes/unique-site counts; exact joint-site counts and multiplicity histogram, both before and after the two-native cut. All held-out rows are included, especially those rejected by the cut. Signed vectors can expose coherent direction and gross disagreement descriptively. Quantile square-root display is floating point; the exact integer numerators remain authoritative.

Failure directions: fitted calibration residuals are selected optimistically. Inlier-only RMS can shrink when most correspondences are discarded, and a coherent physically wrong map can fit almost perfectly. All-row residuals can increase through wrong matches, localization error or model error; they cannot identify which. Consensus fractions can be inflated by repeated descriptor rows at the same location. Extents can appear broad through a few outliers, and unique joint sites do not establish statistical independence. The trace is not a standalone quality metric or an acceptance replacement. Holdout values never select another seed or map.
