Why we made Affinity free, and how we’ll keep it that way
We’re sharing why we made Affinity completely free, how our sustainable model makes professional design tools accessible to everyone, and why creative freedom is at the heart of it all.
Press assets for Affinity
When we
announced
⁠
(opens in a new tab or window)
that
Affinity
⁠
(opens in a new tab or window)
– our professional-grade suite for designers, illustrators, and photo editors – was now completely free, the creative world lit up. In just four days,
more than one million people
have signed up to Affinity – the equivalent of filling Wembley Stadium 11 times over. It’s a launch rate faster than most new creative apps on the App Store, and a remarkable signal of how ready the professional design community was for change.
Among the excitement and first impressions, we also saw fair questions:
How is this possible? What’s the catch? Is my data safe?
We understand the curiosity, so let’s take a closer look at why Affinity is free, how it works, and what it really means for the future of creative tools.
Free, by design
Making Affinity free is a continuation of something that’s been at the heart of Canva since day one: the belief that everyone should have access to great design tools, no matter where they live or how much they can afford.
Canva has been built on a simple idea: make powerful creative tools available for free, and charge only for more advanced features like premium content, collaboration, or AI-powered tools if they are needed. It’s a model that’s powered Canva’s growth for more than a decade, allowing us to stay profitable while keeping design accessible to everyone.
Today, Canva is a rapidly growing company with over 28 million paying customers and $3.5 billion in annualized revenue. That sustainable foundation enables us to keep investing in both our free Canva experience and the next generation of creative tools, including offering Affinity completely free.
The expert team that built Affinity prior to Canva’s acquisition continues to build Affinity today, now supported by the investment and stability to grow even further. The product stays true to its heritage while continuing to celebrate the craft, precision, and power that professional creatives love.
A new era for creative professionals
Affinity has long been the choice of designers who care deeply about craft – the ones who notice the smallest details, stay up late perfecting the final pixel, and expect their tools to keep up with their creativity.
By making Affinity free, we’re opening professional-grade design software to everyone. There are no hidden conditions, just an upgraded Affinity experience bringing together vector, photo and layout tools in one place for the first time ever, available to all.
This shift removes one of the biggest barriers in the creative industry. For too long, professional tools have been locked behind expensive paywalls, putting them out of reach for students, emerging creatives, and talented people around the world. By making Affinity free, we’re helping more people design, explore, and build their careers with world-class tools.
When professional designers choose Affinity, they set the tone for how creativity flows through their teams, clients, and organisations. Their craft shapes design systems, influences workflows, and drives how creativity scales. As we continue to build more seamless integrations between what professionals create in Affinity and how those creations can be scaled and deployed in Canva, the entire creative ecosystem benefits, from individual designers to the world’s largest brands.
Your creativity stays yours
We also know that when something is free, people naturally wonder about their data. So let’s be clear: your creativity is, and always will be, yours.
Affinity stores your work locally on your device. Canva does
not
use any of your Affinity content to train or develop AI features, and that includes anything you create using Canva AI tools within Affinity.
If you choose to export or upload your work to Canva, you stay in control. You can review or change your data preferences at any time in your Canva settings.
What comes next
Making Affinity free is about more than software. It’s part of our bigger mission: to build one of the world’s most valuable companies, and use it to do the most good we can.
That mission guides everything we do. It’s why we’ve given more than
$1 billion worth of premium Canva products
⁠
(opens in a new tab or window)
to schools and nonprofits, and why we’ve committed
another $100 million
⁠
(opens in a new tab or window)
to help end extreme poverty in partnership with GiveDirectly.
Now, we’re bringing that same spirit of accessibility to the professional design world, empowering more people to create, learn, and grow with tools that were once out of reach.
This is just the beginning. We’re continuing to invest deeply in both Canva and Affinity, expanding features, strengthening integrations, and supporting the global creative community that’s at the heart of it all.
We believe creative freedom shouldn’t come with a cost. If you’re curious or even skeptical, that’s okay. Stick around. We think you’ll like where this is headed 💜
