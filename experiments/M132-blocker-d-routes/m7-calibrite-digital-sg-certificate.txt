Certificate of Analysis
CCDSG ColorChecker Digital SG

This certificate is provided based on data from Munsell Labs at X-Rite. The color sheets
have been manufactured in accordance to the specifications for Munsell Color services.
The instrument used to measure the color sheets mentioned above has been calibrated
against NIST traceable standards.

The shelf life of the color sheets is a minimum of 2 – 4 years if stored under proper
conditions. To maintain the colors, the color standards should be protected from
fingerprinting, soiling, abrasion and exposure to excessive light or temperature changes.
When not used, the color standards should be stored in a dark cool environment.

Analysis Parameters

The following instrumental parameters were used to generate the data provided in this
certificate:

   •   Illuminant: D65
   •   Observer: 2° standard observer
   •   Wavelength Range: 400 nm to 700 nm
   •   Bandpass: 10 nm data
   •   Sampling Interval: 10 nm data
   •   Instrument Geometry: d/8
   •   Specular Component: Data is provided for SCE (non-specular) measurements
   •   Color Space: CIE L*a*b*
