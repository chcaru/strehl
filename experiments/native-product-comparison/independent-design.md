**Fixed practical procedure.** First view both complete delivered photographs using the same sRGB-aware viewing path, at a modest common display size. This overview is for composition, crop, gross discontinuities and region selection; downsampling cannot establish fine-detail quality. Then choose up to four pairs of common physical subject regions, using visible landmarks independently in each image, and record each output's integer crop rectangle. Coordinates are not interchangeable because the upstream lens correction and crop differ. Show crops at 1:1 pixel sampling, with equal display magnification and labels. Do not resample one output to register it, equalise histograms, change exposure, sharpen or denoise. Optional enlarged inspection uses the same declared nearest-neighbour factor on both.

Region selection is explicitly exploratory after the full-image view. Cover a low-detail region for grain, a fine-texture region for continuity, an occluding boundary for double/missing structure or occlusion halos, and a high-contrast edge for chromatic rims; one region may serve two purposes. If a category is absent or correspondence is unclear, say so instead of inventing a surrogate. Include the visibly worst native defect if present and label it an adverse diagnostic, not a representative sample. Preserve the region list once chosen; any further region is labelled follow-up exploration.

For each pair describe visible structure, not just contrast: grain/clumping alongside neighbouring texture retention; broken or doubled ridges and displaced detail; edge width and bright/dark occlusion bands; coloured-rim direction and spatial pattern. Lower visible grain can result from softer detail, lower tone contrast or Lightroom chroma reduction. Stronger edges can be sharpening, ringing or noise. A chromatic rim can be development/CA as well as fusion. An isolated visual symptom therefore identifies a candidate production defect, not its proven cause. Do not calculate PSNR, a sharpness/noise winner, a causal alignment error or a quality score without independent truth.
