# A bounded spatial focus-front test on the chip

2026-09-25. Root commissions a distinct geometric focus-prior hypothesis after
the direct learned selector's visible seams. Read the prior-art search and
addendum in `quality-september25/chip-planarity-triage.md`. M67/M73 establish
structural invariance checks, not correct absolute focus. M70 preserves useful
off-peak information. M145's equally spaced rail approximation does not make
in-camera frame indices a physical depth scale. V-DILUTE and prior chip
source-field/weighting failures remain closed.

Question: do complete through-focus contrast curves across this nominally flat
object support a shared planar spatial coordinate and a monotone, nonuniform
frame-focus schedule? Fit whole curves, not noisy per-location maxima. A fixed
Gaussian response approximation is rejectable; it is not a measured optical PSF.
Fix the arbitrary coordinate gauge and finite solver budget before execution.
Per-region amplitude/offset nuisances must not secretly fit held-out focus
position, width or schedule. A monotone remapping of one spatial projection is
indistinguishable from the free schedule; it cannot falsify planarity here.

Use only the existing299 same-developer chip TIFFs and frozen source transforms.
Extract a fixed sparse set of disjoint native patches, one frame at a time.
No new original-RAW decoding, complete aligned-frame volume or decoder is needed.
Apply spatial filters to individual2D fields. These toned-image contrast curves
are proxies; the RAW noise model cannot be subtracted in these units. Contrast
can rise with noise or artifacts, and a low fit residual can reward smooth wrong
predictions. Neither is a quality metric.

Independent generated controls precede real-data interpretation: a nonuniform
planar positive, flat null, genuinely two-dimensional curved surface, occluding
two-depth mixture and a shifted-front destroyer. The model must reject wrong
geometry while still fitting a real positive. Freeze disjoint spatial holdouts
and a second frequency-band prediction, with independent nuisance/width rules.
Prediction is scored against measured held-out curves, not the fitted plane
re-expressed as its own target. Keep all failed cases and raw prediction errors.

Author owns model.py/design.md; separate author owns stage.py/stage-plan.md;
independent reviewer owns controls and admission rules. Root runs the bounded
jobs. No parameter rescue, favorable subset or changed grid after real results.
An inadequate model closes this fixed approximation, not all physical models.
A passing proxy could justify designing one native consumer test; it cannot
establish RAW fidelity, ghost removal or a full-image gain. Any later improvement
shown to the owner must include the complete stack output and master.
