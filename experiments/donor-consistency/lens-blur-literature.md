# Calibration is not blind ownership estimation

VERIFIED primary-source check, September19: [Learning Lens Blur Fields,
Lin et al., 2025 revision](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.11535v2) models optical
blur across focus and image position, including sensor effects. Its acquisition
procedure uses known displayed patterns and black/white captures, registering
sharp ground truth into each RAW capture's coordinates (supplement S-I-B/C).
It does not establish blind kernel recovery from these arbitrary crystal stacks.

ARGUED implication for this program: do not treat that paper as permission to
fit unrestricted kernels to the target streak and call the fit proof of common
surface ownership. A compact source can absorb unrelated positive structure
into its fitted kernel. An independently constrained calibration or a different
ownership witness would be needed. No new calibration capture is requested,
no repository implementation was read, and no kernel fitting was performed.
