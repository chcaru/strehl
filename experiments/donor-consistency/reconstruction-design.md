# One reconstruction-order counterfactual

**GO for one bounded photographic test; no promotion or automatic labels.**
Keep the original three masks, donors28/33/25, late69 control, seven bands,
shared terminal mean, complete input domain and held development. The previous
Gaussian coefficient-mask recipe remains closed. Change only the order of
spatial masking and AC reconstruction.

The measured signed accounting licenses this isolation. At the dark fan,
retained baseline G1 contributes +2013DN and the prescribed donor contributes
-327DN, with shared base +69DN and hull correction about -41DN. These are
3x3-quad point-minus-reference contrasts, not light energy or ownership shares.
They show a live retained baseline term despite full finest-mask membership;
they do not prove that deleting it alone fixes the photograph. The existing
masked donor fields cannot predict the proposed unmasked donor AC output.
See `attribution-analysis-1/accounting.md` and the prior joint photo failure in
`results-review.md`.

For each CFA plane, collapse actual resolved baseline coefficients once to
`baseline_pre`; separately collapse their AC levels with terminal zero to
`baseline_ac`. For a prescribed source, build its full original-domain pyramid,
zero only its terminal level, and collapse to `donor_ac`. Apply the unchanged
finest masks at their original output-quad coordinates after these collapses.
Freeze the root-selected f32 evaluation order: accumulate source-ordered
`A = sum(m0 * donor_ac)`, then `baseline_pre + (A - coverage0 * baseline_ac)`,
and apply the original baseline InputRange hull. This keeps only a fixed
accumulator while sources stream through. Normalize any finest-mask overlap with one
shared divisor as before; no reduced mask enters this operator. An exactly
zero total mask copies baseline final bits, with no subtract/add round trip.

In real arithmetic this is the shared mean base plus a convex mixture of
baseline and donor AC. At mask1 it removes all baseline AC, including coarse
AC. That is the specific property the old reconstruction lacked. Independently
check the equivalent mean-base expression within the existing f32 tolerance;
do not require differently ordered collapse/additions to be bit-identical.
Use the same mask and source for all four CFA planes at the existing quad
centre convention. No per-channel masks, crop re-decomposition, CPU regather,
noise-model change or second stored source collection is needed.

The strongest failure is a brightness/colour or texture seam through the
radius16..24 transition. A donor's AC plus another mean base is not that source
photograph: different low-frequency scene content can leave a broad offset,
and multiplying reconstructed AC creates transition gradients. A real thin
feature at a competing depth may be cut or softened there. InputRange bounds
amplitude but does not establish correct detail; a nonzero lower hull can also
restore residual brightness. A chosen source replaces multi-frame averaging,
so grain can rise even with perfect mechanics. Retained single-source noise
must be reported, not recast as detail. Native CFA changes stop at the masks;
final MHC can spread their RGB influence by its existing finite stencil.

Before one complete baseline/candidate/late triplet, reuse the small odd-domain
control with live varying masks and independent full-context AC reconstruction.
Require exact zero/outside CFA bits, shared-plane geometry, terminal-base
equivalence, correct overlap, original-hull bounds with a live clamp, and input
immutability. Require a live difference from the old coefficient-mask operator
and a competing sharp feature crossing a taper; a constant fixture cannot test
the order change. The mean-base offset and cancellation case must be nonzero.
These controls admit the experiment, not quality.

The full triplet must reproduce baseline RGB16 exactly and retain complete
photographs plus the existing native windows and visible mask boundaries.
Joint photo success requires contracted target fans with compact cores and
real facets retained, preserved donor25 structure, and no damaging transition
or conspicuous grain cost. Better than late69 alone is insufficient. Remote
controls must remain exact outside the mask plus actual MHC support. A failure
closes this reconstruction order with the frozen supports; no radius, donor,
feather or band sweep follows. An automatic source-assignment program remains
conditional on an actual photographic improvement.
