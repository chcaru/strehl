  REGISTERED PRECISION REQUIREMENT (secondary reduction): the
  per-aperture 10-90% rise must be measured to 1 sigma <= 0.35 um,
  which is about 1.4% of the rise, for a 4-sigma separation between
  adjacent hypotheses. Above 0.5 um the ladder degrades quickly
  because it is a difference of squares of three similar numbers.

2.8  THE PRIMARY REDUCTION IS BETTER CONDITIONED, AND HERE IS WHY
  m88lib.fit_fc fits A * diffraction(f_c) * pixel * gauss(sigma) to
  the measured SFR. It separates the cutoff from the subject term by
  the SHAPE of the MTF rolloff, not by its width alone, so sigma
  absorbs bevel softness and residual defocus while f_c stays free.
  That gives one f_c per aperture directly, and the test becomes
  f_c(N) * N = const, which is a mean of three ratios rather than a
  difference of squares.

  REGISTERED PRECISION REQUIREMENT (primary reduction): f_c must be
  measured to 5% relative per aperture for a 4-sigma separation. That
  is a far weaker demand than the rise ladder's 1.4%, which is why
  fit_fc is registered as primary and the rise ladder as the
  independent cross-check on it, not the other way round.
