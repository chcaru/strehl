S0  INTERNAL CONSISTENCY OF THE CAPTURE (does the photometry make sense?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    If these are the same feature at three apertures, contrast should fall
    roughly as the aperture AREA, i.e. as N^-2, absent exposure compensation.
      f/5.6 -> f/8   contrast ratio 1.79   N^2 ratio 2.04   (87% of prediction)
      f/5.6 -> f/11   contrast ratio 3.78   N^2 ratio 3.86   (98% of prediction)
    Consistent with a fixed exposure and the same physical feature.
    This does not prove the identification; the verifier's template
    correlation (0.811 at f/8, 0.728 at f/11) is what does that.

T1  THE ENDPOINT RATIO  (assumption-light: needs no fit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    A sharp edge REQUIRES rise(f/11)/rise(f/5.6) = 11.0/5.6 = 1.964

    isotonic  measured ratio 0.791 +/- 0.335      sharp model is 3.50 sigma away
    erf       measured ratio 0.902 +/- 0.269      sharp model is 3.94 sigma away

    Both estimators fall BELOW 1.0 where the sharp model demands 1.96:

S1  THE INSTRUMENTAL FLOOR  (the one confound that mimics intrinsic width)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    A fixed instrument-side blur enters the fit as a constant term in N,
    which is EXACTLY the signature of intrinsic width. So a floor large
    enough to account for w would make this whole verdict an artefact, and
    it has to be budgeted rather than waved away.

      sensor pitch                  5.12 um  -> pixel-aperture rise 4.10 um
      G1 sublattice pitch           10.24 um  -> worst-case sampling  8.19 um
      combined floor (quadrature)               9.16 um

    The sampling term is a WORST CASE and is deliberately unfair to my own
    conclusion: a slanted edge is sampled sub-pixel by construction, so a
    competent reconstruction beats the raw sublattice pitch. Charging the
    full pitch is the most hostile assumption available.

      isotonic  w_measured 19.05 um  -  floor 9.16 um  =>  w_physical 16.70 um
      erf       w_measured 17.35 um  -  floor 9.16 um  =>  w_physical 14.74 um

    A physical width of 15-17 um SURVIVES the worst-case floor: YES
    The floor cannot manufacture this result. That is the point of S1.

VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Independent refit reproduces the verifier's w to within 3 um on both
  estimators: YES
  k lands at or near zero on both -- no optical growth is required by the data.

  T1 (ratio, independent-sigma) says 3.5-3.9 sigma against the sharp model.
  T2 (constrained fit) says 2.0-2.5 sigma.
  The verifier quoted 2-2.5 sigma after conservative allowances. My T2
  agrees with that order; T1 looks stronger for the reason stated in the
  docstring and is NOT the number to quote.

  CONFIRMED: WIDTH-DOMINATED. The feature carries an intrinsic width of
  roughly 17-19 um and cannot serve as an absolute optical reference.
  It remains usable for exercising SFR machinery.

  HONEST LIMIT: three apertures spanning a factor of 2 in N is weak
  leverage. This excludes the sharp-edge model at ~2-2.5 sigma; it does
  not measure w precisely, and the 95% intervals are wide (3.7-25.2 um).
