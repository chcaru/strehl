**ARGUED leverage:** writing `K=sqrt(a*2000+c)`, the proposed transform has
`H'(s)=K/sqrt(a*s+c)`. For small contrast about one pedestal, the same local
derivative approximately multiplies signal energy and paired noise energy;
their ratio can therefore remain unchanged. A weak grating alone is not a
positive test of this mechanism. The fixed Veil and BrightRidge contain large
finite brightness changes within filter support and can express a difference.
Their existence does not guarantee a benefit. Any positive must beat both
canonical and matched-MC-linear, so a floor-estimator change alone is not
credited to the nonlinear guide. Reconstruction remains in linear CFA units.

the known focused far-texture component, and the known focused neutral-glint

Report numerical rank/conditioning; an invalid fit is unqualified, not rescued.

Keep signed component coefficients and all three-arm values. For nomination,

the prospectively selected nomination bars, not a photographic quality claim.

A single full-truth correlation can conceal erased texture beneath a preserved
glint, or a dimmed glint beneath preserved texture; the joint fit separates those
directions. Coefficient recovery can itself reward an overshooting copy of the
template, and the intercept can absorb a brightness error. Therefore retain the
fixed full-truth RGB/green/chroma error bars (1.10 versus both references), the
separate BrightRidge and WeakColour protections, and the 5% positive benefit
requirement. Coefficients above one are not evidence of extra detail.
