## Decision, including how it fails

Useful local headroom requires at least one predeclared focused donor to make the objectionable broad/duplicated boundary visibly cleaner **and** preserve both source-supported hair groups, colour and nearby petal texture, without a seam or conspicuous single-frame grain. Mere dimming, deleting faint genuine hairs, or beating the deliberately blurred donor is failure. No sharpness score or clipped-pixel count decides this.

If complete source purity cleans the boundary but loses one real hair group, record a source-ownership conflict, not a successful correction; it would not license wholesale donor selection. If haze remains in the actual pure donors, stop trying to fix that target through coefficient coherence alone. If one arm does pass, it licenses a separate automatic-ownership question, not default promotion, noise neutrality or SOTA. The likely single-frame noise cost must be shown honestly; these natural textured sites are not calibrated flat controls.
