**DERIVED estimator and unavoidable cost.** Hold the saved `z,t,sigma` and `q=exp(-delta²/2)` fixed, with `delta=(t-z)/sigma`. Full-rank arithmetic reproduces constants, linear and quadratic curves: `sum h=1`, `sum h delta=0`, `sum h delta²=0`. These are commissioning identities, not quality gates. Unless all retained mass is exactly at delta=0, the last identity requires negative weights. Thus convex-range protection and exact quadratic cancellation cannot both hold generally.

Finite/nonuniform sampling needs its actual `sum h² variance_k`; near-singularity, focus error, clipping and visibility changes can make it much worse.
