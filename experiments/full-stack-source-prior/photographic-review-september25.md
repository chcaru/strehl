# Complete marigold and ore: no promotion

Root independently inspected the source-prior author's completed comparison on
2026-09-25. This is a photographic decision, not a sharpness-score claim.

Both original baselines reproduce the delivered RGB16 pixels exactly. The
complete 488-source marigold pair is retained in `marigold4-full-1`; the
207-source ore pair is in `ore100-full-1`. The recorded commands, environment,
input hashes, source revision bindings, executable hashes, timing and resource
limits are in each run's bindings, config, process and output receipts.

VERIFIED inspection used both complete views for orientation, plus the frozen
384-pixel native target pairs generated by
`experiments/quality-september25/compare-tiles.py`. Display is identically
rounded RGB16/257 with the master ICC; native tiles are unresampled. The view
receipts bind their masters and target coordinates.

- Marigold: the fan of real hairs at [3230,3000,384,384] and bright-rim hairs
  at [1436,1596,384,384] show no convincing repair of the haze/contact problem.
  Full views show no convincing broader gain. Genuine faint hairs must not be
  counted as ghosting simply because a candidate removes them.
- Ore: the bright grain target [1208,748,384,384] has no convincing recovered
  facet detail or glare repair. The blue control [2908,2768,384,384] remains
  visually similar. A reduction in white-pixel count would not by itself count
  as recovery of detail.

The two images resolve their automatic tone separately under the same writer
policy. This is a limitation of this prior comparison; the separate highlight
factorial locks actual baseline tone bits. Neither comparison supports a
production change. The generated far-component failure remains binding, and
there is no parameter sweep to rescue this prior.

MEASURED operational times were 651.921 s / 5,261,152,256 bytes peak job
commitment for marigold and 317.125 s / 5,822,685,184 bytes for ore. These jobs
partly overlapped other work; these are resource records, not relative speed
benchmarks. No SOTA claim follows.

The independent renderer reviews in `experiments/highlight-rendering/` also
reject their fixed alternatives. Work moves to the confirmed upstream numeric
range defect on garnet and a separate, bounded source-pure marigold diagnostic.
