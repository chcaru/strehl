# Independent boundary-radiance decision protocol

PREREGISTERED 2026-09-09 before any new scored render. This reviewer authors mathematical controls and fixes the decision choices; root owns builds and numerical execution. No result or deployable quality benefit is claimed here. The charter and native-green-probe/new-quality-directions.md bind the prior failures; M87's oracle-alpha success does not establish native visibility, and its thin-structure failure remains relevant.

## Frozen operator domain and refusals

Use exactly the original same-spectral-colour sensels in a native radius-2 square (5x5 sensels), all five existing frames, with both green phases sharing a spectral field at their true coordinates. No larger/adaptive neighbourhood, robust weight, ridge, basis expansion or parameter sweep. Treat only the declared |x - 0.15y - 142| <= 8 band, with support wholly inside the admitted image. Store original observations as u16 CFA; image arithmetic and sufficient statistics are f32. Inject the existing per-plane noise model; no local private noise estimate or truth-radiance input.

The six unknowns are foreground/background intercepts and two slopes about the target native centre. Prediction uses the K=0 **unit-cell composite row**, not the centre's selected layer and not the alpha-weighted intercepts alone. A cell crossing the silhouette has nonzero first moments even when pupil blur is zero. The scene's declared depths, normal, offset, pupil scale, focus positions and identity geometry are explicit experimental oracle geometry. The estimator may not consume hidden amplitudes, phases, slopes or ideal target pixels.

Require at least 12 valid observations. Scale the 6x6 normal matrix by its diagonal, reject a normalized Schur pivot below 1e-4, before taking its Cholesky square root, and do not repair rank. This is a fixed engineering pivot rule, not a measured spectral condition-number bound. Reject nonfinite/negative predicted radiance or predicted variance. Require the predicted target variance q^T N^-1 q not to exceed the minimum injected single-observation variance among valid observations in that support. Invalid rows, values or variances must be refused without changing accumulated state. A fit's residual is recorded but does not set a cutoff: the held textured output must be able to expose affine-model mismatch rather than having a tunable residual veto.

The variance ceiling is a falsifiable software refusal, not proof of non-amplification against the much stronger baseline merge. The model coefficients weight their own synthetic observations, so numerical controls validate the estimator, not physical camera calibration. The real deciding noise control is the fixed flat temporal population below. Any insufficient/rank/variance/nonfinite refusal keeps the existing output value exactly; no-treatment must preserve its bits.

## Independent numerical controls

The accompanying Rust source uses independently specified affine coefficients and literal full-rank rows, explicit constant layers, exact half-disc moments, and exact zero-blur half-square first moments. These can reject wrong signs, coordinate origins, alpha-only prediction, or centre-layer output. An independent moment evaluation changes integration order: integrate clipped sensor-cell area and first moments at each pupil ray, then integrate the disc. It uses f64 only inside the test reference; product arithmetic remains f32. It does not call the production point/cell moment formula to manufacture its expected rows. Two fixed reference quadratures (96 radial by 384 angular, then 192 by 768) must agree within 4e-4 per moment before the production row is compared within 1e-3. These are numerical validation resolutions, not scored model settings. Explicit normalized Schur pivots near 5e-5 and 2e-4 must respectively refuse and accept; the accepted case queries a separately well-observed direction so the variance ceiling cannot hide rank-predicate mistakes.

A product-of-marginal-means destroyer deletes the background visibility/pupil covariance; the analytic half-disc must reject it. A four-native-pixel displaced boundary must change a named moment/prediction. Repeated identical visibility rows and a missing layer must abstain. A weak target direction must trip the variance ceiling. No-treatment includes signed zero and NaN payload preservation; an accepted estimate must actually change a different destination. Actual raw-colour indexing and final TIFF identity remain checks of the shared bridge/consumer, rather than inferred from the linear solver alone.

## Closed finite render comparison

The baseline is the current default HardFloor, with no pedestal or signed variant. Root runs exactly 18 feature-off and 18 candidate TIFFs: flat/textured scenes crossed with noise-off and the eight existing seeds 701, 709, 719, 727, 733, 739, 743, 751. The 18 new off TIFFs must also match their saved admitted M194g HardFloor counterparts. Add one textured/noise-off candidate using offset 146 instead of 142 (a +4-native-x displaced silhouette), plus one existing genuine-colour no-treatment off/on pair. These are fresh outputs under this experiment; no old M194g data are regenerated or changed.

Reuse the existing M194g production bridge, independent-rgb-tiff.py reader and independent-quality-metrics.py functions. Convert actual RGB16 codes using the existing 14848/65535 DN scale, with no second black subtraction. Reuse existing ideal targets and fixed native region definitions: window x=[104,216), y=[104,136); boundary |x-0.15y-142|<=8 intersected with that window; background/foreground surface and endpoint regions from the same saved reader. Keep all channels separate. No new renderer, TIFF parser or generic verifier is authorized by this protocol.

Score flat first. For each RGB channel and each fixed window/boundary region, candidate temporal variance over the eight exact seeds must not exceed baseline temporal variance. Also require non-worsening noise-off and eight-repeat-mean MSE against the independently known flat target; lower variance cannot excuse bias. Retain the existing paired delete-one uncertainty and repeat statistics as descriptive sampling limits, not a reason to erase a measured finite-population increase. Do not compare spatial variation to temporal sigma. If this flat gate fails, stop quality scoring/expansion and report the failure.

Only after flat passes, require strictly lower noise-off and eight-repeat-mean boundary MSE in every RGB channel, non-worsening full-window MSE, and non-worsening full-window maximum and 99.9th-percentile absolute error. Require non-worsening complex wave-coefficient error for each existing foreground/background wave and RGB channel in the fixed surface masks; amplitude alone is not fidelity. Keep the maximum worsening site and counts of changes above 10 DN visible. Pooled MSE can reward smoothing, temporal variance can reward bias, and amplitude can reward wrong phase/overshoot; none may substitute for its paired check. These are exact finite-output decision comparisons, with no fitted tolerance or retuned region.

The correct geometry must improve boundary MSE relative to the fixed shifted-geometry destroyer, and the destroyer must cause a nonzero row/prediction or acceptance/output difference. A shifted model may abstain; that is a model-sensitivity result, not evidence that native geometry has been estimated. If no candidate sites are accepted or no emitted output changes, report no demonstrated benefit, never successful safety as photographic improvement.

All feature-off and genuine-colour no-treatment identities are exact byte checks, with a live colour-destroying contrast in the existing colour fixture required to show that neutral imagery cannot make the test vacuous. Charge complete process time, owned CPU, peak memory and accepted/refused counts from real runs. If the fixed affine candidate loses texture, noise or extremes, stop it without a larger basis, neural prior, alternative window or new fixture. A win admits only the next question of obtaining usable native geometry/visibility; it cannot change the source default or establish SOTA.
