3. Link candidate centers and paired edges across adjacent source intervals in the map gauge. ## Original-RAW consumer and complete output

The likelihood must predict each **original physical CFA sensel**, using the inverse of the saved reference-to-source geometry (plus the already declared color-coordinate convention), then shared pupil and sensor-footprint integration. Do not fit against an already warped CFA buffer: its correlated interpolation noise and changed signal would undermine the original-sample claim. Preserve each source's active origin, physical phase, black/white levels and original input identity. White balance and output color development follow reconstruction.

Use sparse ribbon/contour objects with global IDs and a spatial index. For each output tile, load its radiance sheets and all objects intersecting the tile's maximum defocus/placement/footprint support. Predict, accumulate the one injected noise-aware likelihood and its gradients in bounded pupil batches; discard query intermediates immediately. Geometry is evaluated at each shared ray before compositing. Sheet radiance remains full native resolution even when geometry has few parameters. Do not retain an autograd graph over every source, ray and tile.

For source I/O, use an isolated, hash-bound **unaligned u16 CFA cache** outside the capture corpus, or one decoded source at a time. Any local layered result enters the complete image only where geometry, original-sample predictions and seam support pass; retain baseline fallback elsewhere. Call this a hybrid complete workflow, not a fully inferred global layered scene.

## Memory and unresolved physical limits

**DERIVED budget, not measured:** at 7032×4688, one RGB f32 sheet is 395,592,192 bytes. Three full sheets (two ribbons plus background) require 1,186,776,576 bytes; parameters, gradients and two optimizer moments alone would require 4,747,106,304 bytes, already above 4 GiB before observations or GPU runtime. Full-frame Adam over three sheets is therefore outside the proposed envelope. A 512×512 tile's three RGB sheets total 9 MiB before support apron, optimizer state, ray batches and cache. A bounded tile/source implementation is plausible, but its actual peak and full-stack passes must be measured; low local GPU allocation is not evidence of practical full-photo time.

Opaque silhouettes do not model translucent hairs, refractive crystal boundaries, pupil-dependent specularity, wavelength-dependent diffraction, aberrations, polarization or focus-dependent illumination. A circular pupil and signed defocus scalar are synthetic assumptions until checked against this rig; Sony metadata does not by itself calibrate them. A common blur after geometric visibility is an approximation, particularly at subpixel edges. Clipped measurements provide censored constraints, not known highlight radiance. Unknown sheet detail can remain unobservable even with correct geometry.

The retained fixed-visibility result improved ridges but still undershot protected glints by about 3.6–4.7% and failed a bright held-prediction requirement. Removing free alpha cannot be credited with solving those radiance problems. The earlier marigold fit lowered held prediction error while visibly harming the image. Require flat-noise, color-only, glint and other-depth preservation alongside geometry; neither smooth boundaries nor lower fit loss are success.

**Stop rule:** if the small independent forward controls fail, do not fit a photograph. If the automatic proposal cannot explain the frozen two-structure family without hand labels, or no bounded tile/source execution plan can preserve its support and runtime, close this route as currently impractical and return to a different source-supported photographic hypothesis. Do not turn either result into a general claim against layered reconstruction.
