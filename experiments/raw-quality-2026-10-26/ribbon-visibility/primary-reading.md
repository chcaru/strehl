# Bounded follow-up on implicit image-stack reconstruction

2026-10-27. Root retrieved the authors' [TAF project page](https://taf.mpi-inf.mpg.de/) and indexed primary-paper sections3.1-3.2 plus supplement section1 through web search. Opening either full main-PDF URL failed because its49,567,016-byte size exceeds the browser limit. This is a partial primary-source reading, not a review of the full paper, its results or implementation.

The [main paper](https://taf.mpi-inf.mpg.de/resource/An%20Implicit%20Neural%20Representation%20for%20the%20Image%20Stack%3A%20Depth%2C%20All%20in%20Focus%2C%20and%20High%20Dynamic%20Range.pdf) describes coordinate MLPs for flow, depth and all-in-focus radiance, followed by a differentiable disk-defocus model and tone mapping. Its shown normalized spatially varying disk operator does not itself express shared-ray first-hit visibility. That displayed equation cannot replace the occlusion controls commissioned here. The approach supports considering spatial implicit fields as a representation; it does not establish native-CFA reconstruction or validity for this macro rig.

The [supplement](https://taf.mpi-inf.mpg.de/resource/Supp_An%20Implicit%20Neural%20Representation%20for%20the%20Image%20Stack%3A%20Depth%2C%20All%20in%20Focus%2C%20and%20High%20Dynamic%20Range.pdf) describes SIREN predictors and reports about22minutes for2000 training iterations on five256x384 images using an RTX8000. This is the authors' reported configuration, not a Strehl timing or an extrapolation to RTX5090/full-resolution RAW. A direct adoption would require a practical source/tile implementation and independent photographic evidence. No external implementation was read or copied, no network/model was trained, and no new experiment is dispatched by this note.
