# Continuous thin-structure visibility from original CFA observations

2026-10-26/27. Root changes architectural direction after the shared-color guide's actual adverse blue-detail loss. This is a new bounded research prototype, not a photographic improvement or a remedy already shown on insects.

## Changed question and prior limits

The current shared guide passes flat and crossing controls but cannot preserve different-source color evidence in its adverse case. Earlier guarded independent R/B guidance already completed a vitamin-C photograph and increased magenta/cyan fringes without useful structure (`rb-residual-full/results.md`). Do not restart that recipe under another name. Native positive kernels, ordinary local-placement interventions and free per-pixel alpha also have qualified negative records.

Known visibility materially improved the earlier layered RAW inverse, including genuine ridge components, but did not satisfy its bright/glint requirements. The actual marigold free-alpha fit used a CFA-frequency opacity lattice to fit data while harming the image. The changed representation is continuous, depth-ordered opaque thin geometry with explicit widths and endpoints, rather than one independently fitted opacity per sensel. Full-resolution radiance remains separate from geometric complexity. Removing a harmful degree of freedom does not prove that remaining radiance inference is safe.

Use the existing `structured-visibility/independent-design-review.md`, especially its shared-pupil and footprint warning, and actual `layered-raw-inverse/independent-visibility-oracle-results.md` and `marigold-constrained-raw` reports. The saved-alpha diagnostic's0.04175DN numerical control failure did not reach its intervention; it is not evidence against this new representation. Do not spend this allocation repairing that diagnostic.

Primary prior art is [Liu, Dubrawski and Narasimhan, CVPR2017](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ILIM/projects/IM/MultiLayer/), which explicitly models thin structures and mutual defocus occlusion. This is not a novelty claim or permission to substitute products of marginal coverages for shared-ray visibility. Root read the author page and paper abstract; no external implementation was consulted.

## Minimal implementation target

Represent two finite opaque ribbons at different depths over a background. Start with the smallest continuous straight/capsule or low-order curve representation that can express widths below one native pixel, a crossing, endpoints and independent depth order. Producer and independent reviewer must agree on exact coordinates, optical assumptions and subpixel integration before root execution. Do not build a broad spline/topology framework yet.

At each original physical CFA query, evaluate depth order along the same pupil ray and sensor-footprint subpoint before averaging. Store observations as u16 CFA and perform reconstruction arithmetic in f32, using the one injected sensor model. Never create an aligned-frame volume or use a demosaiced RGB stack as observations. Use the available GPU for bounded ray/footprint batches, and retain a separate independent dense reference for selected controls. Supply geometry/PSF units explicitly; these are synthetic operator assumptions, not a calibration of the Sony rig.

First demonstrate the observation path on flat/equal-color, known translated thin structure, two differently colored crossing depths, wrong front/back order and a no-structure/glint foil. True thin structures must survive at multiple Bayer phases. A symmetric blur change must not be relabeled as geometric displacement. A translucent object is an explicit model-mismatch case; opaque coverage is not physical transmittance. Numerical tolerances must be justified by sampling/integration precision before data are scored.

Then one bounded unknown-geometry inference on a fixed generated source set may be prepared, keeping radiance and optical assumptions explicit. The independent truth must not be the same implementation re-evaluated. Require actual source-prediction and final pinhole-image evidence; lower training loss or a smooth mask alone is not success. No full-resolution photographic claim follows from this small test.

## Practical photographic path and stopping

Before an original-photo fit, identify a plausible automatic proposal/initialization and a complete-image consumption path, including uncertainty fallback and preservation of other-depth detail. Do not treat hand-drawn curves as a general stacker. The retained marigold evidence may exercise visibility; the weevil remains the primary insect acceptance stack, with bug2 and flat/mineral transfer still required. Complete all-source outputs, full-resolution masters and independent native inspection remain mandatory for every claimed photographic gain.

Preparation bounds: no new RAW reads, no heavy execution by agents, no install, no default changes. Root schedules each first run. Target one GPU consumer with current-source batches, below4GiB host/GPU allocation and a120second small-test budget; this is a proposed envelope to verify, not a measured performance claim. Avoid a long investigation of one thin-structure crop. Stop or change the exact question if the cheap independent discriminator fails; failures of one representation do not disprove layered reconstruction generally.
