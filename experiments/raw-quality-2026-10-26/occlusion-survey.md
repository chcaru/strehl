## 2. Structured visibility, with CFA leakage separated from representation gauge

Changed premise: the failed fit used independently free native-pixel alpha. The observed alpha acquired near-Nyquist oscillations while the two radiance sheets were comparatively smooth; the saved algebra attributes most returned high-frequency error to `(alpha_final-alpha_initial)*(near_final-far_final)`. This is concrete adverse evidence about the representation, not proof that all RAW inverse models fail.

The useful architectural alternative is a compact geometric visibility description: continuous oriented strand/edge boundaries with subpixel coverage from integrated geometry, and separate high-resolution radiance. Do not let each Bayer-lattice site independently encode optical/model disagreement as opacity. This representation must allow multiple crossing depth layers and real subpixel hairs; a generic smooth alpha grid would erase the very detail being protected. It must eventually infer boundaries from original CFA observations at runtime, not retain manual scribbles or treat elected focus indices as opacity.

Primary literature supports the distinction, not a result on this corpus: Liu, Dubrawski and Narasimhan's CVPR2017 project models mutually occluding thin structures and optical blur with layer geometry and mattes. Their author page describes analytic differential updates to avoid full rerendering during inference: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ILIM/projects/IM/MultiLayer/. Only the author page was successfully read; the paper PDF fetch timed out, and no implementation source was opened. This is prior art, not a novelty claim or evidence that its exact inference is suitable for Bayer data.

Before implementing this alternative, run the already proposed one-shot saved-field falsifier from marigold-constrained-raw/results.md: fixed3x3 binomial average of final alpha, restore its54 seeds, leave both radiances fixed, then compare original held-source predictions and returned composite. This has not been executed in the reports inspected. Nearly unchanged predictions with loss of lattice demonstrate a weakly observed harmful coverage mode; large prediction degradation shows that the model uses the mode to explain data/mismatch. Neither outcome is itself an admitted repair.

A prospective structured model then needs an independent generated flat first, two crossing thin structures at different depths, a Bayer-period disagreement foil and changing background. Evaluate held sensel phases and held focus positions, retaining noise uncertainty from the single injected sensor model. Require correct center/width/topology on the known geometry and unchanged output under the exact harmless alpha/radiance gauge.
