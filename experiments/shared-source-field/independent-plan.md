## Choices and rejection power

The charter's relative excess ratio fixes the earlier saturating unary before
measurement. It still measures local green detail, not ownership. Four Potts
paths can make the wrong late fan coherent. Raw-cost spread is evidence
activation, not a best-versus-second-best confidence: two equally good depths
plus one poor source can activate. A common green guide may have no brightness
edge at a real depth boundary. The tests deliberately include those cases.

The source field must control the actual shared four-plane, full-AC consumer.
An attractive index map, smoothness, or matching the proposed arithmetic earns
no quality credit. The injected model is shared with the noise plant: these
controls test an operator, never calibrate the A7C II sensor.

A specific limitation is testable: if the focused source has r=0 at a tail
and the late source alone has positive r, their unary gap is1. Four messages
bounded by .25 can at best tie that gap; a bright edge in the baseline guide
reduces the available penalty further. Spatial agreement cannot be presumed
to infer the remote compact core. The fan truth bar remains mandatory.

## Score commissioning and limits

Exact truth, half-amplitude truth, neutralized colour and1.4x contrast are
commissioned before image scoring. RGB RMSE can reward smoothing; pair it
with template recovery. Recovery can reward overcontrast/correlated wrong
structure, so cap it at1 and pair it with RGB/chroma error. Flat RMS is a
same-domain error against constant truth, not sensor calibration. A forced
wrong-source replay must worsen fan truth or fail retained-detail bars; if it
does neither, the quality fixture is not an effective destroyer and admission
is refused. No gradient-energy or field-coherence quality score is used.
