## Records and exact provenance

`fields` and `frames` are small named catalogs using the existing model's
constructor fields. Each case names a frame, foreground/background fields,
literal mask/depth records, native coordinate, call kind and required arms.
Resolve those references directly; do not supply defaults or fit a new phase,
gain, coordinate or reference. A later adapter can turn those records into
`Scene`, `Frame`, `render_cell`/`render_point` calls without a new general
certificate framework. `author_methods` is a prospective semantic choice:
analytic-enabled/disabled for cells; frozen-point for the one point oracle.
It is not a claim that an implementation already exposes those entry points.

The original geometry authority is the unchanged
`independent-geometry-controls-2/result.json`, SHA256
`a178a9d32f49b8f2b2af8737f7285de89d9ea70abe0e3be7cf5bb9002c3796b4`.
Every reused case names its original check and retains its saved exact height.
The original120 check records, including rounded extreme-ray heights and
classification, remain there. None was generated again or replaced.

For a saved tangent record, reconstruct offset exactly as
`m*[3*(q0.x+s.x)+4*(q0.y+s.y)] - saved_exact_height`, with rational arithmetic,
where q0 is(7,11) for identity or(15/4,37/8) for scale2, and m/s are the
registered mask multiplier/origin shift. The result is exactly binary64
representable; serialization checked that identity. The forward centre is
centre+s and translation is translation-s. Rotated offsets are copied directly
from the saved eight records. Neither step calls a selector or renderer.

These120 radiance cases retain the original constant F15000/B9000 fields.
Their field transform under the canonical origin change is therefore literally
unchanged: affine coefficients0, no waves. There is no claim of large-coordinate

Other frequencies/phases are **DERIVED parameter serialization**, not evaluated

The legacy cases retain original frame/scene coordinates and externally hashed

## Equality, power, refusal and resources

Require all IDs unique, all catalog references resolved, all declared counts
and source hashes exact, finite numeric values and complete case/method/arm
coverage. Reject duplicate keys, unknown/missing required fields, invalid
types or source substitutions. This is a bounded input reader for these
records; its successful parse is not a numerical commission.

The named actual-consuming faults have fixed target IDs and an error criterion
strictly greater than.004e. The untouched/restored companion must meet the
ordinary.004e component limit; textured component RMS stays.0008e. Equality
at.004e does not provide the specified fault power. A zero-over-zero mutation
must refuse as nonfinite; duplicate/source mutations must refuse admission.
An inert fault remains a failed commissioning control: do not choose another
phase or amplitude to rescue it. The equal-field visibility cases are scalar
nulls and cannot establish correct branch geometry by their values.
