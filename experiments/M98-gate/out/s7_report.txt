H3 / prediction (c) -- is mean n_eff a description of where detail is?
==============================================================================
  No per-pixel n_eff map exists on the shipped CLI (grep of
  crates/strehl-cli/src/*.rs finds no map output), so PREREG's
  declared substitution applies: local n_eff is estimated from the
  ROI's own above-cutoff noise floor, n_eff_loc = floor(0)/floor(T).
  BIAS DIRECTION (rule 18): in a detailed ROI, signal leaks into the
  f in [0.30,0.45] floor band, so floor(T)/floor(0) is pulled toward
  1 and n_eff_loc is UNDERSTATED. The bias runs TOWARD H3. H3 is
  therefore only reportable if it FAILS -- which is what happens.
