Disposition: retain this as a **DERIVED constraint; do not execute a standalone Bayer witness**. The points below are requirements for a future actual source-motion forward regression, not a new experiment authorization.

First, declare the optical model being tested. Circular continuous optics, a finite discrete pupil acting on a continuous sinusoid, and the actual pupil acting on a bilinear reconstruction of native samples are different operators. The seven-focus even/odd split is conditional on inversion symmetry. The general diagonal swapped-alias claim requires swap symmetry; an arbitrarily rotated finite pupil need not have it exactly. The specific proposed axis-Nyquist pair needs only quarter-turn symmetry plus inversion, which the equal-angle dense pupil has in real arithmetic. Verify numerical leakage rather than assuming an exact stored-float null. The common separable PSF treats the two axis modes equally; its nonzero response must be witnessed, not inferred from the word diffraction.

Second, apply a known translation **once at each ray address**: `x+t+d*u`, to premultiplied near radiance, alpha and far radiance before composition, then the existing PSF and CFA. Do not translate an already rendered RGB raster, and do not pre-resample the latent raster and sample it again. A bilinear reconstruction's fractional phase response includes attenuation and is not simply the continuous `exp(i*w*t)` phase factor. Half-pixel x/y translations can expose this pair, but the half-pixel diagonal is a weak negative: in symmetric cases it can annihilate each Nyquist-axis mode separately. Use a fixed quarter-pixel diagonal negative, or independently require each individual translated mode to retain a nonzero response while their CFA difference cancels. This tests cancellation rather than disappearance.

Third, choose one boundary convention. The actual operator uses zero extension; generate full96 worlds and score the retained16:80 interior after all optics. For shifts of at most half a pixel, radius12 and the radius3 PSF, that margin covers the integer sampling support. Do not introduce circular image shifts and then claim a result about this zero-extended consumer. A Fourier calculation may use an infinite/periodic lattice, but label it as a separate derivation. Represent the native Nyquist patterns by exact integer parity, avoiding avoidable trigonometric construction leakage.

For a future source-motion regression, freeze DC, perturbation amplitude and phase schedule, and require both worlds remain positive and uncensored. Keep the zero perturbation, live lower-frequency colour positive, separate axis-mode survival witnesses, identity pair, diagonal negative and non-collinear positive. Compare **expected original CFA samples** and the unchanged reference-gauge delivered `B(P)` between the two worlds. Price finite leakage against the injected model; report both per-sample normalized RMS and the aggregate whitened difference when discussing distinguishability. A tiny nonzero singular value or per-sample discrepancy alone is not a noise-recoverability claim. Any Fourier operator must be the actual declared tent/PSF/translation operator, not a circular-optics surrogate.

**Decision value:** the standalone identity Bayer ambiguity is largely predetermined and cannot diagnose the saved free-flat failure. The stronger project precedent is `experiments/quality-september14/results.md:73`: independent continuous-scene colour/alias controls and full rank did not prevent the real joint inverse from producing periodic cyan/blue stipple and worse noise. The same report's lines83–85 show that corrected geometry weakened apparent diversity and that four prescribed residual updates also failed photographically. A new ideal rank witness would not overturn this evidence.

Therefore stop this standalone diagnostic. Preserve the positive/live-diagonal controls only when source motion is actually implemented in a reconstruction whose original-CFA observations and delivered image can be tested. Such controls can reject a broken forward model; they cannot themselves choose a successful inverse, explain the saved colour errors or establish usable diversity in the real stacks. The next funded choice should change an image-producing operator with a falsifiable quality comparison, not merely add another proof of mathematical possibility.
