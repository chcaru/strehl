# Independent pre-data screen: CA before MHC

Root executes; the numerical author does not verify this work. This is a fixed, bounded generated test followed, only if admitted, by one complete photographic pair. It does not validate optical-profile calibration or promise to fix the vitamin-C source fans.

The current arm applies MHC to the original white-balanced CFA, then a composed colour/alignment pull. The candidate resamples each original colour lattice to remove lateral CA before MHC, then applies the common alignment pull. The staged-after arm first performs MHC, then separate CA and alignment pulls. Staged-after is an order/two-pass comparator, **not a blur-matched placebo**: its native RGB lattice differs from the candidate's two-native-pixel R/B lattice.

Use original u16 RGGB fixtures, odd decoded active origin, asymmetric guarded native crop, warm WB, and the injected A7C II noise model at ISO100/400. No derived noise constant. Store neither aligned source volumes nor f16. The actual signal and original-noise probe must use the same chosen gather. Report the resulting guide/probe/floor evidence for each arm; a changed noise transfer cannot silently reuse the current arm's beta.

Fixed scenes are flat first; nearly achromatic chip-like edges and fine lines; genuine chromatic edges plus live R/G/B fine components; and the same coloured signal with source-dependent, spatially varying defocus. Five sources, both identity and one fixed fractional/rotation alignment sequence. Generate original sensor samples directly from continuous analytic radiance by independently inverting colour and common pulls, with no pre-resampled truth image. Use the actual profile's radial derivatives with independent per-colour affine offsets representing a fixed off-centre patch of a 7032x4688 sensor. This tests the order at realistic local displacements, not the calibration of those derivatives. Score in the output-native gauge after the same final MHC camera-linear consumer, within a fixed support margin.

Mechanical controls precede quality: zero-CA and absent-CA bypass are exact for every arm; identity green known sites preserve original rounded DN; distinct CFA site constants and affine colour ramps catch ordering/phase errors; source arrays are immutable; unsupported colour-lattice pulls refuse even when the old common pull is covered. The staged-after green CA is identity, so its green inputs, actual probe and final green CFA planes must equal current exactly. A known nonzero-CA neutral scene must distinguish correct from wrong-sign correction, and the correction must actually change samples. Fractional crop/phase and the odd decoded origin are explicit. CPU/MHC or analytic checks must not reuse the candidate's coefficient-generation code.

Fixed quality admission (per ISO and phase, no pooling hides a failure): flat RGB and R-G/B-G error RMS must be at most 1.05 times current; every structured scene's RGB and chroma error RMS must be at most 1.05 times current; every live true-colour component must retain at least 0.98 of current truth-capped recovery. Current component recovery below 0.5 marks that component unexercised and refuses admission. On the nearly achromatic scene, candidate chroma error must improve at least 5% over current at both ISOs/phase choices. Report staged-after against the identical truth and bars, without requiring it to pass. Wrong-sign correction must be worse than correct on the noiseless neutral scene. These are permissions for a photographic test, not promotion criteria.

Truth errors can reward smoothing when detail is absent, so genuine colour components and sharp edges are mandatory. Component retention alone can reward manufactured contrast, so recovery is capped at truth and accompanied by RGB/chroma error. Half-detail and one additional fixed blur of exact truth must fail the component or structured-error guards; desaturating exact truth must fail the colour guard. A score that cannot reject these destroyers is not admitted. Noise suppression alone is not sharpness or colour recovery.

No profile fitting, kernel/radius sweep, new demosaic, changed merge weighting, or automatic source-ownership claim is in scope. A small-test failure remains recorded; it must not be bypassed by describing reduced chroma as quality. Root owns fresh compilation, controls and any complete chip/slide render.
