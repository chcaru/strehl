# Independent design and controls: consistent reconstruction

Frozen before execution, 2026-09-25. Root owns builds and runs. The operator and consumer authors do not own these controls. This is one generated-only test; no RAW job or photographic admission is implicit.

## What the intervention can and cannot do

Let A be the exact finite linear matrix represented by current `Pyramid::build`, including all difference bands and the terminal Gaussian. Let S be current expand-add collapse. In ideal arithmetic `S A = I`, but `A S` is generally an oblique projection and is not the identity on arbitrary coefficient arrays. Thus the canonical dual `(A^T A)^-1 A^T` can differ from S on bands fused from different source mixtures. It must agree on realizable untouched pyramids. This is a real, falsifiable change, not a claim that the existing inverse is incorrect.

The objective is the ordinary unweighted sum of squared residuals over every stored coefficient of one CFA plane. Four planes are solved independently. No division by level pixel count, coefficient-noise variance or weight sum is introduced. This assigns more total residual terms to fine levels than coarse ones; it is a declared metric choice, not sensor likelihood or a Bayesian/physical optimum. Terminal coefficients remain targets but the reconstructed terminal, finest analysis band, mean and brightness need not remain identical. Source coefficients, weights, fallback, input bounds, geometry and injected noise remain exact.

Do and Vetterli's [Framing Pyramids (2003)](https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/framing-pyramids/) supplies direct prior art for alternative reconstruction of modified Laplacian coefficients. Their result concerns frame reconstruction and perturbation/noise assumptions; it does not prove improvement for biased, correlated focus-fusion errors. Author/university metadata and the indexed primary abstract were consulted; no external implementation was read. The author PDF endpoint was blocked by its web verification screen. No novelty claim is made.

Two algebraic warnings are commissioned: inconsistent coefficients must produce a nonzero change, and a synthesis-null perturbation can make least squares introduce error into an image for which ordinary collapse already returns the chosen zero truth. Reducing coefficient residual is therefore not the quality gate. This route does not infer a second physical depth, undo source veil or repair geometry, and does not reopen prior layered/visibility/depth-selector failures.

## Fixed numerics

One preconditioned CG solve, at most 32 iterations, fixed before results. The parameter-free preconditioner is `S S^T`; it changes convergence, not the objective. The solver works on correction delta about canonical collapse x0 with actual f32 targets `C-A(x0)` retained once. It recomputes the correction residual for admission rather than trusting a recursive CG residual. The actual full-image objective is separately recorded and must not increase.

The error bar is 0.02 camera DN **RMS**, including final x0+delta addition rounding. In exact linear arithmetic, `S A=I` gives `S S^T=(A^T A)^-1+Z Z^T`. With nonnegative synthesis entries and `B=levels*max(S^T ones)`, both `B*norm(r)` and `sqrt(B)*norm(S^T r)` upper-bound image L2 error. The smaller bound, divided by sqrt(native plane samples), is used. This is a numerically commissioned exact-linear bound; it is **not an interval enclosure of every f32 rounding operation**. The explicit correction system and actual full objective must not be conflated after finite arithmetic.

The terminal DC information is sparse by approximately 4^-L, so a small relative normal residual alone is not accepted. Nonfinite values, curvature breakdown or a cap reached without the declared accuracy are explicit refusals. Refused iterates remain diagnostic only. The adapter may return baseline placeholders to save a complete screen, but `candidate_valid=false` means no candidate quality scoring and failed numerical qualification.

Pre-execution norm amendment following root review: dense-oracle solved-image accuracy uses the same 0.02 DN RMS bar, with max error bounded by sqrt(n)*0.02 DN. The latter is a consequence of the RMS contract, not a separate strong accuracy claim. An initial draft's max<=0.02 solver assertion was stricter than the declared stopping contract and was corrected before any build or result. The separate untouched forward/collapse round-trip check retains max<=0.02 DN.
