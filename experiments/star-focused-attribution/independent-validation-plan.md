Only actual gathered source versus merged output has an exact common grid and
development path. Inspect all three sources at native scale before selecting

boundary present in an actual gathered source and missing or conflated at the
same output location after merging. Record other selected sources' appearance
at that location and a second defensible subregion so one bright point or a
favourable source does not carry the conclusion. Rims, adjacent pore pairs and
the grain contour provide different structural comparisons; increased grain,
edge amplitude or coloured stipple alone is not a positive finding.

If raw context seems structurally better but the gathered patch does not retain
it, distinguish a possible gather-path limitation from a merge limitation and
retain the geometry/CA caveat. If the selected sources and merge show no clear
missing feature, report a bounded null: no acquisition limit, perfect-detail
retention, noise-equivalence or renewed placement claim follows. These are
structured photographs, with no independent optical truth or flat-noise arm.

inconsistency; do not repair it with a registration fit or tolerance. Successful
