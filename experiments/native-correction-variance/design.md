## Actual consumer readout

Let e_arm,j,k=Lap_k(noisy arm luma)-Lap_k(own noiseless arm luma). At each coefficient compute the unbiased temporal sample variance across64 seeds, then use the actual production smooth5 and the frozen ROI mean to obtain V_arm. Equivalently this is 64/63 times [mean_j smooth5(e_j²)-smooth5((mean_j e_j)²)]. Do not subtract a spatial image mean, smooth along the seed axis, or replace smooth5 with another filter.

The measured quantity is DeltaV=V_corrected-V_off, never Var(e_corrected-e_off). Compute its standard error by paired delete-one-seed jackknife of that complete unbiased statistic, preserving corrected/off pairing. Report raw second moment and ensemble mean/bias information separately: the squared sample mean itself contains finite-ensemble noise and is not automatically deterministic bias. Statistical reductions may use the existing higher-precision scalar accumulator; image kernels remain the current f32 operations.

## Frozen decision and rejecting inputs

For each of the32 rows (8 cells x2 bands x2 ROIs), tau=max(0.10*abs(DeltaV),3*paired-jackknife-SE,0.002*V_off). Candidate error must satisfy abs(PredDelta-DeltaV)<=tau in every row. Every core row must additionally have DeltaV>0 and 3*SE<=0.25*DeltaV. Failure of the precision condition is INCONCLUSIVE, not a successful approximation. Adjacent has no positivity requirement; retain signed absolute discrepancies, and label a near-zero delta uninformative about a relative gain. The baseline floor term prevents an unstable ratio from winning at zero. This is a finite engineering bar, not a calibrated confidence guarantee.

Power must also be demonstrated on the actual ensemble: replace proper shot d*k*Y with d²*k*Y, and proper read d²*r² with d*r². Each wrong factor must violate the SAME tau in both identity-flat core bands for its noise component. If the small gain cannot separate either wrong model, declare INCONCLUSIVE even if the candidate fits. Independently chosen algebraic controls demonstrate code sensitivity but do not replace this measurement-power requirement. No larger gain, sample-count extension, threshold sweep or rescue population follows.

Unity/off must have exactly equal relevant consumed planes, luma, variance and band bytes/hashes, with live nonidentity and gain witnesses. No observer may be treated as harmless by argument; any newly introduced observer needs its matched hash placebo. Wrong gain factors are concrete model falsifiers; varying gains across reconstruction support and correlated G1/G2 contributions can reject the local approximation.
