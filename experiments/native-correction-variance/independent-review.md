Each arm subtracts its own noiseless processed coefficients. It is not the variance of corrected-minus-off. The standard error is the exact paired delete-one-seed jackknife of that complete statistic. Retaining these tiny diagnostic band coefficients for this purpose is preferable to a second gather pass; no aligned CFA/RGB stack is required. A 32 independent two-seed-pair alternative was considered before root clarified 64 paired corrected/off observations; it is not used. Neither uncentred raw-moment uncertainty nor the squared ensemble mean is substituted for temporal variance or deterministic bias.

The independent source adapts the author's actual variance/jackknife routine to scalar-degenerate inputs. Independent f64 literal sums and leave-one calculations provide the oracle for unity, deterministic pedestal, gain plus pedestal, and a nonlinear paired sequence. A live variance-of-difference destroyer is more than 20 times below the proper variance increment on its nominated input. A separate pair of noiseless constant observations requires zero variance despite changed brightness. These are arithmetic controls on the measurement path; the actual spatial noiseless pedestal and unity/branch witnesses remain separate producer obligations.

The decision requires exactly 32 unique mapping/field/component/band/region rows, each from 64 observations. Candidate error is measured against tau=max(0.10*abs(delta),3*paired jackknife SE,0.002*Voff). Every core increment must be positive and have 3SE<=0.25delta. The nominated wrong shot and read factors must each be rejected in identity-flat core bands0 and1 by the same tolerance. Failure to resolve those conditions is INCONCLUSIVE, never an automatic candidate pass; sufficiently resolved prediction failures remain individually recorded as REJECT evidence. No sample-count, gain, radius, noise or threshold extension is allocated.

The prediction must include both the current model's change from brighter corrected signal and the missing known-gain term. In corrected-signal coordinates the proper shot term is d*k*Y and read is d²*r². The wrong models are d²*k*Y and d*r² respectively. The reported prediction is corrected minus off after the fixed beta/variance reduction, not merely the added term. Baseline floor bias is reported separately and never fitted away. The source decision controls explicitly allow a shared baseline bias while rejecting a wrong increment, and require inconclusive status for uncertain, zero-response or insensitive wrong-factor cases.
