# Independent interpretation of signed through-focus traces

**Retain a dark-region association hypothesis; do not admit an automatic
ownership discriminator or merge.** The result is not uniformly
nondistinctive: raw-DN changes connect the dark core with both extensions more
strongly than with the competing ridge. That finding does not transfer to the
facet, and signed finest-Laplacian box means fail the spatial-sensitivity and
ridge-positive checks. No new threshold, interval, channel, lag or coordinate
was selected in this review.

For the full dark boxes, every CFA plane supports the positive DN relationship;
it is not created by summing opposed colour signs. Column plane cosines range
0.561–0.788 and fan plane cosines 0.727–0.806 across the two translations.
Dark target permutation gives joint values about 0.05. However, the column's
shifted nulls are not negligible: roughly −0.51 and +0.42 at their strongest
full-box settings. An absolute-coupling rule would credit the negative null,
too. Target reversal retains dark DN cosine 0.36–0.43, showing that broad trace
shape contributes; permutation rejection alone does not isolate ownership.

VISUALLY REVIEWED all three signed-trace plots and unsigned energy context.
The strong dark DN association is largely a shared late broad brightening,
while the nearby ridge stays comparatively steady. It does not locate the
previously observed compact source 33 or prove material flows from core to tail.
The fixed source range ends while several dark means are still rising. No
derivative maximum, focus peak or width is inferred. The ridge changes are only
about 2–6 DN RMS per adjacent source in these means, versus tens to hundreds for
the glint regions; that amplitude difference limits its value as a matched
nuisance control, without turning it into a calibrated noise measurement.

The facet is an explicit colour-cancellation counterexample. Its full-box DN
cosines are R −0.382, G1 +0.075, G2 +0.068, B −0.247; the joint −0.028 becomes +0.022
after one quad. Adjacent structure is more strongly anticoupled, about −0.49.
Neither unsigned energy nor choosing the favourable sign repairs this evidence.

Signed L0 is not a reliable substitute. Dark-column joint cosine changes
+0.182 to −0.116 after one quad; R/B are negative while one green can be strongly
positive. Facet full-box L0 association about 0.196 collapses to 0.036–0.046 on
the checker subsets. Ridge-continuation L0 is near zero and can be exceeded by
shifted or permuted comparisons. Dark-fan L0 remains positive, but target
reversal is comparable to the actual relationship. Signed box averages can
cancel interior detail and expose boundary/phase effects; these failures do
not establish absence of a real optical relationship. Checker subsets share
samples' filtering context and are not independent noise repetitions.

The concrete surviving hypothesis is that the dark core and both branches
share a through-focus brightness component absent from this nearby ridge.
These data do not distinguish that component from extended radiometric change,
clipping or mixed scene structure, and supply neither automatic seeds nor a
preferred source. Any later use must separate association from compact-source
choice and protect the weaker ridge. A general signed-L0 discriminator is
rejected here; the narrow DN observation is not photographic-quality admission.
