F2  ELECTION NEUTRALITY
    --index-map with vs without --photometric.
    Focus is contrast/sigma; scaling data by c scales contrast by c, and
    propagating c into the signal-dependent noise model scales sigma by c, so
    the ratio is EXACTLY invariant. The election must therefore barely move.
    BAR: differing pixels < 0.01% of plane pixels.
    (M99 measured 34 px of 8.25M = 0.0004% and 22 px of 8.24M.)
    This is the claim that lets me tell ORCH the corpus is not invalidated.

    held -- which is precisely the bug M99 warns is silent.

    PASS = the plant moves it. FAIL = the propagation is not connected to the
