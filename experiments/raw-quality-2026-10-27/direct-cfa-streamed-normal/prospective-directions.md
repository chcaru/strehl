## Decision

One direction merits a bounded discriminator: **continuous geometric visibility
with independently textured, independently colored radiance at multiple depths**.
Do not launch another selector-exponent, source-count, scalar blur or generic
spatial-coherence variant. Do not dispatch a second speculative architecture in
parallel. A conditional generated positive would qualify mechanics only; actual
photographic evidence must earn any complete parametric reconstruction.

The current model measures original sensels correctly, but its prediction is
still `A_i X`, with noise/focus-weighted Haar residuals. A does not include
defocus, aperture-dependent first-hit visibility, focus-dependent radiance or
optical aberration. Different-depth contributions sharing an observed sensel
must therefore be reconciled through weights and a single latent image. That
is a missing modeling capability, **not evidence that it caused this run's
softness, halo or color error**. A good result would first demonstrate that the
previous sampling/reconstruction stages mattered. A bad result must be
interpreted against the retained flat-noise failure before blaming optics.

## Concrete changed mechanism

Retain the current original-u16 readers, per-sensel phase, held global geometry,
injected noise, f32 arithmetic and final direct-RGB development. Change the
scene evaluated inside the original sensor-cell/pupil integral. For every
sensor subpoint and pupil ray, intersect an ordered set of continuous surfaces;
take radiance from the first hit, and only then average. One far textured
surface and finite foreground ribbons are the smallest nontrivial case.

Use continuous finite boundaries/depth parameters rather than a free CFA-grid
opacity field. Radiance is separate: each visible surface has its own spatial
camera-RGB coefficients, with no fixed R/G or B/G ratio and no copied green
texture. The renderer must preserve both a near fine strand and a far coarse
colored pattern; it must not replace that sum of depth contributions by one
full-pixel source label. Crossing structures keep alternative depth order and
finite end caps. Missing-object and single-surface alternatives are explicit.
An absent/unsupported foreground cannot be manufactured to improve residuals.

This is a limited extension of the capsule consumer, not a claim to have solved
a general surface engine. The first proposed change is independently varying
texture/color on the ribbons and background, then unknown continuous geometry;
not more capsules, more optimizer steps or a free opacity lattice. A native
reference-cell image is the declared output. Any retained in-focus optical
response must be stated explicitly, rather than silently promising a pinhole
image sharper than the evidence supports.

Specular scale/glint brightness can change with capture without any visibility
change. A fixed Lambertian-radiance model may explain it by falsely widening a
ribbon or changing its depth. Focus breathing also requires a consistent
reference-ray gauge. Low residuals cannot distinguish these alternatives.
Sony focus codes can order captures, but do not supply a calibrated distance,
pupil, defocus trajectory or polarized-reflectance model. Those assumptions
need independent existing-data support before an actual photographic inverse.

## One small direction test, then a real-data decision

Propose one fixed64x48 original-CFA domain and seven focus views, with a full
support apron, five training views and two held views. Reuse the independently
integrated shared-ray reference, original noise authority and complete-image
scoring. Start with flat/no-object. The decisive positive has two finite
crossing ribbons, independently varying RGB texture along them, and a coarse
colored background at a different depth. The texture must not belong to the
fitter's low-order radiance basis. Record the known-geometry conditional result
separately from the unknown-geometry result. Neither is photographic evidence.

Keep just the necessary adverse/null controls: equal-depth/no-occlusion;
wrong order and product-of-marginal-coverages; true weak-green R/B texture;
known near/far component erasure; a view-varying glint with no object; and a
non-Gaussian annular/asymmetric pupil mismatch. Exercise the same producing
model and scoring path. Reject new lattice, false-color or component loss even
when total RAW residual decreases. Color/width compensation and uncertainty
must remain visible. No parameter sweep or gate relaxation follows failure.
This is a prospective120s/4GiB direction test, not a measured cost estimate.

**The next actual-data step is conditional on the running complete photograph.**
Inspect the whole image first, then all frozen witnesses. Select no favorable
new crop after seeing a fit. If a persistent upper-leg/upper-occlusion witness
is nominated, freeze its complete context and a second independent structure
plus a background null. Use the existing original-u16 cache in actual sensor
coordinates. Require a common geometry/optical account to predict disjoint
spatial support and held captures, while keeping independent color structure.
Do not fit separate optics to each desired outline or use a supplied hand trace
as evidence of an automatic solution. The returned whole witness must visibly
explain the problematic boundary without deleting neighboring hairs/scales.

If the nominated content is only a glint, if radiance changes explain it equally
well, if the required optical support cannot be established, or if no actual
whole-witness improvement appears, **stop before full-image implementation**.
The small capsule positive cannot override this. Current source observation
has not yet supplied that release evidence.

## Full-photograph route and honest remaining work

If both bounded stages support the mechanism, the general route is a streamed
native-CFA proposal pass over all299 sources, retaining compact candidate
geometry/focus-support records; then jointly fit visible surface radiance from
the original sensels. Native per-phase cues can propose candidates, but are
neither depth truth nor a raw-argmax output reference. Competing crossings,
finite ends, absent objects and independent colored backgrounds must survive
proposal/refinement. Regions outside demonstrated support retain the complete
reference reconstruction through an explicitly tested boundary composition.

Only a bounded number of local surface/radiance fields and current-source
scratch may reside on device. Plugin halos must cover all contributing rays,
and overlapping regions must share geometry/radiance constraints; independent
tile fits followed by a cosmetic blend would reintroduce discontinuities.
Automatic proposal/topology, a supported optical trajectory and safe region
composition are **unimplemented, substantial requirements**, not small adapters.
An exact full-size cost checkpoint is mandatory; the tiny ray test supplies no
competitive-speed estimate. Final acceptance requires every source, complete
full-resolution masters, unchanged development, native/Helicon comparison and
independent full-image review, followed by bug2/flat transfer.

## Primary literature and lookup record

[Liu et al., CVPR2017](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~ILIM/projects/IM/MultiLayer/)
show that thin-structure depth/matting needs explicit mutual occlusion and
spatially varying defocus; their method uses calibrated kernels. This motivates
the representation, not its transfer to the Sony rig. Their implementation was
not consulted. [Ikoma et al., ICCP2021](https://www.computationalimaging.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/DeepDfD_ICCP2021.pdf)
compare simple depth-layer convolution with an occlusion-aware approximation
against ray-traced images. Their phase-coded optical system and trained model
are not usable Sony calibration or a pretrained solution here. The present
proposal retains actual shared-ray visibility rather than assuming averaged
alpha composition is exact. No external implementation code was opened.
