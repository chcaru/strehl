# Independent final delivery controls

Prospective source-only protocol,2026-10-27. Root executes `delivery-controls.py --out <fresh outputs> --renderer-binding <executable.json>` under300s wall/1200CPU-seconds,8GiB host/8GiB Torch allocation,four workers. The executable JSON is a strict path/SHA256/byte binding. No RAW, photograph reconstruction, parameter sweep or quality admission is included.

Three complete panels are fixed:24×32 constants[-192,1200,3700] camera DN;31×47 independently colored signed sinusoids;23×37 neutral camera DN equal to `.12*(15360-512)/as_shot_WB`. Each has an8-pixel latent apron, global reference origin(-8,-8), output(0,0,W,H). Arrays are f32 throughout. The signed structure formula is in the worker, not generated by the integration kernel. Its distinct channel frequencies and signed values expose wrong channel and origin handling; odd rectangular geometry exercises all output parities.

The actual `ReferenceDelivery` constructor and `.integrate` path used by the frozen `delivery.integrate_rgb` wrapper are compared with frozen CPU `integrate_reference`536aa094, including complete boundaries. The control retains that engine solely to record its actual start/end metadata, including PTX/DLL/source bindings and launch counts. No producing source or arithmetic changes. Error arithmetic/reductions and normalization are unchanged from the commissioned forward controls: maximum absolute error divided by max(1,reference absolute maximum), RMS error divided by max(1,reference RMS). Limits are normalized maximum1e-5 and RMS2e-6. Constants also compare to the independent constant target. The signed structure is passed through actual integration again with origin(-7,-8), then with R/B input channels swapped; each must fail the same comparison. Complete latent, CPU/GPU delivered and foil f32 arrays are saved before gate assertion. No numerical tolerance changes after observations.

The renderer consumes the actual integrated arrays, using the actual native executable/JSON interface. WB/matrix/levels come bit-for-bit from the pinned299-source native export receipt. Tone and exposure bits come from the exact `DisplayBaseline` in the held CA-off linear-master TIFF, not rounded log values or a newly estimated tone. The held RGB16 master supplies the required ICC identity. These complete source files are hashed before/after; only their metadata is inspected here, not their photograph pixels. Development source bindings are also checked by the native process.

Five native invocations are fixed: the three integrated panels, the same neutral with deliberately unit WB, and the same neutral plus an erroneous512-DN black pedestal. All are expected successful native executions; the latter two are numerical foils, not parser refusals. The positive neutral's interior4-pixel-inset must have maximum R/G/B spread at most two16-bit codes. The two foils must exceed that unchanged criterion. They exercise WB and DN-unit errors through the actual producing developer. This does not qualify off-neutral color characterization, highlight response or photographic quality.

Every native result is independently read completely as single-page RGB16 with exact dimensions. Pixel SHA must equal the renderer's producing-buffer/readback SHA; ICC bytes must equal the held native master; description and development bits must match its receipt. Tone remeasurement, demosaic and remosaic must remain absent. Input file SHA/mtime and all f32 bits remain unchanged, and the signed fixture's negative count must be positive and exactly match the native receipt's count before development. Existing downstream gamut/sRGB handling may map or clip values; the separately retained camera-DN master may not be clamped or overwritten. Whole native PNGs use exactly(value+128)//257 and the same ICC, with no resized crops or retone.

Commands, expected/actual exits, stdout/stderr, source/runtime bindings, complete fixture masters and all observations are retained before assertions. Success status is `COMPLETE_DIRECT_CFA_DELIVERY_CONTROLS`, with `all_cases_completed` and `mechanical_pass` true and `quality_claim`/`photographic_admission` false. A failure preserves observations and exits nonzero. This commissions the final consumer only; old reconstruction noise/convergence failures and full-photograph requirements remain in force.
