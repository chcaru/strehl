# The photograph decides the next implementation

2026-10-27. Root decision after independent integration review of
[the prospective visibility memo](prospective-directions.md). The full299
observation is still running. This records allocation discipline, not a new
image-quality result or an authorization to run another numerical job.

Do not launch the proposed64x48 ribbon screen now, and do not make visibility
the exclusive next architecture. The earlier capsule result already qualified
conditional shared-ray mechanics. Textured radiance and unknown geometry
would reopen identifiability work while automatic topology, supported Sony
optics, general insect surfaces and full-image composition remain unresolved.
Prior width/color compensation and the failed marigold transfer are material
counterevidence to a direct photographic release.

Photographic success need not mean discovering new shafts or subdivisions.
Cleaner separation of existing hairs, faithful thin boundaries and improved
color can be substantial gains when the same real structures remain intact.
The V3 and Helicon comparisons support that target; they do not identify the
cause of a pale rim as occlusion rather than glint, radiance change or fusion
leakage. The31-source observation supplies no unambiguous omitted topology.

After inspecting the complete running output, choose by its dominant visible
failure. A credible detail gain without broad noise/color damage earns
unchanged bug2/flat transfer and practical execution work. Broad noise or false
color first requires reconstruction fidelity; a ribbon model does not explain
failures already present on flat subjects. Persistent localized boundaries may
justify a bounded actual-data visibility test with the existing upper-leg and
upper-occlusion witnesses, their neighboring structures and held captures.
No hand trace or improved residual qualifies an automatic photographic repair.

Keep simpler general fusion changes eligible when they have a concrete
original-CFA consumer and an immediate complete-image route. Negative results
for particular learned cross-scale and dual-tree implementations do not reject
all spatial or cross-scale consistency. No further toy extension is released
without an automatic actual-photograph route and evidence that it addresses
the defect observed in the full output.

For future expensive solvers, plan complete-frame intermediate deliveries
before launch where feasible. They must retain iteration/status metadata and
all original input support; crops and synthetic residuals cannot replace them.
The current frozen run remains unchanged and its declared64-step evaluation
continues. Do not restart a healthy solve solely to retrofit that convenience.
