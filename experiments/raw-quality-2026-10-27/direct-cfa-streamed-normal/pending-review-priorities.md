# Photographic priorities before the direct-CFA result

2026-10-27. **PROSPECTIVE / candidate unseen.** Refreshed the complete native CA-off, Helicon B4 and StackMFF V3 photographs first, then existing native-size hair, scale, rim, background and upper-occlusion witnesses. This was read-only viewing: no reconstruction, metric, new crop, resampling or tone change. The [fixed presentation protocol](full-photograph-view-protocol.md) and all ten original rectangles remain unchanged.

**ARGUED acceptance standard:** newly revealed topology is useful evidence, but is not a necessary condition for substantial improvement. Existing hair shafts, scales and surface transitions can look substantially more faithful through better continuity, separation, edge shape and tonal gradation. Conversely, stronger contrast, brighter glints or a smoother background alone do not establish that improvement. Image appearance and demonstrated source-detail recovery are different claims; the retained source observations do not supply a sharper ground-truth photograph or prove recoverable missing structures.

## Three priorities

1. **Coherent occlusion contours with structures at different depths retained.** The long pale lower wing rim, diffuse surround beneath the upper-leg hairs and green/cyan junction below the raised blue surface remain conspicuous in the references. A useful change would reduce distracting spread or duplicated-looking contours while preserving the underlying tonal transition, crossing hairs, dark gaps and adjoining material. Simply blackening the boundary, suppressing fine hairs or desaturating the junction fails this test. Some bright contour signal may belong to the photographed surface; this review does not assume every pale pixel is an artifact. Primary witnesses: bright-wing-rim, upper-occlusion, blue-joint and upper-leg-hairs, checked against the whole composition.

2. **Clearer material and thin-feature rendering without hardening artifacts.** Hair shafts remain soft; rounded scale boundaries and thorax texture are often difficult to separate from glitter and coarse grain. B4 makes several contours firmer, while V3 reduces some haze but softens some scale boundaries. A meaningful candidate gain could retain exactly the same structures while improving their smooth shape, continuity, local shading and separation across the photograph. Narrow gaps, diagonal strands and dark surface texture must survive. Stair-stepped outlines, dark ringing, exaggerated grain or erased fine structure are regressions, even if the result initially looks sharper. Use turquoise-scales, thorax-texture, elytron-seam, dark-elytron and eye-and-antenna as well as the hair witnesses.

3. **Preserve iridescent color and highlight character while reducing distracting contamination.** Blue, cyan, green and warmer scale patches must remain locally distinct; real glints need not disappear. Less spill or haze can help if compact highlights and surrounding texture survive without false hues, posterization or new colored traces. V3's background witness has faint coherent green curves despite reduced random speckle. Background-black is therefore the first crop guard, not a certified flat-noise measurement. It cannot be traded away for a locally cleaner edge.

## Decision procedure and limits

Open the complete candidate first, then complete references, then every fixed witness at native size under the unchanged display. Report beneficial and harmful changes together. A substantial full-photograph improvement in edge and tonal fidelity may count without new topology; an isolated favorable crop does not. Numerical convergence, prior generated failures and operational cost remain separate findings and cannot substitute for the photographic verdict.
