# Prior photographic constraints on final CFA color completion

2026-10-27. **SOURCE/REPORT REVIEW only; no new experiment authorized or run.** The native spectral299 photograph is still pending. Its generated missing-color errors justify a specific completion-stage question, not a prediction that the weevil will improve. This note reviews retained reports rather than reprocessing or re-viewing their historical image files.

## What the earlier negatives actually establish

- [M95-CDIFF, sections3–4 and10](../../../docs/research/M95-cdiff.md): CDIFF's chromatic signal gain was small (luma+0.0124dex, below its0.03bar) and the four100% panels were reported visually indistinguishable. CDIFF2 gained more signal but increased artifact share by0.0590 and worsened all-channel pixel error. Neither shipped. This was a selected crop and a downsampled AHD pseudo-truth, not a complete-current-weevil quality verdict. A fitted per-bin gain hid one normalization defect; exact known-sample preservation could itself hide an erroneous green refinement. Neither sharpness nor known-site identity alone qualified completion.
- [BUILD-SHARP section31 and32.7](../../../docs/research/BUILD-SHARP.md#31-the-directional-demosaic-was-built-measured-on-the-owners-own-captures-and-rejected-s27s28s-headline-is-refuted): Hamilton–Adams green plus color-difference reconstruction was rendered on actual bee/tweezers stacks. Detail-site gradients slightly fell, while defocused-region HF power rose to1.5016/1.1698 of MHC. The reported bee crop acquired a connected maze pattern; the fine-hair crop showed no benefit. The failure was structured false texture, not increased classical overshoot. Off-axis synthetic performance also deteriorated. This rejects that recipe and its grating-to-photograph inference; it does not ban every adaptive completion method.
- [M95-demosaic](../../../docs/research/M95-demosaic.md) and [M104b](../../../docs/research/M104b-demosaic-lever.md): loudness can reward nearest-neighbor aliases, and the historical grey-ruler comparison found no qualifying alternative. Their decoder-derived truth, periodic subject and historical optical-cutoff assumptions must remain scoped. Neither the old cutoff nor an assertion that linear interpolation cannot invent structured error is a valid currentweevil guarantee. BUILD-SHARP's printed-ruler optical attribution also has explicit corrections; it supplies no present teleconverter/code86 ceiling.
- [BUILD-DEMOSAIC](../../../docs/research/BUILD-DEMOSAIC.md) and [V-GATHER](../../../docs/research/V-GATHER.md): a lower R–B high-pass statistic can accompany greater error because reconstructed channels become correlated. Quiet chroma is not automatically faithful chroma; source-noise admission and final completion are distinct causes.
- Complete [vitamin](../../phase-packed-rgb/results.md#complete-photographic-result-no-promotion), [chip](../../phase-packed-chip/full-results-review.md) and [current weevil](../weevil-fusion-order/photographic-review.md) twelve-plane trials were photographic nulls. Fine color/texture changed without useful cleanup of fans, rims, hairs or scale surfaces. The flat-chroma1.078951 penalty remains. Those trials retained per-source MHC/Catmull estimates and removed final MHC; they were not tests of a genuinely original-CFA completion alternative. They neither prove final MHC harmless nor provide a successful shortcut around it.

## What is new, and what it does not repair

The [saved spectral stage audit](../native-spectral-fusion/pilot/analysis/stage-audit-results.md) separates physical CFA sites from inferred sites. It reports known-site differences at most0.00033004DN, no negative CFA values, and substantial independent-color/green-diamond attenuation or cross-color terms introduced at inferred sites. This is stronger stage localization than a power ranking. It still uses declared generated components; their regression rank is not general image identifiability or proof of erased insect structure.

The same audit finds flat-noise penalties, shared-bin loss and red-cancellation error already in CFA. A completion improvement must not absorb those failures into a new “MHC caused everything” explanation. More accurate inferred chroma, unchanged known sites and faithful neutral detail are separate obligations. The actual full photograph must decide whether a proposed method addresses a visible problem worth pursuing.
