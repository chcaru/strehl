## Comparison and decision

The [native CA-off complete output](../../../outputs/raw-quality-2026-10-26/weevil-107-ca-off/stack-full.jpg), [Helicon B4](../../../outputs/raw-quality-2026-10-27/weevil-107-fine-comparator/b-r4-desktop-2/stack-full.jpg) and [B8](../../../outputs/raw-quality-2026-10-26/weevil-107-comparator/b/stack-full.jpg) retain recognizable hair shafts, surfaces and iridescent color, despite their unresolved softness, glare and pale contours. They are substantially better photographs than any checkpoint. Their masters and exact mapped witnesses are linked in each checkpoint index. Helicon uses its own alignment/gauge and predeveloped, toned RGB inputs; its 7020×4676 output and roughly 1.02 correspondence scale are not a pixel-identical RAW estimator comparison. That caveat does not explain the present near-total loss of visible subject.

The earlier inverse replaced the insect with large colored blocks. Replacing those blocks with a near-black field is not success. Nor is rejection conditional on discovering new topology: faithful improvement of existing edge or tonal structure would count, but it is not present here. The next visible requirement is recovery of usable subject radiance, color and continuous material—not further local sharpening or a relaxed display.
