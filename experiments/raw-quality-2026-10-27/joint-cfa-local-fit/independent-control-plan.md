## Question and early discriminator

**The full-value local quadratic proposal is closed and will not be built.** With repeated geometry, common spatial/noise weights and one nonnegative scalar admission per source, its common normal matrix makes the joint polynomial a convex average of per-source fits. Sources with component recoveries(1,epsilon) and(epsilon,1) cannot yield two recoveries near1. Signed spatial weights do not remove this restricted source-mixture obstruction. This is not rejection of all original-CFA fitting or changed geometries.

The replacement freezes a linear original-sensel operator A with normalized Lanczos3 evaluation and source-square quadrature; H is a complete orthonormal Haar transform of each original phase residual. It fits H(A X-y), with source/band weights fixed from original CFA energy and the injected variance. It does not fit blur or visibility. X contains continuous-basis RGB coefficients with an8px apron. The delivered image is three reference-unit-cell RGB integrals, developed directly: it is neither four reconstructed CFA planes nor an additional MHC output. Do not inherit the old polynomial-exact or arbitrary-input identity assertions.

**After operator checks and flats, test same-channel fine-near/coarse-far coexistence before attractive color positives.** The new band distinction can address the old scalar obstruction, but independent source-grid Haar bands are shift/scale dependent, overlap physical frequencies, and can react to aliasing. The surrogate's1e-4 admission floor, squared weights and zero-start64-step CGLS are algorithm choices. They do not establish identifiability, noise calibration, conditioning or observed radiance in the nullspace. A practical clipping policy and contract-correct full-photo fallback remain unresolved; the small prototype explicitly refuses clipped inputs.

Setting a=1 alone does not make this a Parseval null under unequal V. N counts original sensels once; regrouping rows must not multiply evidence or add a hidden prior.
