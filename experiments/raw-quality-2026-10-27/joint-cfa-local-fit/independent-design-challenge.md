# Independent challenge: original-CFA local reconstruction

2026-10-27. **ARGUED / DERIVED, source review only.** No RAW read, implementation, fit, build or numerical experiment was performed. This reviews the proposed estimator, rather than treating compliance with the original-CFA contract as evidence of better photographs. Root owns any subsequent release.

## The full-value local polynomial is not selected

The radius-three quadratic has a real difference from the October16 positive kernel: signed spatial equivalent weights can preserve curvature that a positive local average suppresses. Its joint normal equations also avoid interpolating each source first. Those differences do not fix its proposed scalar source admission.

**DERIVED restricted obstruction:** with repeated geometry and common spatial/noise weights, the normal matrix is common to all sources. One nonnegative scalar admission per source therefore produces a convex average of their fitted polynomials. If two sources have fine/coarse recoveries `(1, epsilon)` and `(epsilon, 1)`, the recovered pair sums to `1 + epsilon`; both cannot approach one. Signed spatial weights do not remove this temporal mixture restriction. Do not implement this known-failing full-value recipe. This is not a rejection of every local regression or original-CFA method.

The actual [October16 full-photo result](C:/dev/blog/death-of-software/projects/strehl/experiments/native-cfa-kernel/results.md) matters: both isotropic and per-colour anisotropic positive kernels softened rails, contacts and weak scratches across complete299-source photographs, despite numerical parity and finite support. Their shared focus front is a confound, not proof that native samples lack detail. The [September14 Lanczos signal screen](../../quality-september14/joint-cfa-lanczos-verdict.md) passed before the [actual photographic refusal](../../quality-september14/joint-photographic-verdict.md): softer rails and cyan stipple remained. Neither a better forward basis nor lower held residual is sufficient. The [known-variance reconstruction](../raw-blur-compatibility/known-variance/independent-results.md) likewise recovered genuine component amplitudes while retaining excessive off-template RGB/chroma error.

## Changed question: fixed band-residual original-CFA fitting

The proposed `sum(f,b) ||sqrt(W[f,b]) H[f,b] (A[f] X - y[f])||^2` can use different sources at different scales, so it escapes the scalar obstruction. It is distinct from [consistent reconstruction](../../consistent-reconstruction/design.md), which fits already fused coefficients after the old gather. It does not yet specify an implementable or physically calibrated reconstruction.

Freeze these meanings before code:

- `y` is original black-subtracted sensor data with literal phase, active origin and injected per-phase noise. `A` maps one declared continuous radiance basis to the integral over each transformed original sensel. Stored placement maps run reference-to-source; projecting a source footprint into the reference requires the corresponding inverse, with native/quad units explicit. Uniform square sensitivity is an approximation, not a Sony pixel-response calibration. For a quadratic, transformed source-square covariance `A_linear A_linear^T / 12` belongs in the second moments; evaluating only its centre or the reference-square integral is a different operator.
- `H` must operate on same-spectral observations with declared physical spacing. Filtering a Bayer scalar image as if adjacent R/G/B were one signal is invalid. G1/G2 can constrain the same spectral field, but retain different coordinates and supplied noise parameters. Freeze whether `X` is a CFA-site output followed by final MHC or three full colour fields; these have different nullspaces, memory and final interpolation.
- `W` is a frozen, original-CFA, noise-gated focus preference. Calling filtered residuals independent noise observations would double-count correlated information. Even diagonal original noise becomes `H Sigma H^T`; overlapping bands are correlated. The objective may deliberately be a heuristic focus-weighted norm, but its residual is then neither a calibrated likelihood nor an independent-band sample count. Data-derived weights also invalidate a simple unconditional propagated-variance claim.
- A geometry-only `A X` does not model defocus or same-ray occlusion. Positive weights on incompatible blurred observations bias the solution. Per-band preference can help practical fusion, but a small residual, a convex objective and a converged solve cannot establish absence of ghosting, halo or false colour. Regularization must be explicit, applied once with stated observation-count normalization, and must not silently supply missing colour information.

## Small decisive controls, not another parameter campaign

Use the already scoped independent continuous panels and flat-first guards in [independent-control-plan.md](independent-control-plan.md). Add or preserve these load-bearing distinctions:

1. **Producing-operator null:** under uniform band weights, record the actual frame operator `sum H^T H`. If the bands are Parseval, the objective and gradient must reduce to the direct original-sensel residual; otherwise compare against that explicitly retained frame operator. Do not assume an overlapping pyramid is Parseval. Check the actual `A/A^T` dot product, including source footprint, boundaries and all colour phases. A shifted phase, wrong map direction and substituted reference footprint must be live foils, with a flat placebo.
2. **Focus-alias counterexample:** on R sites `x = 2n`, the source-cell means of `cos(2*pi*.20*x)/sinc(.20) - cos(2*pi*.30*x)/sinc(.30)` cancel, using `sinc(t) = sin(pi*t)/(pi*t)`. Add a positive DC pedestal. Symmetric blur attenuates the two physical frequencies differently and creates nonzero sampled curvature: an original-CFA second-difference cue can prefer the blurrier frame. Keep one fixed diverse-phase companion that exposes the genuine sharp structure. Test whether admission discards it, without searching phases or thresholds. Rank of a local polynomial matrix does not certify observability of these frequencies.
3. **Different-depth positive:** two bands sharp in different sources must both survive with autonomous weights. Oracle cofocused or oracle band admission can isolate the operator, but is conditional evidence only. Preserve independently coloured detail, flat-green false-colour and the real8DN green component separately. Whole-image error must constrain components outside the named templates. The old [band-weight oracle](../../band-weight-oracle/results.md) improved near/far detail but still failed curved-boundary and glint chroma; do not treat scale separation alone as sufficient.
4. **Repeated-data null:** literal duplicates cannot create extra information or reduced uncertainty; independent repeat exposures can reduce noise but cannot resolve an identical-phase alias pair. Keep missing-colour/unsupported-region refusal explicit. A same-source fit is not automatically exact for a finite basis or smoothing objective; report its bias rather than installing a copy shortcut that bypasses the operator.

For attribution, compare joint fitting with per-source fitting through the same frozen operator/admission, and retain the actual current MHC/Catmull consumer as the practical baseline. Neither an argmax reference nor lower fitted residual is truth. The compact opaque crossing and clipped/unsaturated glint companions remain necessary; transparent frequency superposition is not an occlusion model.

## Practical release boundary

**ARGUED:** the multiband idea warrants a precise source design, not immediate full-photo implementation. A fixed linear operator with fixed weights can stream one original u16 frame into each objective/adjoint pass without an aligned float volume. But every solver pass revisits the stack:299 full originals are about20GB before any solver state. Re-decoding all originals each iteration is not a practical plan. Pin an original-u16 cache or bounded source/tile traversal, the exact boundary coupling and a finite work limit; do not infer speed from a tiny panel. Full-size cost must include weight formation, all repeated source visits, adjoints, final reconstruction and output. Direct full-RGB fields also have the earlier false-colour nullspace risk; a new prior is not self-validating evidence that it is resolved.

After the smallest fixed operator/focus screen, choose a bounded complete flat/weevil photograph or close the recipe. Preserve generated failures if root separately authorizes a diagnostic photograph. Do not continue a radius, basis, regularizer, iteration or admission sweep in lieu of photographic progress. No photographic admission, native implementation or quality improvement is established by this review.
