This job excludes the earlier RAW development and geometry solve; it is not a matched end-to-end speed ranking against Helicon.

Helicon B/C use their own alignment and the same predeveloped TIFFs; their roughly 1.02 scale difference remains and SIFT correspondence is only a locator. V3 uses held native geometry but a distinct OpenCV cubic RGB gather, pre-merge tone/sRGB encoding and shared RGB probability fusion. Its receipt explicitly says `native_RAW_reconstruction=false` and `whole_workflow_comparison=false`. Thus this is a complete learned-fusion comparator with retained Strehl placement, not the native original-CFA reconstruction requested by the user, nor an isolated test of focus decisions.

**ARGUED decision:** retain the demonstrated local haze reduction as a useful observation, but do not promote this fixed V3 recipe or call it SOTA. It does not resolve the principal softness, long occlusion halos or glare, and the scale/background regressions matter. The [31-source review](../weevil-through-focus-support/observation.md) supplies no missing-detail truth that would convert stronger apparent contrast into genuine recovery. No claim extends to marigold, minerals or the flat diagnostic corpus. No parameter sweep or further run is authorized by this report.
