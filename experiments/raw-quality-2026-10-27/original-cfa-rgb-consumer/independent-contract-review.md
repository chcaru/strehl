## What actually changes

Let `y_f` be the original u16 mosaic, `D_f` the fixed black/WB conversion, `M_f` the phase-dependent linear MHC operator and `W_f` the held geometry/Catmull sampling. Evaluating a sparse row of `K_f = W_f M_f D_f` directly from original sensels avoids materializing source RGB. Its values are nevertheless exactly the per-source MHC/RGB-warp values, apart from declared f32 summation ordering. It still performs color interpolation and geometric interpolation; one kernel invocation is not one physical interpolation. It does not jointly use different frames to resolve missing colors, sampling aliases, clipping, occlusion or defocus.

Compared with the current `align_cmd.rs` full-resolution path (MHC at line2806; u16 writeback at line2478 onward) and `merge_cmd.rs::extract_planes`, a direct RGB output consumer can genuinely remove remosaicing, intermediate round/container clamp, black clipping and the final second MHC. Those operations are nonlinear/information-discarding and cannot be erased from the old operator merely by multiplying linear matrices. Retaining signed below-black/overshooting f32 values changes that policy and must be disclosed. Keeping only original u16 storage, bounded f32 consumer scratch and no aligned-frame volume satisfies the storage/gather invariants. It does **not** justify calling reconstructed RGB band coefficients original CFA observations. Deliver RGB directly through color/development once, without relabeling it CFA and invoking MHC again.

This distinction is already implemented for a single source: [BUILD-DEMOSAIC](../../../docs/research/BUILD-DEMOSAIC.md) and `crates/strehl-cli/examples/build-demosaic-truth.rs::once_rgb` at line563. Its measured table is mixed: once/fullres chroma error is 331/281 DN on noisy flat, 464/448 on neutral detail, 521/595 on colored detail, and 847/1259 on vivid detail. The report's fullres-minus-oracle error is not the measured benefit of removing the second demosaic. Neither these controls nor the prior rejected direct-plane precision trial establishes a visible weevil gain. M95 also rules out interpreting increased band energy or reduced R-B variation as recovered detail.

## Load-bearing consumer requirements

1. **Keep frequency-dependent source admission.** A single learned probability or focus scalar applied to whole RGB values repeats the known fine/coarse obstruction: for transfers `(1,e)` and `(e,1)`, one convex source weight cannot retain both components. Use declared RGB bands with separate source weights per band and a common weight for R/G/B within a band. A full-native RGB pyramid changes the current four half-resolution CFA band grid; do not imply canonical weight parity or a basis-only comparison. This permits different-depth contributions; it does not solve visibility or prevent boundary modulation.
2. **Propagate the one original-sensel noise model through the actual composed rows and bands.** For fixed rows `l` and `m`, covariance is `sum_j l_j*m_j*v_j`, including shared original sensels and cross-color MHC terms. Coalesce repeated original addresses before squaring coefficients. Independently injecting RGB or band noise would destroy this covariance. Reusing the original CFA noise draws through the whole new consumer is defensible; copying old CFA-grid beta/floors is not. Frozen-weight propagation is conditional, not the unconditional variance of data-dependent source selection. Learned focus probabilities alone are not a calibrated noise gate.
3. **The one-source reference is the existing once-RGB path.** Identity placement should yield ordinary one-time MHC; fractional placement should match MHC followed by the declared RGB warp. It need not match the current double-MHC master or missing-channel optical truth. Known-channel identity, active-origin/CFA phase, shared-sensel noise and f32 row-composition checks can reuse the existing commissions rather than create another estimator survey.

The smallest useful next release would therefore be the complete band-preserving once-RGB consumer, priced as one streaming source plus bounded tiles/probes, with explicit direct-RGB writer parity. Reuse flat/noise, real-color and same-channel near-fine/far-coarse guards, retaining their failures, then show complete weevil/diagnostic outputs with held geometry and common development. Do not spend a lane merely removing rounding: prior evidence gives no substantial photographic expectation for that alone. Any successful image would establish this particular consumer's utility while leaving strict joint original-CFA reconstruction and physical occlusion handling unresolved.
