# Independent complete-photo review: no useful detail gain

2026-10-27. **VERIFIED visual observation of saved outputs.** This reviewer did
not author the producing adapter or exporter. Both complete photographs were
viewed first, then all ten paired native384 crops under the same recorded
RGB16-to-display conversion and ICC. The tool reduced complete images to an
orientation overview; the384 crops were displayed at native pixel dimensions.
The earlier oversized multi-image tool response was truncated and was not used
as review evidence; all ten pairs were subsequently inspected in small groups.

**Decision: reject this fixed fusion-order change as the sought weevil quality
improvement.** The complete subject, coloring and broad rendering remain close
to the baseline. Small differences in texture and highlight/color rendering
are visible, but I cannot identify meaningful new hair separation, narrower
scale boundaries, improved seam clarity or recovered weak structure. This is
not a claim that the two RGB outputs are numerically identical.
