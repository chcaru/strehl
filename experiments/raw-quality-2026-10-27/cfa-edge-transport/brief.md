## Scope and hypothesis

Apply one bounded, common-coordinate nonlinear edge-steepening transport to the retained exact merged CFA before the ordinary single final demosaic/development. Preserve all four CFA phases and use f32 arithmetic; no RGB editing, original replacement, aligned-frame volume or learned/invented texture. It is an explicit appearance operation on the complete native reconstruction. It cannot fix missing depth structure, identify a lens PSF or establish a resolution gain.

The prospective family is a noise-gated common edge flow, inspired by shock filtering's nonoscillatory goal. A smoothed green guide supplies a shared displacement sampled at the actual physical position of each CFA phase; a nonnegative same-phase interpolation produces each value. Limit one pass to at most0.6 native pixel displacement and leave insufficient evidence unchanged. The injected camera model may be used as an explicitly conservative reference scale, never described as calibrated merged covariance or privately re-estimated. Do not use per-color edge directions. Producer must reject an implementation that necessarily blurs fine structures more than it steepens useful edges; root/reviewer approve one exact formula before execution. No strength/kernel sweep.
