## What is actually different

The authors' primary [2007 EUSIPCO paper](https://www.eurasip.org/Proceedings/Eusipco/Eusipco2007/Papers/c5p-j01.pdf), sections2/4, explicitly restates the BIPC method from their 2007 Signal Processing Letters paper. For a noise-free convolution with a centrally symmetric PSF, its Fourier transfer is real. Squaring normalized phase removes either sign of that transfer. The resulting translation phase ramp is doubled. This handles sign-changing transfer bands that ordinary phase correlation does not cancel. The paper's similarity extension is a separate phase-bispectrum construction; ordinary magnitude Fourier-Mellin does not inherit the same invariance. Its implementation uses windowing and padding, and the experiment is not a calibration of this Sony/Viltrox macro rig.

**DERIVED limits.** On an N-point discrete axis, a doubled ramp identifies translation modulo N/2. The inverse-FFT peak must be divided by two and unwrapped within a declared seed-relative interval. Periodic texture can create additional ambiguous peaks inside that interval. Squaring also doubles phase perturbations; frequencies near an OTF zero or the injected noise floor do not become trustworthy because their normalized magnitude is one. A numerical epsilon only prevents division by zero. Flat/noise-only inputs must refuse, and a sign-changing positive input must succeed so refusal-all cannot pass.

## Physical and implementation objections

A hair over a scale, moving focus ownership, clipping, polarized specular glints and an occlusion boundary generally do not share one spatially invariant convolution. Two individually symmetric depth PSFs do not make their changing visible mixture a common-PSF image pair. An asymmetric PSF can carry real phase that BIPC will interpret as translation. Squared phase does not distinguish that bias from camera motion.

Windowing also matters: multiplying a blurred image by a fixed window does not commute with blur, and shifted windows truncate different content. Zero padding does not recover missing support. Breathing creates scale-dependent displacement across a tile; translation-only peaks cannot themselves solve the full similarity. Preserve the current coarse initializer, explicit tile coordinates and global direct-pair solve. Record any seed warp/window/resampling introduced by the new measurement: it can itself change the result, as M78 demonstrated.

Use the original two green sublattices separately, with their actual sensor offsets and two-native-pixel pitch. No MHC is needed for this cue. Agreement between G1/G2 is useful but correlated; reverse-pair and cycle agreement can also share bias. None is an independent truth label. The peak confidence is not NCC, so its numerical threshold cannot inherit NCC's0.5 cutoff. Freeze one refusal/ambiguity rule before actual outcomes and retain every rejected tile/pair and the resulting graph population. Do not rescue it by discarding short spans or fitting a straight breathing track: [rule6's M103 amendment](../../../docs/agents/engineering-ledger.md#rule-6) requires per-frame placement in the consumer's gauge and plants carrying measured curvature.
