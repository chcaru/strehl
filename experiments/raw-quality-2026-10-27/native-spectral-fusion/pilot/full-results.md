# Complete weevil spectral pilot: photographic regression

2026-10-27. **VERIFIED photographic rejection of this fixed recipe.** The
complete299 candidate is more resolved than its deliberately uniform-aggregation
control, but it is visibly worse than the held native output. It adds a blue/green
veil and broad contour haze, weakens hair and scale separation, and introduces
fine patterned/checker-like texture. No product default, transfer campaign,
SOTA admission or photographic improvement is justified by this result.

The [independent photographic review](independent-full-photographic-review.md)
reached the same rejection after all five whole photographs and all fifty fixed
witnesses, without reading root's verdict. The separate
[independent integrity audit](independent-full299-review.md) verified the
complete run and artifacts while explicitly withholding quality admission.

The user requested a good stopping point and status report while this job was
running. Root finished it and the complete presentation/review. The separately
prepared final-CFA color pilot remains unbuilt and unexecuted; it is not a fix
demonstrated by this report. The broader quality goal remains unresolved.

## Decision and limits

Close this unchanged spectral-fusion recipe as a photographic regression.
Do not spend another full299 run on an unchanged recipe, promote it, or carry
it to bug2/flat60 as an apparent improvement. Retain its full outputs, controls
and source bindings. This result does not disprove every spectral, multi-frame
or native RAW reconstruction architecture and does not establish an optical
or capture-quality ceiling.

The prior controls5 mechanical pass and six structured quality failures remain
part of the evidence. The stage audit localized some generated losses to final
color completion, but it did not establish the cause of this real photograph's
veil, patterning or halos. The separate independent-color completion proposal
is therefore still preliminary research, with known neutral-detail/false-color
tradeoffs and no photographic result. At the user's stopping boundary, no new
experiment is authorized by this report; continuation requires resumption.
