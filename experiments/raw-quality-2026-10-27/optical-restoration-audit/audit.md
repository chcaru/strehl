# Optical restoration after the complete green-phase rejection

Status: ARGUED from a bounded source/report audit, 2026-10-27. No RAW decoding, pixel fitting, numerical execution, build or image-quality claim. The complete green-phase and matched-mean arms remain rejected; this does not reopen their performance or transfer work.

## Decision

There is no presently identified, capture-specific optical kernel with which to justify a physical optical inverse on the complete weevil. The missing quantity is the channel/field/focus-dependent transfer from an independently constrained object to original sensor measurements, in native sensor pixels. Marked aperture, a 2x teleconverter, coarse focus code86 and a soft-looking subject do not identify that transfer. This is a parameter-identification gap, **not** a claim that restoration cannot help or that the current optics have no recoverable passband.

Do not schedule an assumed Airy/Gaussian-width sweep or reuse the refused bristle as a PSF. The immediate useful decision is in the already commissioned complete flat/native-source/Helicon comparison: determine whether a fixed, visibly identified bar separation or other genuine structure is supported by originals and the external workflow but lost by native reconstruction. Such a finding would directly favor a reconstruction intervention over attributing the defect to optics. It would not calibrate a PSF. Do not elect a per-band argmax reference or treat a more contrasted glint as that positive.

For an optical trial specifically, the minimum missing observation is an independently specified object feature (including its finite width/edge quality) at the current rig's relevant focus/magnification, with a second feature/field position and held focus observation. First check whether the existing calibration slide has a recoverable physical specification sufficiently strong to provide that constraint; the directory name and visually straight bars alone do not. This audit does not request another capture or launch another metric campaign. If that specification/support does not exist, retain the gap rather than fit a lens kernel to unknown subject softness. The available flat state57 is not automatically a calibration for weevil state86.

## Current quantities and the narrow route if the gap is closed

The retained sensor observer is signed camera-domain CFA before WB/MHC, in the reference149 crop `[68,46,6896,4596]`. Original/gathered143 and149 can expose a transfer through the consumer, but the fixed bristle family refused held transfer on both sources and both green phases. Its final section was more concentrated/higher than source143. That is neither a lens PSF nor proof of improved resolution. The full original/master bindings and refusal are retained in [consumer-spread results](../../raw-quality-2026-10-26/insect-consumer-spread/results.md). The two green phases must retain their physical offsets; they are not independent repeat exposures.

If an independent optical constraint becomes available, the distinct trial is a **single bounded joint reconstruction from original u16 CFA observations**, with f32 forward arithmetic, calibrated per-source noise, and a final camera-linear latent field developed once. It must include the actual sampling footprint and runtime geometry; it may stream source tiles rather than retain an aligned volume. Known per-channel sensor responses may differ, but independent color source elections and per-plane post-sharpening are not a substitute for that common observation model. One cannot apply a global convolution across an occlusion and call its residual lens blur; two-depth overlap must either have a visibility model or remain unchanged/refused. This is a conditional route, not a source-ready algorithm or a claim that a cheap full299 inverse currently exists.

Before that one complete-photo trial, a small fixed screen must include: a delta-kernel identity/null; a low-signal input using the same injected model first; a deliberately wrong kernel/geometry that the held original-CFA prediction rejects; and a known latent with colored structures, compact glints and an opaque two-depth crossing. A supplied blur plant tests implementation and whether a metric can fail, not camera calibration. The **known-real positive is currently missing** for a restoration claim: it must be independently supported feature separation, not an edge slope, spectrum boost or fitted target reconstruction. Flat-bars/points cannot silently fill that role without object support. Whole weevil/master and a representative transfer photograph then decide whether the result is useful; increased contrast with unchanged resolution may still be a perceptual result, but must be described that way and must preserve color, background and depth structure.

## Bounded source-retention follow-on after the flat comparison

ARGUED, five-minute source audit requested separately by root. Root's new independent photographic evidence is that flat source30 already shows clear QR corners, dots and upper wedges while the native stack is softer; source30 is also the global reference. This supports investigating source retention before an optical inverse. It does not show that every input sample survives actual gathering or that one source covers the complete slide.

The smallest distinct consumer trial is **direct full-pixel fusion using the unchanged current finest-band weights**. For source `i`, obtain current native gathered `known4[i]` and canonical `w[i,0]` from the existing BandWorker/field seam. Alongside the exact canonical Pyramid baseline, accumulate four f32 numerators `sum(w[i,0]*known4[i,p])` and one shared denominator `D=sum(w[i,0])`. Divide where `D>0`; use the ordinary all-source mean where `D=0`. Keep original u16 storage, the same original-order gathers, injected noise/probe, crop, CA setting, WB and normal writer. No new argmax, focus guide, threshold, phase direction, spatial warp or smoothing is introduced. Each source is consumed once; no aligned-frame volume or second source pass is necessary. First-source copy and one-source collapse need an explicit identity path so multiplication/division rounding is not mistaken for a photographic change. This is a small test-only accumulator/adapter, not an already implemented mode.

This is distinct from [cross-scale-source-prior](../../cross-scale-source-prior/design.md): that rule multiplied existing coarse-band weights by reduced normalized finest-source support, retained the finest coefficient and terminal mean, and performed two replayed source passes. Its [complete chip result](../../cross-scale-source-prior/chip-results.md) had a limited real border/contact gain, persistent color stipple and no new fine resolution; its generated far/coarse and glint failures remain. The proposed direct accumulator instead tests whether preserving each admitted source's whole CFA tuple avoids the current frequency-dependent source mixtures and terminal mean. The current source weights themselves remain suspect; this test does not validate them.

It is also distinct from the rejected [native coherent estimator](../../native-coherent-estimator/design.md): that used legacy27x27 Tenengrad,17x17 regularization, `Gate::new(4)` and its uncalibrated-for-current-gather noise constant, with a two-pass elected-source admission. Its [results](../../native-coherent-estimator/results-september25.md) lost genuine far/coarse content and failed before any photograph. Calling the stock `--merge estimator` is not a shortcut: `preflight_cover_backend_support` refuses `cover=crop`, and `Ctx::field_for` calls the old `warp_frame_in_place`; that would change geometry and guide as well as synthesis.

Scope the first observation to all60 flat sources. Commission the actual source30 gather and ordinary writer as a one-source whole-image identity/reference before interpreting the full pair; do not use a per-band winner as truth. The smallest cheap adverse remains near-fine/far-coarse content plus colored thin lines and a low-signal flat: finest-weight full-pixel fusion can suppress a farther coarse structure just as a fine-conditioned prior can. A failed adverse stays failed; root may separately authorize a complete flat mechanism observation, but it cannot license a general mode or automatic weevil transfer. Compare complete candidate/baseline masters with the already frozen source30 and Helicon workflow evidence. A faithful source shape retained with less broadening is the intended photographic question; noise amplification, wrong-depth ownership, color change or erased structure rejects usefulness.
