Today: four screens, annotated

Offscreen renders of the installed desktop (1680 × 1050), taken on 20 November 2026 with the owner's library. What is here is capable and carefully made. The trouble is how it is arranged: by the order features were added, and around the engine's vocabulary.

123456

After a stack

1The photograph gets 8.1 % of the window. The instruments keep their full layout after completion; there is no finished state.
2Nine panels show the same thing, detail kept by plane and size, in type from 8 px. "Direct fits", "admitted", "fallback", "accumulated", "at pin" are the engine's words.
3The rail mixes four kinds of thing: places (Projects, Capture), steps (Adjust, Edit, Export), tools (Agent, Review, Activity) and actions (Stack starts a render at once; Layout resets the layout).
4The library is a tree of camera folders: "107MSDCF", "Result 02", 22-pixel thumbnails, creation order, no dates or sizes, nothing can be removed.
5No answer to "is it good?" Refused planes are a count, coverage sits in a side pane, and the Review pane states it gives no grade. No time estimate either.
6The timeline is the best instrument here, and it is at the bottom: a bar per plane, a line for the region, and a hover that shows a plane's region on the photograph. Version 2 keeps it and puts it on every station. With 1,488 planes it shows four thumbnails.

Capture

1One scrolling column of more than fifty controls mixes set-once items (connection, storage folder) with per-shot ones. Power is at the top, error correction near the bottom, finished captures at the very bottom.
2Start is disabled by about ten conditions and shows none of them. An unapplied edit is enough.
3Focus is three numbers: position, start, end. There is no picture of the range, the planes or the step.
4The live view has peaking and zoom but no histogram, clipping or exposure control, though the camera host accepts ISO and shutter. Progress during a stack is a line of text and a 4-pixel bar.
5Long tasks wait silently. Calibrate step sat on "Setting the camera…" for minutes with the camera switched off; measuring and compacting cannot be cancelled.

Edit

1One set of sliders, two scopes. Drawing a region silently points Light and Colour at it; the only cue is this header.
2Ten sliders and a look. No crop, white balance picker, curve, sharpening, noise reduction, healing or histogram, in the model or on screen.
3The one repair tool is hidden. "Source repair" is a collapsed section under a selected region; it needs a full re-stack and disables Export until then.
4This is the app at its best: the photograph large, the timeline below. Version 2 makes this the rule.
