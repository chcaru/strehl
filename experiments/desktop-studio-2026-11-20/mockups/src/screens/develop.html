1Macro finishing tools. Crop, a white balance picker, healing, backdrop clean-up and a scale bar are absent from both the editing model and the screen today. They are the difference between a stack and a finished plate.
2Depth brush. Today's "Source repair" is a collapsed section that needs a full re-stack and blocks export. Painting an area and choosing its planes on the rail is the same idea made direct: instant preview, then a background rebuild of only that area.
3The rail on demand. Hidden for colour work, it opens with the depth brush and shows the filmstrip, the painted area's sharpness trace and the chosen range.
4Scope is explicit. Drawing a region today silently retargets the same sliders. The scope switch sits above them and takes the region's colour.
5Looks, explained. "Adaptive" profiles become named looks with one line each. Choosing one applies it; "Recalculate" keeps the amount instead of resetting it.
6Scale bar from the subject's micrometres per pixel, placed here, written at export.
7Before and after gains a split view and a key, and the surround can be set to black, dark, mid grey or white for judging edges.
