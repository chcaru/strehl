1Pre-flight strip. Start has about ten conditions today and shows none of them. Here each one is a chip; an unmet one turns amber and says what to do, for example "Bounds: mark the end".
2Exposure aids and a test shot. ISO and shutter are already in the camera host's command set but not on screen. A test shot reads clipping and shadow level from one real RAW before a long stack is committed.
3The rail replaces three number fields. Lens position, start, end and every planned plane are drawn on one axis, over the survey's sharpness profile. Drag the handles or use the keys.
4Step with its consequences. Calibrate step measures the sharp width; the table turns that into planes, minutes and gigabytes for each choice, with the risky row marked.
5Named before it is shot. The subject and stack name are asked here, so nothing arrives in the library as "stack-1794611692641-9758".
6One place for what happens next. Checking, retakes, automatic stacking and power-down are four switches in one step, instead of three sections apart.
