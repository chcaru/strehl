Design system

A small set of rules, applied everywhere, replaces fifteen type sizes, four button styles and two theme treatments. The components below are live and follow the theme switch at the top right.

Six rules

1

The photograph is the page

Whatever station is open, the picture is the largest thing on it, on a neutral surround. Evidence comes to the picture; the picture does not shrink for the evidence.

2

Plain words first, numbers one step behind

Every reading has a name a photographer would use and a sentence saying what it means. The measured value and the engine's term follow it, never replace it.

3

Amber means "selected", never "careful"

The accent marks the current thing and the main action. Status has its own colours and its own shapes: ● fine, ▲ look, ◆ failed, ○ not yet, ◐ running.

4

Nothing waits in silence

Every task that can take more than two seconds shows what it is doing, how far it is, what it is waiting for, how to stop it, and what happens next.

5

A disabled control says why

On the control or beside it, in words, with the fix one click away. Never only in a hover.

6

One word for one thing

Plane, stack, render, version, export. The table at the foot of this page retires the synonyms.
