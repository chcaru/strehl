# Native chip crop: joint reconstruction

VERIFIED by independently viewing `joint-comparison-1/render/{mhc,joint-nine,joint-seven-heldout,wrong-map-seven}-native.png` at their native 328-pixel size and reading `joint-comparison-1/heldout.json`.

This crop does not show a photographic improvement. Both joint reconstructions thicken and soften the long vertical rails; their cyan/blue edges acquire conspicuous regular stipple. The small horizontal rail segments look less cleanly separated than in the MHC anchor. Seven and nine frames are visually close. The deliberately wrong-map result is visibly worse. There is no evidence here that the new stipple is recovered fine detail.

The seven-frame arm genuinely excludes frames 147 and 151. Across common held-out observations its R/G/B RMSE is .037061/.079358/.167225, below the anchor's .090720/.107488/.188070 and the wrong-map arm's .053462/.098334/.193766. These errors can favour softened structure; they do not overturn the native-image verdict. Nine-frame numbers include the predicted observations and are not held-out validation. Error-squared/observation-variance ratios of 171.6/723.9/758.7 omit reconstruction uncertainty and model mismatch, so they are not calibrated chi-square results or proof that registration is the cause.

Two bounded discriminators can use the nine retained u16 crops, with no RAW reread or full-stack render:

1. **Transferable local displacement.** Measure a small translation correction to each existing map on fixed, identically low-passed green patches. Fit on G1 in spatial training tiles and test on G2 at its actual CFA coordinates in separate spatial tiles. Keep gain/offset nuisance terms in both corrected and uncorrected arms. Report displacement in native pixels of reference149 and held-out prediction change; consistency across the two green phases matters more than the fitted residual. A planted translation is the positive control and a blur-only copy is the placebo: if the latter invents a comparable displacement, this estimator cannot diagnose registration. Freeze successful corrections before reconstructing anything.

2. **Residual blur after accounting for displacement.** On the same fixed low-pass band and held-out partition, compare the translation model with translation plus one differential-blur term proportional to the 2-D Laplacian. Refit the translation terms jointly with this term, retaining the same gain/offset terms, so the blur term cannot merely inherit a fixed translation error. Report the incremental held-out improvement and coefficient sign across G1/G2. A blur-only plant must exercise this term; a translation-only plant must be explained by the translation arm. This is a local small-blur discriminator, not a complete optical PSF estimate. Failure to transfer means neither simple explanation is established.

The two residual signatures have different local structure: displacement produces first-derivative edge residuals; a small symmetric blur change produces a second-derivative residual. Repetitive rails, coloured optical behaviour, motion/occlusion, or larger defocus can defeat either approximation. Stable correction plus held-out improvement would justify a single new patch reconstruction; a lower training residual alone would not.
