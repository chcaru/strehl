# Eight-frame chip crop: final photographic judgment

VERIFIED by independent native-size viewing of the two 328-pixel images in `mhc-catmul-mean-2/render`, the matched source MHC and local joint inversion in `joint-local-comparison-1/render`, and `joint-backprojection-comparison-1/render/{local-eight-mean,local-eight-four-updates}-native.png`. The latter receipt reports the same eight frames146–153, exactly four updates, unchanged initializer bits and pixel-exact product development of the baseline. No code or RAW files were changed for this review.

The corrected-map mean is the clearest useful photographic result among these stack treatments on this crop. Compared with the original-map eight-frame mean, it gives tighter, more continuous long rails and better-separated horizontal ladder segments. It is substantially cleaner than the local full-inverse reconstruction, which shows strong regular cyan/blue stipple. Against source MHC, it exchanges some fine edge crispness for smoother edges and background; these views alone do not demonstrate added resolved detail.

The four fixed RAW residual updates add modest edge contrast and texture, particularly to the horizontal ladder segments. They also increase cyan/blue variation and bright edge speckle. I cannot identify a newly separated, source-supported feature that the corrected mean missed. They avoid the full inverse's severe periodic stipple, but do not show a decisive net photographic gain over the corrected mean. The result is a sharper/noisier tradeoff, consistent with the independent flat-noise controls, not a demonstrated quality breakthrough.

The image judgment therefore favours retaining the corrected-map mean as the reference result from this patch. The useful measured intervention is local placement followed by the same mean operation. Lower observation residual does not justify promoting either inverse reconstruction. This is one crop, one short focus window, and one set of fitted local corrections; it is not evidence of general performance on deep focus stacks, mixed-depth occlusions, or the complete diagnostic corpus. No iteration tuning, new solver variant, PSF experiment or new RAW decode was performed for this judgment.
