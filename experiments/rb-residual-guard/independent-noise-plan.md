# Independent residual guard arithmetic control

Fixed before the image screen; one helper called by that same test. No old
`rb-own-guide` files are changed and no new calibration is attempted.

Under a zero-mean linear Gaussian coefficient and an independent equal-variance
probe estimate, eight independent pair differences give
`Vhat = sum((La-Lb)^2)/16 = sigma² * chi²(8)/8`, so `Loriginal²/Vhat` has the
F(1,8) law. This premise is not established for rounded MHC/Catmull/CA data,
signal-dependent injected variance, or content-seeded probes merely by checking
the arithmetic. The actual flat fixtures remain binding.

The threshold uses survival probability `.01/(2*N*nb*25)`. Its union bound covers
the two colours, source count, reached AC bands and the existing 5 by 5 support
for one local coefficient-support query. Correlation among these events does not
invalidate the union bound, but this is neither a full-image nor a literal native
output-pixel family-wise guarantee: pyramid collapse has larger support.

Root's retained SciPy references (`quantile-reference.json`, generated by
`quantile-reference.py`, SciPy1.17.1) independently supply six thresholds:
N/bands `(2,1),(5,4),(9,4),(16,7),(70,7),(299,7)`. Both threshold and survival at
the independent reference must agree within relative `2e-7`; inverting a tail
and checking it against the same tail alone would not be evidence. Halving each
threshold must increase its tail beyond five times the target. Invalid tail
probabilities, coefficient values and variance values must follow the fixed
author/root refusal contract, not silently activate a different estimator.
Pre-run clarification: zero R/B variance refuses activation (abstains), including
nonzero signal; it does not reject the entire source. Negative/nonfinite variance
and nonfinite signal are errors. Zero variance is not infinite confidence. The
constructor binds q to the declared source count and reached band count, and
exactly that many sources must be supplied. Root also fixed image arithmetic
before execution: solve the scalar quantile in f64, round it upward to f32 once,
then compare finite f32 `L*L > q_f32*V`. The smallest f32 value not below q is
checked independently with its bit predecessor; mask operands remain comfortably
away from that boundary.
Scalar survival at positive infinity and inverse survival at probability one are
valid endpoints (zero and zero respectively), distinct from forbidden nonfinite
image operands. The scalar helper admits the retained large-N references; the
actual generated activation/image consumer remains bounded to at most16 sources.
Finite image operands whose f32 square/product overflows must also be refused.

Literal spatial controls use an odd production pyramid. A single R exceedance
at an interior coefficient activates exactly its Chebyshev-radius-two support;
a B exceedance at a corner exercises clamped-edge support. Additional source and
band impulses verify OR across sources and channel isolation. A huge green
coefficient alone must never activate R/B. A literal enumeration of each queried
5 by 5 clamped support is the independent oracle; there is no second dilation
and no cross-band expansion. A subthreshold case leaves all masks zero, and
doubling variance can turn a chosen exceedance off. All witnesses must exercise
both zeros and ones, so a constant mask cannot pass.

The unsmoothed pair-variance API is checked against literal eight-pair squares
before any energy smoothing, with unequal channels and a spatial impulse.
Applying the energy smoother before the residual test must differ on that
impulse. A missing factor-two division must also be rejected. Coefficient
variance uses `1e-5 + 2e-5*max(abs(expected),abs(actual))` per value; mask and
parent-zero identity checks are exact. Canonical/own mixing and camera-green
identity remain the main image/consumer reviewer's responsibility.
