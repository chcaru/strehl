## Choice challenges and required resolutions

1. **Name the actual quantity the gate sees.** In current `BandMerge::add_frame_with_gain`, the luma pyramid produces `L_k`, the code squares it and applies `smooth5` to obtain `E_k`, and separately Gaussian-reduces the supplied variance to `V_k`. The hard floor is `gate*effective_beta*max(V_k,0)`. The observed weight also depends on actual admission mode, exponent/significance and any mean-coarse bypass. Comparing unsmoothed temporal `Var(L_k)` alone to the floor misses the consumer. Retain actual `L_k`, `E_k`, `V_k`, selected beta/fallback, floor/weight and bypass/base context. Independently compute the temporal variance of `L_k`, then apply the matching spatial averaging when comparing its noise contribution at the energy consumer. Paper's temporal mean structure belongs separately from this noise term: raw `E_k` contains squared scene signal. The producer author has agreed to a read-only hook after actual weight creation and before accumulation, plus four plane coefficients read from the already prepared production cache. That design addresses the observation point; its concrete patch still needs source review.

2. **A placed identity map is not an all-channel identity null.** `warp_frame_in_place_fullres` uses the current native path: black subtraction/WB, MHC channel scratch planes, site-specific gather, unbalancing and u16 storage. It composes the gear-guarded CA profile from the actual capture path even when the supplied similarity is identity. In particular, R/B may move while green remains unchanged. No-map bypasses this route. The charter's literal “no RGB conversion before measurement” conflicts with preserving these existing native internals; root must clarify that this prohibits a separate RGB-input/alternate consumer, not removal of the native gather's internal transformation. Do not disable CA or switch gather to manufacture equality. Within each fixed arm, observer off/on/placebo must preserve output bytes. Between no-map and placed identity, retain the actual transformations and beta; equality is an observation, not a demanded calibration result.

3. **ISO160 is a finite control condition, not a calibration optimum.** Its practical merit is the existing same-ISO/exposure paper and dark blocks, both designated controls. It probes only the lower read-gain regime; it cannot adjudicate the high-ISO gain-step issue already addressed by M61. The groups differ in time and release mode and may differ in temperature/state. Never subtract the dark block into the paper block or jointly fit them into a matched physical calibration pair. Preserve all four planes and within-plane neighbours: M68/M72 already defeat a one-number blue or special-green interpretation. Known per-plane disagreement is a retained historical expectation to interpret, not a newly discovered general defect. Code quantization matters at low signal; an automatic extra1/12 DN² in an empirically fitted dark-DN floor can double-count it.

4. **Change the estimator/reference coupling rather than repeat the recommendation's mean formula.** For paper, my proposed main contrast is `((x0-x1)^2+(x2-x3)^2)/4` at each fixed address, using frame4 as a separate signal-prediction witness. The all-five unbiased sample variance, with4 temporal degrees of freedom per scalar site, becomes a sensitivity result. Under equal means and independent equal-variance noise the pair contrast is unbiased and has2 conditional Gaussian degrees of freedom; unequal means add nuisance power. Frame4 is not part of either pair, which breaks that direct prediction/outcome reuse. It remains a noisy signal witness, not a fitted truth. For dark, use the11 disjoint pair contrasts alongside the all22-frame sample variance with21 temporal degrees of freedom. These sensitivity arms share images; they are not independent replications. The protocol author has accepted this contrast/reference rechoice in its source proposal.

5. **Do not purchase precision with a pixel count.** Five paper frames are still five temporal observations. Spatial neighbours, overlapping band supports and sensor row/common-mode components are dependent. Treat window/pair dispersion as observed diagnostic information; do not use `4*Npixels` as independent chi-squared degrees of freedom. A conditional Gaussian interval can be labelled at a fixed scalar site/linear coefficient if its assumptions are explicit; it is not an exact physical interval for quantized/clamped values. The smoothed squared energy is not one Gaussian scalar and receives no such interval. The protocol author proposes descriptive pooled ratios with pair/block dispersion, not a fabricated whole-crop confidence interval. Very coarse overlapping coefficients do not restore missing temporal replication.

6. **A nuisance screen must be able to reject without choosing model agreement.** Do not admit paper sites only when temporal residuals agree with the same sensor variance being challenged. Freeze source-input drift/illumination/shift screens and their absolute units before pixel reads, with a known nuisance, a null and explicit minimum texture/sensitivity. Do not fit and subtract a temporal brightness or geometric correction after a refusal. A shift smaller than a screen's detection floor remains unresolved; “no detected motion” is not proof of stationarity. Paper texture can amplify tiny shifts and spatial SD cannot replace temporal noise. If the five-frame burst cannot support the promised nuisance bound, refuse the affected physical attribution while retaining decode/observer/transport observations. This is still pending a cohesive protocol with actual thresholds and power examples.

7. **Keep clamp effects out of a gain verdict.** Record source u16 and signed black-subtracted values before consumed nonnegative planes, then green luma and actual model variance. For illustration only, a continuous zero-mean Gaussian passed through `max(X,0)` has variance `(1/2-1/(2*pi))*sigma²`, not `sigma²` (DERIVED, no simulation run). Real quantization, pedestal offset and spatial correlation can change that illustration. The current white beta probe deliberately uses a high pedestal and large noise amplitude to avoid the clamp. It therefore does not certify the physical low-signal clamp transfer. A post-clamp mismatch may be a correctly observed processing effect rather than an incorrect gain. Do not pool pre-clamp dark calibration with consumed-clamp comparisons or patch a single covariance term to force agreement.

## Crop, phase and probe population

The selected128x128 Crop reaches levels128,64,32,16,8: **four Laplacian bands plus the base**, even when the requested maximum band count is seven. `exponent_at(k,nb)` uses the actual depth, so this is also a different finite-depth weight schedule from the full frame. No default or coefficient is changed, but its evidence is local opt-in Crop evidence. M130 already repaired the historical late-crop area loss on its registered image arm; neither that image-identity inset nor the older cover-default failure describes a white-noise population at all boundaries of this small crop.

I independently chose phase-aware support strata instead of labelling a fixed-width border “interior.” Source derivation: the original-level radius of Gaussian level `k` is `2*(2^k-1)`. A Laplacian coefficient adds at most `4*2^k` at an even index or `3*2^k` at an odd index. Including the final five-tap smoothing gives the following sufficient energy-support radii about original coordinate `2^k*q`:

* even coefficient index `q`: `8*2^k-2`;
* odd coefficient index `q`: `7*2^k-2`.

Apply each axis independently. In a128-wide domain, the resulting fully supported index intervals are k0:`5..121`, k1:`7..57`, k2:`7..25`, k3:`7..9`, inclusive. These are SOURCE-DERIVED support counts, not executed controls or observed samples. The independent protocol author must confirm the derivation against the exact source before freezing it. A simpler worst-phase bound leaves only one k3 coefficient; the phase-aware bound can leave nine sites, all with heavily overlapping supports. Neither is a large independent interior population. Preserve per-axis phase, whether each stencil touches left/right/top/bottom replication, and global quad/CFA origins. A copied-row, phase/address or boundary mutation must affect the intended fixed stratum; a centre-only check cannot stand for it.

`gatefloor::run` limits probe bands to `min(4,sizes.len()-2)`. On this crop it can provide beta only for k0..2; k3 falls back to the white `BandGain`. The pool also trims `max(width/8,3)` and requires at least256 retained sites:128/64/32 levels can satisfy that size rule,16 cannot. Actual observation must distinguish measured beta, missing/nonfinite beta and fallback. `BandWorker::fold` truncates at the first nonfinite entry rather than accepting later entries across a gap.

The old probe precision was measured on other domains, not these128-crop fields. Each frame index uses its own probe realization; finite probe sampling variation can make placed-identity beta differ from white and between frames even without a sensor-law defect. Preserve the raw beta operands and resulting prediction dispersion. Do not reuse a historical0.5% band or require no-map and identity output equality. The independent decoder corroborates source codes; it cannot turn a simulated probe's Monte Carlo difference into physical camera evidence.

The remaining useful question is a finite comparison between frozen sensor prediction and independently observed temporal behaviour at actual native consumer inputs, with attribution separated from nuisance, clamp, boundary and probe sampling. A premise refusal or a known limited mismatch can be the result. No profile fitting, default promotion, M1/M2 rehabilitation or SOTA verdict follows.
