# Independent native photographic review: full star-sand pair

**Verdict: no identifiable photographic improvement at native display size.** I cannot point to a newly separated pore, restored ridge, or recovered surface feature in the candidate. The intervention changes numerical values, but that is not a visible second-scene image-quality win.

All twelve native PNGs were viewed: core, four transition boundaries, and far control, baseline/candidate for each. No resized overview, enlarged image, gradient score, or change-count threshold was used to judge quality. The crop coordinates and input TIFF identities are frozen in `receipt.json`.

The core retains the same rounded, low-contrast pore rims and soft granular surface texture in both arms. The cyan/pink bright points remain similar. I do not identify a concrete lost structure either, so the verdict is a photographic null rather than a clear regression. Slight local brightness or texture changes are insufficient to claim restored detail.

The right and bottom transition views preserve the visible grain outlines and openings. I see no obvious rectangular boundary, displaced contour, or new seam in any of the four transition views. No convincing new colour-stipple cost appears. These structured crops do not establish unchanged sensor noise or replace a flat-noise control.

The distant ridged-shell PNGs look identical at native size. The saved receipt reports a maximum RGB16 difference of 1/65535 in that crop, so it is not byte-identical at 16-bit precision; this is not evidence of a visible effect. The far-control listing and global changed-pixel count were not used as quality criteria.

The prior held-out low-pass guide improvement therefore does **not** demonstrate a useful full-stack photographic improvement on this ROI. This local intervention should not be presented as having restored porous-grain detail. A matched source/gather/merge comparison is the next useful diagnosis: it can show whether one of the selected source observations carries clearer structure than the merged image. Failure to find such a source among three selected frames would not establish an acquisition or optical limit.

No source, runner, configuration, image, or production code was changed during this photographic review.
