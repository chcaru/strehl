# Actual marigold source contributions

Status: implemented, UNVERIFIED until independent controls and root execution.
This is a source-attribution experiment on the corrected488-frame marigold4
render, not a new treatment, quality promotion, or SOTA claim.

The earlier complete coarse-source prior and renderer factorial did not repair
the visible haze. The fixed112/152/320 source-pure test showed why replacing one
complete source is inadequate:112 removes the farther hairs,152 removes the
nearer tips, and neither clears all haze. The known mean-base and optical-support
closures in the current quality path remain closed. This observer asks a new,
bounded question: **which actual contributions retain or erase those two real
signed structures, and does a distinct temporal group add unwanted context?**

Prior-art boundary, checked before execution: `docs/research/V-DILUTE.md`
