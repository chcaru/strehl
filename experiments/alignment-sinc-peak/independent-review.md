# Independent sinc-peak protocol and source review

2026-09-09. SOURCE-VERIFIED / DERIVED before numerical execution. This reviewer authors only independent controls and this review; root builds and executes. No original capture, native ladder, new alignment solver or image render is used. The finite actual-NCC comparison is in design.md; source author and independent verifier do not execute their own work.

The independent literal callback invokes the real radius4 refinement helper. Nine accepted positive inputs are analytic Gaussian, broad rotated ellipse and crossed Gaussian peaks at zero and both signs of(0.1875,-0.3125). These continuous functions have independently known maxima; none was generated by Lanczos interpolation. Their coarse0.15px error bar detects sign/large false-maximum errors, while the centred0.001px bar, reflection/transpose and positive affine score transform0.25*s+0.125 commission symmetry and offset invariance. A wrong shift sign must exceed0.5px on the signed controls. The actual48-row field test supplies the much tighter accuracy evidence.

Two ringing challenges multiply a positive Gaussian envelope by a bounded cosine-product carrier with frequencies2.6 and2.2 radians/pixel. Its maximum is analytically fixed by the envelope and carrier; the candidate may decline it, but an accepted result farther than0.15px fails. This is one concrete adversary, not a guarantee of detecting arbitrary ringing. Seven mandatory refusal inputs cover flat scores, NaN/infinite support, plateau, separated equal maxima, insufficient radius4 union support and an outside-cell maximum. The last uses a rotated convex quadratic centred(0.625,0.25) whose actual discrete maximum remains the supplied central sample: it exercises a valid integer winner with a continuous maximum outside the permitted cell. No input is selected after observing a fitted peak.

The source helper's normalization and derivative quotient are consistent by inspection. It uses finite9x9 union support, scores relative to the integer peak, and a fixed Newton/backtracking search with at most80 full interpolant evaluations. It accepts only a finite, strictly interior local stationary point with negative-definite Hessian and a small Newton step. It does not certify the global maximum or absence of all interpolation-induced peaks. The compact radius4 support is only once differentiable at an integer tap boundary: the second derivative there is one-sided. The analytical derivatives therefore cannot validate their own peak truth; the independent fields and symmetry/refusal controls supply a different check.

The source seam preserves both actual NCC score loops, their contrast/peak/status rules, the integer winner and the original two parabolic expressions. Ordinary public calls enter the const-false implementation. Explicit candidate mode changes only the fractional offsets after integer admission, and every declined helper result falls back to the original parabola values. In the warped route, the refined reference residual still passes through the existing linear map when forming the returned moving-space displacement. The old matcher is not given a new periodic-texture uniqueness guarantee by a local interpolation refusal.

The frozen actual-field population contains all48 paired rows: six signed fractions, two real NCC paths and four content/noise blocks. Both modes receive identical directly sampled continuous images. Matrix truth and inverse error conversion must be literal independent algebra rather than the production transform helper. Same-focus requires20% RMS improvement per route, an absolute0.075px maximum, and the added guard max<=old max+0.005px. The latter was independently requested so one worsening fraction cannot hide behind a better mean. Defocus, mixed-depth and noisy blocks have their fixed nonregression/absolute bars. Every block needs at least4/6 active sinc rows and a changed result; fallback-only or too-small old error is inconclusive. The signed rows and separate maxima remain visible, and no fractional histogram can accept the candidate.
