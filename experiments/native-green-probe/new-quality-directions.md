# New quality directions: one bounded shared-pupil reconstruction experiment

2026-09-09. **ARGUED recommendation, not a quality result.** Primary papers and canonical project sources only; no code, data, model weights or dependencies downloaded, no RAW decoded, no implementation or numerical experiment run. The current program checkpoint and SYNTHESIS architecture were read. Exact probe speed gains do not settle photographic quality.

**Recommend one small, opt-in two-layer radiance reconstruction prototype at the current pyramid output consumer.** Its new question is whether spatially varying radiance integrated through the same pupil as foreground visibility can improve the already saved textured boundary. This is narrower than a general inverse stacker. It must not become another oracle-alpha demonstration: M87 already has that result.

## Three distinct routes

1. **Visibility-aware optical inversion.** Hasinoff and Kutulakos jointly recover layered radiance, masks and blur from aperture-bracketed images. Their efficient forward model independently blurs layers and composites with defocused alpha mattes. The paper explicitly calls this approximate: it fails when the visible portion's mean texture differs from the full footprint. Our focus sweep and shared-pupil fixture require the *conditional* footprint, not that product-of-means approximation. [Primary paper, sections 3–5](https://people.csail.mit.edu/hasinoff/pubs/hasinoff-aperture-2007.pdf).

   **Prior result that survives:** M70's almost collinear off-peak spectra and M20's inverse-filter losses argue against a generic iterative sharpening core. M79's independent review explicitly leaves opaque visibility-changing reconstruction open. However, M87 already reports that known alpha makes its two-component regression work on its old plant; its real-image correction destroys hairs, and its global foreground-focus parameter fails. New evidence would be a win on the independent, textured, shared-pupil M194g observations through today's pyramid TIFF path. No old scalar alpha fit, focus-index propagation, or profile-flattening score is sufficient. This is the selected bounded route.

2. **Direct CFA multi-frame reconstruction.** Wronski et al. estimate RGB from local, motion-mapped same-colour RAW samples with anisotropic kernels and robust weights, avoiding a separate demosaic. The cross-frame sample phases are information; merely swapping Catmull–Rom for Gaussian interpolation is not the method. The paper also requires usable optical detail and phase diversity, and discusses failure without them. [Primary paper, sections 3, 4 and 7](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.03277v1).

   **Consumer and limit:** `load_frame` currently demosaics, warps and requantizes each frame before band fusion. A real implementation would accumulate original photosites at the fusion consumer, not replace the final demosaicer. M194b's saved analytic-colour/geometry fixture has independent chromatic structure and phase diversity, so it can reject colour erasure and phase mistakes. Its colour arm has no chromatic defocus; the scalar optical arm covers defocus. B-algorithms already proposed Wronski, and M182 shows that amplitude alone rewards bad interpolation. This remains plausible, but requires retaining focus-conditioned cross-frame weights at original sample locations. It is a larger production change than the bounded layer prototype; macro diffraction may leave little sampling headroom. No super-resolution or speed gain is inferred from the phone paper.

3. **Learned RAW fusion with an explicit observation check.** Araujo, Ponce and Mairal train a RAW-to-RGB focus stacker on 94 real, 30-frame bursts with commercial-stack pseudo targets. The paper identifies fixed burst length, prealignment and pseudo-target defects as limitations. Its RGB training objective is not a calibrated CFA observation-consistency constraint. [Primary paper, sections 3 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.17846v1).

   A future prior could be coupled to a physical re-render residual, but a small residual cannot certify detail in the optical null space. The same M194b/M194g saved truth could expose hallucinated colour, lost texture or boundary error. Training-domain coverage, usable model rights/weights, large-stack fusion and an actual CFA/visibility observation operator are prerequisites; this review did not establish them. B-algorithms already records this literature, and M164 does not turn a qualified surrogate result into a SOTA claim. Do not start training or import a network in this continuation.

## Smallest proposed production experiment

**Operator.** At each treated boundary site, represent each of two surfaces by a local affine radiance patch: value and two slopes, six unknowns per spectral colour in total. Fit these from observed same-colour CFA samples across the existing five frames and a small, fixed neighbourhood, using the injected noise model. Both green phases share the same spectral field but retain their actual native coordinates. Do not borrow scene amplitudes, phases or slopes from the truth definition.

The design-matrix coefficients are the zeroth and first spatial moments of each surface's **visible** pupil footprint, integrated over the sensor cell. The foreground decision and background intersection use the same pupil ray. For the fixture's straight silhouette and uniform disc, these are circular-segment moments plus cell integration; no image-sized optical simulation is needed in the product. This is a proposed new estimator, not an assertion that moments or numerical approximations have been implemented correctly.

**Exact insertion.** An optional accumulator observes each decoded frame after existing defect handling and before `load_frame` calls `warp_frame_in_place`. It reads original CFA, levels, WB and injected noise and keeps only bounded sufficient statistics. After pyramid collapse, its accepted local estimates replace sites in the four output planes before `Merged::Bands` reaches `write_merged`; demosaic, crop, colour development and the actual TIFF writer stay the current consumers. No aligned stack is stored. Off/no-treatment must be byte-identical. This needs a small product operator and source hook, not a new truth renderer or comparison harness.

**Existing truth.** Reuse M194g's saved 320×240, five-frame CFA inputs, ideal image, fixed window/boundary regions and existing TIFF scorer. Its textured scene has independent foreground/background waves under shared-ray visibility; the published noise-off window error remains about 62–63 DN. That is an unresolved output error, **not proof that this model will fix it**. Its flat arm is a common-radiance noise control, not a positive contrast-boundary example. M194b's saved colour arm remains the genuine-colour no-treatment control.

**Rejecting inputs and decision.** Run the existing noisy-flat control first. An inverse that amplifies its noise fails. Then the existing textured boundary can reject the affine approximation itself: its short-period waves need not be affine over the footprint, so smoothing them to improve a boundary average is a real failure. Require improved known boundary fidelity without worsening the fixed detail-window fidelity or temporal noise; keep local extremes alongside pooled error. MSE can reward smoothing, variance can reward bias, and wave amplitude can reward wrong phase or overshoot: retain their existing paired readings, not a new aggregate score. Identical visibility rows must cause a rank-deficient fit to abstain. A displaced boundary supplied to the prototype is a model-error destroyer, not a candidate setting. No-treatment/disabled output identity is mandatory.

**Main prerequisite and stopping point.** The first software experiment may take only M194g's declared geometry, depths and pupil scale as explicit experimental inputs. It may not read latent radiance. This is an oracle-*geometry* limitation and cannot admit real-stack use. M87 already establishes that estimating visibility on thin structures can dominate everything else; no current trustworthy automatic boundary/PSF source was identified here. If the affine model loses on the saved textured truth, stop this prototype rather than add a parameter sweep, larger basis, neural prior or new fixture. If it wins, the next decision is whether measured native geometry/visibility can support it, not default promotion. Timing is an additional measured cost, never presumed from the six-parameter solve.
