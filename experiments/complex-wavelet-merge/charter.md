# One-level complex coefficient fusion, identity RAW fixture scope

Root allocation after the complete green-match photograph retained its target
artifacts. Prior-work audit found no native RAW/known-truth test of this complex
directional fusion operator. External comparator differences do not establish
which part helps. This is a bounded operator test, not a new product architecture
or an implementation of the complete comparator.

Author owns a test-only Rust one-level analysis/synthesis port with the full
MIT notice from the pinned Petteri Aimonen implementation. Preserve its complex
coefficient phase, filter origin, separable order and periodic boundary. Reuse
the actual native source gather on existing generated u16 fixtures at identity
geometry only. Keep four CFA colour planes and the existing camera developer.
Compare to actual BandWorker with bands=1 and the same mean coarse component;
comparing a one-level coarse mean to the seven-band product would change scope.

The proposed rule uses a fixed smooth5 spatial support on each directional
complex guide energy and its propagated injected variance. Use the existing
SoftPower rule and constants, one weight shared by all four CFA coefficients
of that direction. Zero supported weight returns the coefficient mean. LL is
the plain mean. Do not take the median of three directional weights: an ideal
oriented grating has two inactive detail directions and that median would
erase the active direction by construction. The faithful hard complex maximum
with H/V/D donor vote is an unsafe comparator/control, not a shippable merge
reference or evidence of noise safety.

At identity, assert the actual gathered known CFA sites equal the original
black-subtracted sensor values and the guide is the original green-pair mean.
For independent identity quads, the complex coefficient variance is the sum
of the injected guide variance times squared magnitude of the 6x6 complex
analysis kernel. This is propagation of the supplied noise model, not an
independent stage calibration. Do not reuse Laplacian beta. Refuse fixture
clipping in the control that requires this noise relation, and record that
domain restriction. A separate clipping witness must not become a claim of
clipping safety.

Fractional resampling introduces covariance and is explicitly outside this
initial test. No fractional RAW replay is allowed until the composed transfer
or actual injected-noise probe is implemented and checked for this transform.
The one-level result cannot establish multilevel quality. No parameter sweep,
new optical fit, original RAW read, full photograph or production default is
authorized by this allocation.

An independent agent owns fixtures and interpretation; root builds and runs.
Commission roundtrip, constant, impulse and phase/origin behavior before the
quality cases. Test flat fields first, then real-colour, varying focus, and
single horizontal/vertical/diagonal structure with independently known truth.
Pair error with truth-capped component recovery, and show each destroyer can
fail. Preserve exact source arrays, active-origin addressing and dimensions.
Use fixed modest fixtures; a failing operator closes its exact rule without
an open-ended sweep. All work stays below this experiment directory except
one minimal test-only include in the CLI.
