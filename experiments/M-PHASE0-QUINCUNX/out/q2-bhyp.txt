C8  direction of the hard-cutoff idealisation, stated not assumed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
    A real MTF decays to zero rather than stepping. Energy just below the
    true cutoff is therefore attenuated and may go undetected, so the
    aperture that reads null has a true cutoff slightly ABOVE the band
    edge, not at it. f_c is proportional to 1/B, so the true B is slightly
    SMALLER than the lower bound derived above. Both bounds move the same
    way, and the effect pushes AWAY from H1 and H2, never toward them.
    The exclusion is therefore conservative; the inclusion of H3 is not.
