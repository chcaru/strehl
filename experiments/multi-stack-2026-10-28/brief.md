## Controls and bounds

Alignment consumes every frame placement in the common anchor gauge; state
sensor/plane pixel scale. Exercise curved tracks, shuffled pairs, disconnected
graphs, reversed/unequal ladders and large viewpoint changes. Never chain poses
or smooth across bracket boundaries.

Repeat fusion consumes CFA values and injected noise. Flat noise controls run
first, then duplicates, misregistration, motion, clipping and light drift, with
known sharp and no-op single-pass controls. FRC agreement cannot establish
accuracy under shared processing or fixed-pattern noise.
