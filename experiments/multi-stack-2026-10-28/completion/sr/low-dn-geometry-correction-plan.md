# Low-DN geometry correction plan

Status: ARGUED, unimplemented experimental operator. The known failing max-held-tile NCC positive and unchanged0.15sensor gate remain the baseline.

The current instrument samples moving pixels bilinearly, computes integer-displacement NCC, and fits a three-score/quadratic peak. Its high-SNR alias correction is tested, but at insect DN each24x24quad holdout produces a noisy translation; the maximum across several holdouts exaggerates that error. Larger kernels alone can also mix focus/depth regimes. Replacing the maximum with a mean would hide local motion and is not the proposed fix.

First correction arm: retain the frozen low-pass and disjoint training/held-out tile locations; after the broad NCC search, refine each holdout translation continuously against the original low-passed moving plane using the owned Catmull-Rom interpolator. Fit and eliminate one local brightness gain and offset, then iterate a bounded2D Gauss-Newton update over the same complete footprint. Keep every admitted pixel and every heldout patch; no residual-based tile rejection or smaller threshold selection. Report both old and refined per-patch residuals. No new sensor-noise model is estimated.

Commission the full actual code path on the existing measured-curvature high-bandwidth color plant at the exact insect signal/noise levels and multiple independent seeds. Exercise flat/one-axis, wrong saved placement, clipping and split motion first; use independent larger seed families for validation after implementation. The unchanged0.15 bound is assessed against known consuming-placement truth. A false positive must reject the arm even if nominal fit residual improves. If low-DN precision remains insufficient, report it as unavailable and do not reinterpret lack of rejection as accuracy.

A separate, predeclared fixed46x46quad/3x3grid geometry arm can test increased information per spatially separated holdout: spacing includes the exact Gaussian and search halo, leaving5train/4held tiles within the192quad window. It changes the instrument, not the0.15 acceptance threshold; compare against24/4 under identical inputs and preserve split-motion negatives. This is not appropriate where the sealed focus map provides no sufficiently large same-depth support.

The chart census uses three independently nominated photographic regions and exact exposure gains before either estimator's outcomes are seen. Its higher recorded signal can establish a useful scoped positive even if the insect remains unavailable. Any future direct local correction must return the actual consuming per-color pulls, fit all direct pair constraints globally in the chosen anchor component, and score those pulls on disjoint holdouts and cycle geometry. No edge chaining.
