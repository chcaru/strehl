# Frozen focus/coverage island nomination

This is a separately named research arm. It preserves the fixed-centre runs,
including their unavailable witnesses, native refusals and unqualified meshes.
Selection is **conditional on the already bound upstream guide focus/coverage
masks, source rankings, maps and initial FIT evidence**. It is not blind to all
validation photons. No native held residual, NCC score or inspected appearance
is an input to the rule.

Before stage-two measurements:

1. Retain the same36 nominal anchors and their original order. Around each,
   inspect the existing4quad guide lattice within an L-infinity radius96quad.
2. Require covered and usable-focus flags in both guides at the reference
   candidate and its initial-FIT-similarity prediction in the moving guide.
   Query the moving guide by nearest lattice centre, exactly as the fixed arm.
3. Rank candidates by squared displacement from the nominal anchor, then
   reference y and x. Original witnesses remain the first ranked reference
   source and up to three distinct ranked moving sources. These are nominations,
   not depth or geometry certificates.
4. In that frozen ranking, select the first candidate whose complete reciprocal
   reservations pass both original-held partitions, all initial attempted
   ancestor footprints and prior-island nonoverlap. The source-window bound
   includes the other source's window projected through the initial cross-pass
   transform, plus the same declared24quad uncertainty in both directions.
5. Retain an entry for every nominal anchor, including unavailable anchors.
   Record candidate count, all geometric refusal reasons, nominal and selected
   points/displacement, queried mask coordinates/flags, focus ordinal/width,
   exact witness identities and physical mesh-cell assignment. Membership uses
   the selected point and actual cell spacing, not the nominal anchor label.
6. Publish the selection receipt, policy hash and frozen role plan before any
   stage-two source intensity read. There is no post-native retry at the next
   ranked centre. Native, reciprocal, held-out, hull-shape and visibility gates
   remain unchanged. Actual measured witness hulls determine support after
   measurement; three nominal anchors never imply three valid sites in a cell.

The search has at most36 times49 times49 mask queries. It reads the frozen
bounded guides only and streams actual originals later. Unavailable regions
stay visible in reports. Core/native controls include flat, directional and
repeating-pattern pairs, skinny/clustered held witnesses, measured-curvature
geometry and depth-discontinuity failures. A usable focus mask alone does not
establish two-dimensional registration information. A successful nomination
count is not placement, coverage or photographic evidence.
