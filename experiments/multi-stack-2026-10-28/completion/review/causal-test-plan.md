## Independent observer and reference contract

1. Work in black-subtracted linear source or scene-reference units, with fixed common color basis, physical pixel gauge, noise model and display. No per-arm tone fit or brightness normalization. Evaluate HDR uncertainty in the same gained units. Preserve both per-color and green-luma quantities.
2. Choose smooth supports from source-only guides independent of the candidate. A smooth-looking patch is not automatically a uniform-radiance target: test held-out through-focus constancy, drift, dust/structure and clipping. Keep temporal variation separate from spatial FPN. Where focus changes alter signal, label a residual rather than temporal noise and use a different qualified support.
3. Use disjoint temporal blocks for eligibility/illumination checks and measurement. Estimate per-pixel temporal scatter after a predeclared stable-signal model; quantify any fitted illumination degrees of freedom on separate supports. Do not fit a private variance curve that then validates the injected model. Compare to the injected variance and retain discrepancies.
4. A shared signal guide is useful for frozen weights, but is not independent if it contains the same photons being scored. Cross-fit the guide or label the prediction conditional on frozen weights. Test the final adaptive gate/weight algorithm separately across independent replicates. For real full-output temporal claims, require disjoint output replicates with comparable qualified support; several near-focus frames are not automatically repeated observations of the same detail.
5. Do not measure a blend's error to one of its constituents and call it true error/noise. For equal unit-variance inputs, output (1-a)A+aB has true variance(1-a)Â²+aÂ², but variance(output-A)=2aÂ². The unchanged A trivially wins the latter statistic. Use independent held-out observations with their noise term accounted for, or paired covariance explicitly; never use the candidate as its own reference.
6. Estimate uncertainty over temporal blocks and spatial blocks larger than the sampler/filter correlation support. Thousands of interpolated pixels do not imply thousands of independent samples. Report confidence intervals and estimator power, not just a favorable ratio. No sqrt(K) claim from a single image's spatial SD.
7. Couple noise to named signal-retention observables and full-photo review. A universal detail metric is not required for a scoped result, but the observable must reject blur, sharpening, displaced structure and constant output before use. A source-anchored held-out edge/line response is an observable, not calibrated physical resolution. FRC is auxiliary and cannot be the sole anti-blur gate.

The small [commission-observer.py](commission-observer.py) executed flat controls first. Across 64 independent 64 x 64 trials it measured expected variances within 0.49%: 400/100 inputs, 125 equal mean, 80 inverse-variance mean; duplicating A retained its variance. Blur reduced output variance from 80.07 to 5.98 while known signal gains fell to 0.728/0.927; the separate two-frequency sentinel correctly refused it. Sharpen, constant output and a one-pixel shift also failed. [observer-controls.json](observer-controls.json) binds these results. This commissions the observer on known truth only; it is not a product, camera or real-detail validation.

## Shared map and diagnostic requests to implementers

A reusable local field should carry reference gauge, pass/coordinate, physical pixel scale, a focus-support distribution or interval, ambiguity, observation count and calibration/source binding. It must distinguish metric depth from an ordinal focus index. Preserve multimodal uncertainty at occlusion edges; an attractive smoothed depth map is not proof. SR needs per-color phases/conditioning among locally eligible originals; mosaics need local visibility and overlap observations. Neither should reuse a single best index as unquestioned truth. Refused regions retain a valid no-SR/single-view result or explicit hole, not invented evidence.

For the coefficient consumer, retain bounded maps of final normalized group/source weight, predicted sum(wÂ²v), effective independent contributors, disagreement causes and primary fallback. Per-color clipping/support is preferable to a single unexplained global mask where channel availability differs. Prove diagnostics on/off do not perturb final image bits. Replacing equal means also requires a decision about group density: one representative per focus slice versus preserving total measurement precision. Test both interpretations on unequal ladders rather than allowing BTreeSet order or pair count to choose the estimator implicitly.
