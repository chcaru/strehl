Concrete next controls:

1. `profile_for_capture_env` resolves current optics at qualification time. Compare its semantic profile/eps values with the retained calibration/operator used to render the outputs. Current environment/source drift must refuse or be recorded as a different operator; original cache identity does not bind this later optical choice.
2. Flat-first commissioning calls `Qualification::observe` with iid synthetic planes directly. It exercises the numerical gate, but does not reproduce the RegionSampler interpolation and the5×5 guide filter covariance. The comments deriving0.219 raw-sigma and0.546 p95 are iid approximations. Commission the complete gather→smooth→gate path with curved/translated measured-map plants before interpreting its acceptance probabilities. Keeping thresholds as descriptive nominal-model screens is a narrower legitimate scope.
3. Two measurement frames give one temporal difference per acquisition window. Neighboring output pixels share interpolation footprints and guide selection. Do not use1024 pixels or adjacent tiles as1024 independent temporal replicates; use explicit spatial/source blocks and show the sample support. Temporal centering removes static texture only if the same latent signal is justified, not merely because a fitted registration exists.
4. R-only qualification cannot validate developed RGB noise: demosaic and the color matrix mix G/B sources whose temporal support refused. Measure the actual CFA/component or coefficient producer if that is the justified claim. A developed-output claim needs a signal-preserving sentinel and a treatment of those unqualified channels.
5. Keep a common unblurred clean reference and show both conditional variance and transfer differences for every actual consumer arm. The earlier precision softening lesson still applies. Candidate-minus-constituent is correlated and is not a temporal-noise observer.

The next actual-consumer paired assay should freeze guide, geometry, ROI eligibility and arm settings before opening the current measurement originals. Same photons across compared arms are appropriate for a paired causal comparison, while independent temporal repetitions are still needed to estimate variance. Report any output selection/admission dependence separately from the frozen-guide conditional variance model. No all-RGB or real-photograph gain has been established by these source qualification counts.
