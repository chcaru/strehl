The next observer tests predictable changing signal rather than declaring textured focus sweeps stationary. It must separate three outcomes: conditional repeatability, independent-target predictive risk, and signal/structure retention. None alone proves photographic improvement.

The relevant independent-noisy-target result requires the correct conditional target mean; the shared noisy-target variance can then cancel in a comparison. Different focus states do not automatically satisfy this condition. This is the specific restriction inherited from [Lehtinen et al., Noise2Noise, section2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/lehtinen18a/lehtinen18a.pdf), not a proposal to train a neural network. Correlated, signal-dependent output noise also cannot be removed with one iid variance number. General correlated-model risk estimators exist, but applying them here would require their model and regularity assumptions; see [Eldar, Generalized SURE](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.3010). I propose an explicit independent-reference construction first, rather than silently differentiating through hard admission gates.

The initial basis is exactly[1,t,t²]; no model-order search occurs on the real measurements. It is a local approximation to a smooth focus/radiometry trajectory, not a universal focus law.

Predict all three withheld validation states. Preserve all spatial structure. A linear temporal prediction has reference covariance

`Cov[p(t),p(u)] = sum_j h_j(t) h_j(u) Sigma_j`,

where each Sigma_j is the injected camera covariance carried through that guide's actual CFA sampler. Shared guides correlate the predictions. The three validation residuals therefore have a full3x3 temporal covariance, in addition to spatial covariance from interpolation. Do not count pixels as temporal repetitions, replace this by independent raw sigma, or estimate a new sensor noise law from residuals. Source gain scales signal by gain and covariance by gain².

For real signal-dependent variance, the expected signal is the frozen guide prediction. Its approximation and any correlation with guide uncertainty remain explicit. Exact sampling/filter covariance can be propagated from the original tap operator, or commissioned by deterministic original-u16 noise probes through that exact operator. Those probes use the existing calibrated model, never fit a scale to make real residuals look white.

## Actual output observer and covariance

Run the same actual short, long, equal-repeat and precision-repeat core consumer on the two withheld measurement states. Keep the frozen mask for every arm, including delivered fallback/refusal pixels. W guides remain the exact same independent acquisition set for both temporal outputs. They may straddle focus changes, so their real signal response must be tested independently of R agreement. Report raw signed differences and half-difference squared before correction.

Then report a distinct guide-predicted nuisance correction: subtract only the change predicted from guides, never the best fit to the measurement difference. Its uncertainty is not free. For a linear source observer, with d=h(t2)-h(t1), the reference contributes `dᵀ Sigma_G d`. For nonlinear output and guide-dependent precision/admission, the cross-covariance with output is generally nonzero; the identity is

`Cov(D-P) = Cov(D) + Cov(P) - Cov(D,P) - Cov(P,D)`.

Therefore do not label corrected residual variance as output noise merely by subtracting reference variance. Commission this exact output path with joint independent acquisition probes, retain bias and covariance terms, and report the real residual as conditional repeatability until the assumptions needed for a variance estimate have been verified. In particular, hard admission and the native fallback mixture are included.

For a more stable causal comparison, also construct noisy reference targets at the measurement state using the independent R trajectory. Keep R separate from W and from Q/V mask selection. Evaluate every candidate against the same target and report paired squared-error differences. Independent target noise cancels in expectation, but shared model bias does not: if target mean error is b, a risk difference contains a term proportional to `-2 b E[candidate-baseline]`. Report Q/R model disagreement as uncertainty without removing unfavorable pixels; transfer failure blocks a benefit claim. Never let a fitted predictor validate its own mean using its training guides.
