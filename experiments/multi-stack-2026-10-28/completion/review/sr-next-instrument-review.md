## Physical and estimator limits

The data are focus sweeps, not fixed-focus bursts. Consecutive frames may change magnification, local PSF, visibility and color-dependent structure. A phase-rich collection of differently blurred samples is not a set of interchangeable measurements of one latent image. The insect guide is also much darker than the chart guide (approximately183/565/213 versus731/1958/1248 cameraDN), but this alone does not explain Green refusals or calibrate their uncertainty. Neither a geometric phase Gram nor a sharpness score establishes optical bandwidth.

The primary [Wronski et al. paper, sections2,5,7](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/pubtools/5211.pdf) requires accurate subpixel registration, useful aliased content and adequate SNR; its native-size direct-CFA benefit includes reducing demosaic artifacts. Its phone results do not transfer automatically to these macro optics. We have not measured an optical cutoff for this capture, so diffraction/defocus is a plausible limit, not an established explanation. A physical bandwidth check should precede another higher-resolution-grid experiment.

LocalConstant at fixed0.35-sensor sigma is an interpolation/averaging estimator, not an optical deconvolution. Changing to affine or a finer lattice cannot manufacture missing optical information. Conversely, an unchanged native photograph can hide useful conditional color prediction because support is tiny. Test both artifact suppression and signal retention; desaturation and blur can improve a chroma/error statistic while destroying the intended result.
