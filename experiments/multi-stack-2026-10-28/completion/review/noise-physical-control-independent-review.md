## Preconditions for interpretation

The revised contract is suitable for bounded native preparation and controls. It does not yet close a quality or noise outcome.

- Commission the exact two-validation-state covariance, timestamp interpolation and actual-core path. The earlier three-state normalization/cutoff is not automatically applicable. Include zero-noise linear drift, curvature, focus change, wrong registration/motion, blur, constant output, color-only damage and clipped/filter-halo support negatives. Two Q endpoints have no model-fit residual freedom.
- Keep all nine preselected physical witnesses, both reference states and all four temporal windows. Qualification and thresholds precede R/M scoring; no favorable survivor selection. Four windows are limited acquisition replication, not a pixel-count confidence interval.
- Before calling R independent, prove geometry/photometry footprints disjoint from the **complete** scored consumer and guide dependencies, including search, resampling, finite filtering and spatial support. The three geometry patches and separate photometry patch are nominations until checked. Full-photo joint alignment may use all32 but cannot silently become the assay's independent-reference geometry.
- The default photometric arm remains gain1. Any separately declared normalization sensitivity uses only its disjoint patch, preserves its uncertainty and cannot be fit to scored R/M values. Fixed-pattern, illumination, focus and geometry model errors remain explicit even when shot/read-noise roles are disjoint.
- Once full-photo preparation has read all32 originals, later claims must say R/M were excluded from **assay selection and estimation**, not that the captures are globally unopened or unseen. Historical nominations and whole-photo use remain disclosed.
- The balanced16+16 full-photo groups are a separate product preservation test. They balance each four-shot block in ordinal but not exactly in timestamp; retain actual times. Inspect full masters/photographs and all native crops under a common transform. A conditional two-source CFA risk result does not automatically quantify the full32 developed-RGB output.

A defensible positive would state that the named actual consumer lowers the fixed two-state independent-reference predictive risk, retains the predetermined structure/color witnesses, and preserves the complete photograph, with model/geometry limitations stated. Absolute sensor sigma, pure noise separation, extra focus depth and general photographic superiority require additional evidence. If only component masks qualify, report that component scope rather than extrapolating to RGB. No such new real result exists at this review checkpoint.
