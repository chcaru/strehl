# Conditional noise observer audit

VERIFIED source/result review,2026-10-28. Reviewed the actual `noise-transfer-controls.rs` producer, `analyze-noise-transfer.py`, input fixture and `noise-transfer-02/{report,analysis}.json`. No producer rerun or camera decode was performed. This reviewer authored earlier precision-core fixes, but did not author this experiment/observer; conclusions are limited accordingly.

The scoped headline is supported: under injected independent Gaussian temporal noise, identity geometry, planted spatial defocus and one frozen excluded noisy guide, the actual production coefficient consumer has approximately13.0% less temporal variance (about6.8% less sigma) than equal weighting on the fixed original-CFA texture. Noisy-guide flat variance ratios0.87294–0.87447 give about12.6% reduction. Texture ratios0.86817–0.86964 give about13.0–13.2%. These are simulation-conditional measurements, not estimates of existing camera noise or delivered-photograph improvement.

Sound choices: flat exercised first; one injected affine model; fixed original texture includes its existing camera noise as signal; independent seeded perturbations vary by acquisition/trial; paired arms reuse those perturbations; Welford centers each pixel across48 realizations, avoiding spatial texture as sigma; the original midtone mask is chosen before outputs; missing-guide outputs/means/variances are bit-exact equal-path nulls; four planes are filtered separately. The noisy guide is sampled once and held fixed, so its uncertainty across guides is deliberately excluded. The ideal guide arm is explicitly best-case and must not replace the noisy-guide result.

The source retains clean-arm differences, which is essential: noisy-guide clean outputs differ from equal by7.22,19.10,13.06,19.61DN RMS per plane; ideal precision differs by9.23,25.10,17.03,25.22DN. These are changes in deterministic signal transfer/adaptive selection, not temporal noise. They are substantial compared with the few-DN reduction in temporal sigma and cannot be dismissed as harmless merely because global projection is near1.

Actionable observer limitation: the corruption test compares blur/constant/one-quad shift/sharpen against equal-clean and correctly detects those foils. The actual candidate's mean is then compared against its own clean output. An implementation that blurs both candidate clean and noisy outputs could still improve variance and pass this own-reference comparison. The current analysis therefore establishes conditional noise transfer, not common-signal preservation. Retaining the clean differences avoids a false quality claim, but does not close this test gap.

Apply the same common-reference signal observer directly to every clean and mean arm against the predeclared unblurred fixture0, keeping its original CFA noise explicitly fixed as conditional signal. Report per-plane and local-band/edge distortions, alongside same-arm temporal behavior; do not optimize or move the ROI. Production sharpening1.4 changes the intended transfer, so this common-reference residual is distortion evidence, not a universal photographic quality score. A same-transfer comparison or explicit transfer-matching control is needed before turning the variance ratio into an SNR claim. Exercise a deliberately blurred producer/candidate through the actual acceptance decision to ensure a low-noise bad arm is rejected, rather than merely showing a detached observer could recognize blur.

Further limits: only48 trials and one fixed guide; no across-guide covariance, actual focus-correspondence error, registration error, original clipping/flare mismatch, motion or sensor-model calibration is measured. The seed pairing helps variance comparison but no uncertainty interval is reported. No blocker for the present carefully scoped simulated-noise statement; blocker for promoting it as real camera SNR or photographic improvement.
