DERIVED/ARGUED: the diffraction calculations are ideal upper bounds, based on an assumed pupil-factor interval. They do not identify the real optical transfer, eligible phase diversity or deliverable luma bandwidth. Reuse original-CFA data and investigate color sampling; do not revive the previously rejected spectral reconstruction unchanged.

DERIVED: the lateral parallax expression is first order. Exact pinhole relative parallax is s*dz/(D+dz) for that convention, and D is not measured. The arithmetic motivates a depth-dependent consumer but cannot validate a fitted warp, monotonicity, hidden-surface visibility or a real mosaic. The old per-worker memory extrapolation is stale relative to the current serial fold and must be replaced with actual tile workspaces.

ARGUED: scale is a retrieval coordinate, not true focus; no-metadata is not proof of no usable local correspondence. Likewise no commanded ladder or measured stage position does not preclude a product with uncertainty and local evidence. Replace overstrong certificates with tested, appropriately scoped behavior; keep the remaining outcomes active.
