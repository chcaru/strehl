## Findings that change the instrument or claim

1. **W spatial support must follow the filter.** In the reviewed `real-noise-consumer-structure-risk.rs` lines207–223, validity intersects only the four W samples at each center, then the guide values receive a radius8 box mean. `real-noise-consumer-structure-consumer.rs` lines83–86 passes the unchanged mask into `FrozenPrediction.support`. An invalid or clipped neighbor can therefore affect a valid precision prediction. Propagate the complete filter footprint and gather its external halo; do not treat a boundary-clamped research crop as physical support. Corpus accepted this finding and implemented an expanded144quad gather, support propagation and crop; the narrow verification update below closes this source-support finding. A halo-only poisoned guide control must exercise the actual consumer and preserve exact equal fallback where independent prediction is unavailable; all-valid values must match the same physical expanded-filter/crop operator; changing the old crop-edge extension is not an invariance claim.

2. **Independent reference noise is not independent target truth.** For target S, reference R=S+e and arms A/B, the observed paired difference is `(A-S)^2-(B-S)^2-2(A-B)e`. Sharing the same R cancels its squared term, not its model bias or covariance with an arm. Disjoint current R/W/Q/V/M acquisitions address the deliberate photon leak. They do not remove the conditional dependence introduced by historical source nomination, shared fitted geometry, calibration, or a misspecified quadratic temporal mean. Those dependencies remain in the scope statement.

3. **The current averaged R is synthetic-only.** The risk driver multiplies each pass's quadratic reference by0.5 because the plant gives both passes identical latent images. The real Q/V census contradicts assuming that relationship without further evidence. Report `risk_to_pass_0` and `risk_to_pass_1` separately for every arm, with their target ordinals and calibration/gauge. A combined result that improves one target and worsens the other is an appearance/focus tradeoff, not a failed report to hide. An optional-primary arm has an explicitly selected target; it is not a new common truth.

4. **Quadratic qualification has finite power.** Three Q samples exactly determine three coefficients. Their zero residual cannot test the mean model; four R samples provide only one residual degree of freedom. In the saved warped controls, cubic and nonquadratic focus cases fail at1200DN but all576 component blocks pass at200DN. The independent-R strong-blur cubic result can have the wrong risk-difference sign. R independence is therefore not an unbiased-risk certificate on arbitrary real focus trajectories. Keep Q/R disagreement, all fixed masks and adverse cases; do not prune real blocks after inspecting R or M.

5. **Risk and structure must remain distinct.** In synthetic HDR quadratic controls at200DN, precision has reference delta-221.130 and known-truth delta-221.696 DN² with projection0.98956. Strong blur has even lower deltas(-415.639/-380.674) while projection falls to0.72632. The fixed0.95..1.05 transfer diagnostic correctly rejects this foil. Mild blur lies on the tolerance boundary at aggregate0.95007 and fails the all-unit check. Before a color-preservation claim, retain component/state results and add a channel-only damage foil: averaging per-color projection ratios can conceal one damaged component. The current quadratic precision component ranges are0.9780..0.9961,0.9847..0.9945,0.9867..0.9930 and0.9852..0.9924; no present component violation was found. This is a gap in the adversarial control, not an observed precision color failure.
