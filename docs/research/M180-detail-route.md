# M180 — route to an admissible detail statistic

## 1. Verdict

The output-only route is closed. The archive-only route is closed. A
featureless target can license a noise/null claim but cannot license delivered
detail. A marginal natural-image prior does not identify instance fidelity.
PCHIP fixes tone, not truth.

One route remains open in principle:

1. before candidate outputs are read, prove per-cell
   `REFERENCE_INFORMATION_DOMINANCE` using independently calibrated
   higher-sampling references `R1/R2` and candidate-blind, complete-acquisition
   scored-path witnesses `S1/S2`;
2. score the candidate against the qualified reference using M135's
   truth-referenced information coordinate;
3. keep transfer, unexplained repeatable structure, flat/null, lineage and
   common-mode results as noncompensating gates;
4. independently linearise tone with the 33-step PCHIP witness; and
5. expose the complete result through a separately frozen reference-bearing
   M167 adapter and pass P1--P7 plus A1--A6.

This is legitimate rather than circular because the external observations are
fixed independently of the candidate, the target code is concealed until
renderers are frozen, and agreement is measured across disjoint exposures and
calibration lineages. In the finite model, a faithful output and an independent
hallucinated output have identical marginals, but covariance with an
independent reference is `0.25` versus `0`. The outside observation changes the
joint law; it does not merely assert that the output is good.

This is **not yet an admitted candidate**. In particular, M135's information
coordinate intentionally ties under a common invertible post-filter. Its
separate transfer coordinate catches post-render blur, but M167 P4 requires a
strictly decreasing candidate-visible resolution result; equality or refusal
fails. M167 expressly reserves a future reference-bearing schema. No committed
adapter currently demonstrates that the complete noncompensating certificate
passes P1--P7 without weakening P4, and local A6 remains false. Calling the
synthetic architecture a result would therefore overstate the evidence.

## 2. What M164 forbids, and what M168 leaves open

M164's operative construction is exact
(`docs/research/M164-m2-repair.md`, §3):

> Any deterministic statistic of `(A,B)` receives the same input in both
> worlds. It cannot reject `B` in the first and reward `B` in the second without
> an assumption or observation outside those outputs.

Its ruling is correspondingly narrow:

> There is no stand-alone, two-render-output repair for the owner's
> detail-preserving comparative-noise claim without an assumption or
> observation outside those outputs.

That proves failure of a **uniform, distribution-free guarantee** over a world
class containing identical-observation/opposite-label collisions. It also
shows why repeat captures are not enough: repeats identify temporal output
noise, but blur truly lowers that noise and does not identify whether lost
high-frequency variation was scene signal or artifact.

M168's correction is load-bearing
(`docs/research/M168-m2-identifiability.md`, warning and §§1.1--1.3):

> The distribution-free impossibility is sound, but it must not be shortened
> to “no reference-free metric can ever ground the claim.”

and:

> A prior, restricted scene model, calibrated generative model, reference, or
> controlled target can add identifying information; any resulting claim
> inherits that assumption and its scope.

Therefore M164 does **not** prove:

- that every individual output pair is ambiguous;
- that a validated model-conditional posterior cannot be useful;
- that known-noise or known-renderer families are untestable;
- that a controlled null target cannot ground a narrow noise claim; or
- that an external reference must have one particular implementation.

It does prove that any successful route must state, validate and inherit the
scope of the information added from outside the rendered outputs.

The invariance lesson remains unchanged. An information coordinate exactly
invariant to a common filter is exactly blind to that filter. M135 uses that
property only for a narrower information/noise coordinate and puts a
noncompensating transfer gate beside it. Treating the invariant coordinate
itself as delivered detail would repeat D8's error.

The later reference analysis further narrows the surviving assumption:

- sampling ratio alone is not identifying;
- a shared objective only identifies its measured non-null passband;
- `R1/R2` must dominate the complete scored physical path `S1/S2` in
  information and transfer in every licensed cell;
- no strong cell may compensate for a weak/null cell; and
- shared pattern, drift or calibration structure must be bounded and charged,
  not relabelled as signal.

The production dominance manifest still begins `MANIFEST BLOCKED`, so none of
those conditions is presently claim-bearing.

## 5. What would make this verdict wrong

The verdict would need revision if any of the following occurred:

1. A physically acquired, independently calibrated `R1/R2` and complete
   `S1/S2` family passed per-cell reference information/transfer dominance,
   common-mode, support and sufficiency gates, and its candidate then passed
   M167 P1--P7/A1--A6. That would move the route from `UNDECIDED` to admitted.
2. The converter-on shared-objective path failed strict dominance or had a
   null/near-null in the licensed family. That would close this route for the
   current rig or force a different reference optic/claim band.
3. No reference-bearing scalar/interval contract could satisfy M167's strict
   blur ladder without rewarding USM or permitting compensation. That would
   close the presently specified statistical reduction even if the capture
   were available.
4. A separately validated generative model produced calibrated,
   instance-conditional fidelity decisions on representative macro data and
   passed M167. That would make the reference route not practically unique.
5. A previously omitted archive path supplied same-site observations with
   matched, disjoint A/B/V dark/flat lineage and sufficient acquisition
   records. That would overturn the archive-only closure.
6. Any committed source artifact used here failed reproduction or had been
   misread. The recorded source SHA-256 values and all 23 planted mutations are
   intended to expose that.
