## 2. Re-derivation under S2 and S3

### 2.1 S2 operationalisation of “at matched output”

The plan's exact phrase is “at matched output”
(`docs/proposals/2026-08-sota-path.md:1975-1978`). I operationalised it as:

- byte-identical hashed common linear input TIFFs;
- native-size 16-bit linear output;
- identical dimensions, crop, bit depth and frozen ICC;
- a pass on the affine tone-ramp sentinel;
- a pass on native-scale geometry and site retention; and
- no post-stack sharpening, denoising, resize, tone mapping or other
  post-processing.

Those requirements are already executable in the common-input, transfer and
geometry contracts
(`docs/proposals/2026-09-sota-scorecard.md:147-169,173-196,223-235`).
“Matched output” here means matched input/output representation and geometry,
not matched detail. It therefore contains neither a replacement-M1 score nor
the scorecard-authored `98%` threshold
(`docs/proposals/2026-09-sota-scorecard.md:93-98`;
`docs/proposals/2026-08-sota-path.md:2399-2406`).

This substitution removes `(a)` from M2, M3, M4 and M11 in S2. It does not
remove M5's own coherence/focus-margin test, M6's explicit
highlight-detail-retention member, or M9's explicit future-M1 truth.
