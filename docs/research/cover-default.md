# COVER — exact cover-mode evidence and default decision

**Status:** preregistered; measurement not yet run.

This lane uses bit identity and geometry only.  No repository sharpness metric
is admissible.  The authoritative predicates and stop rules were frozen in
[`experiments/cover-default/preregistration.md`](../../experiments/cover-default/preregistration.md)
before this lane ran its harness or opened a treatment render.

Prior evidence was known at registration: BUILD-CROP reported exact crop
windowing and a pyramid border artifact, while M95/M112 and the new
V-HEADTOHEAD3 cover arm refused the coverage rescale as a sharpness cause.
Those results motivate this audit but do not supply its measurements.
