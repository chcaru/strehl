# M164 — does aperture ordering identify resolved detail?

Status: **OBJECTION CORRECT; APERTURE ORDER IS A SCREEN, NOT A TRUTH
REFERENCE**

No capture was opened. In particular,
`D:\personal\macro\requests\7-aperture-pairs\` was not touched. This ruling
grants the proposal its strongest premise: the three arms are
diffraction-limited and their true optical resolving power is known a priori
to decrease strictly with f-number.

## 1. Verdict

**The objection is correct.** Recovering the known aperture order is necessary
for a generally useful detail statistic on this intervention, but it is not
sufficient to establish that the statistic distinguishes resolved scene
information from sharpening. A statistic can respond monotonically to both
true optical transfer and post-render edge amplification. Applying the same
sharpening operator to every aperture arm preserves the true aperture order,
so the statistic passes while still rewarding content that the optics did not
resolve.

The EXIF aperture is external information. The problem is not that
it came from metadata; the problem is that it supplies only a constraint on
the optical operators, not an independent observation of the latent scene or
the renderer's transfer. It narrows the admissible worlds without separating
the two worlds that matter.

## 2. Exact counterexample

Let `X` be the unknown scene, `H_i` the known diffraction transfer at aperture
`i`, and `K` a common convolutional sharpening transfer. The observed
aperture ladder can be generated by either:

```text
world A: Y_i = K H_i X       (ordinary scene, post-render sharpening)
world B: Y_i = H_i X', X'=KX (sharper latent scene, no postfilter).
```

Because common LSI transfers commute,

```text
K H_i X = H_i K X
```

for every aperture. The complete rendered triplet and every EXIF aperture
label are identical in the two worlds. In world A the extra high-frequency
amplitude is post-render sharpening; in world B it belongs to the latent
scene. No statistic receiving only that augmented observation can determine
which provenance is true.

`experiments/M164-aperture-identifiability/witness.py` instantiates this
construction without randomness. It uses the repository's f/5.6, f/8 and
f/11 diffraction cutoffs and a `0.25 cyc/px` sinusoid. At that frequency:

| marked aperture | Airy MTF | unsharpened amplitude score | common-USM amplitude score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| f/5.6 | `0.4456704781` | `0.0222835239` | `0.0314079461` |
| f/8 | `0.2404167769` | `0.0120208388` | `0.0169430051` |
| f/11 | `0.0452826354` | `0.0022641318` | `0.0031912246` |

Both score columns recover the correct aperture order. The invalid
sharpening increases every score by `1.409469446x`. More strongly, the
sharpened relative score vector is

```text
[1.0, 0.5394496353, 0.1016056428],
```

which matches the predicted relative Airy attenuation to
`1.11e-15`. Thus even the correct *relative magnitude*, not merely the rank,
survives a common sharpening transfer. The two constructed worlds produce
identical arrays to machine precision (`max error = 0`), while the sharpened
latent remains a valid intensity signal in `[0.429527, 0.570473]`.

This is a counterexample to sufficiency, not to usefulness. An instrument that
gets the aperture order wrong should be rejected. One that gets it right has
only survived this screen.

## 3. Can the aperture observation be strengthened?

### 3.1 Quantitative Airy magnitude

**Relative magnitude is still insufficient.** At one frequency,

```text
|Y_i| / |Y_j|
  = |K H_i X| / |K H_j X|
  = |H_i| / |H_j|.
```

The common unknown scene and the common sharpening transfer both cancel.
Matching the entire predicted attenuation ratio across apertures can therefore
coexist with a sharpening-sensitive score. The witness shows this to numerical
precision.

An **absolute** transfer calibration could help, but aperture metadata alone
does not supply it. Absolute calibration requires an independently known
input amplitude or reference transfer, plus the wavelength/effective-aperture,
sampling, tone, geometry and sensor pipeline needed to map the Airy prediction
into the scored image. Once those are supplied, the identifying information is
the calibrated reference-bearing experiment, not the aperture order by
itself.

### 3.2 Calibration against Airy scaling

Calibration against order or relative Airy scaling remains a screen for the
same cancellation reason. Calibration against an **absolute, independently
measured** input-output transfer can discharge the ambiguity within the
calibrated model and frequency range. It must be cross-fitted or otherwise
independent of the candidate output, and its assumptions limit the resulting
claim.

The Airy support boundary is useful as a local negative control. At a frequency
where the independently validated optical transfer is zero, repeatable output
structure cannot have arrived through that optical channel. That can veto
invented structure there. It does not certify provenance at frequencies where
the transfer is nonzero, nor does it turn the whole aperture ladder into a
truth image.

### 3.3 Sharpening saturation

A frozen sharpening sweep is a valuable sabotage arm. The witness sweeps USM
amounts `0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0`: the aperture order passes at every amount while
the amplitude score rises monotonically in every arm. The optical support is
unchanged. This catches that particular sharpening-sensitive statistic.

Saturation is not sufficient certification. A clipped, rank-only or simply
blind statistic can saturate under sharpening and still fail to reward a real
optical improvement. The saturation arm must therefore be paired with a
planted improvement, a planted degradation and a flat/null control. Even that
battery establishes behavior only for its registered intervention family
unless a structural argument or reference identifies the signal.

The data-processing statement is also narrower than the proposed heuristic:
postprocessing cannot create new information about the latent scene, but an
invertible filter may preserve information while changing amplitudes
arbitrarily. An output-only score cannot infer that information quantity merely
from the fact that its own value saturates.

### 3.4 Ruling on strengthened aperture use

No strengthening that uses only the aperture labels and their rendered outputs
discharges the hedge for unrestricted natural-scene detail. The ladder can
provide:

1. a necessary ordinal screen;
2. a relative-transfer model check;
3. an above-cutoff artifact veto where a validated OTF is zero; and
4. a controlled optical degradation arm in a broader acceptance battery.

It becomes identifying only when joined to additional information that breaks
the `X' = KX` ambiguity. Calling the ladder alone an external truth reference
would be incorrect.

## 4. What outside observation would discharge the hedge?

The general criterion is:

> An outside observation discharges the hedge only if the augmented
> observation map excludes two admissible latent/process states that produce
> the same observations while requiring opposite detail-versus-artifact
> rankings.

For this program, defensible examples are:

1. **A known signal target or independently sampled reference.** Measure a
   nonperiodic target whose spatial content is known, or acquire an independent
   sufficiently powered reference whose transfer is separately calibrated.
   Higher-resolution independent halves are the strongest route for absolute
   units, bias and power; a same-resolution independent reference can normalize
   relative transfer when its power and independence are established.
2. **Independent stochastic repeats plus featureless sentinels.** Repeats
   identify temporal noise; calibrated darks/flats remove deterministic sensor
   structure; featureless regions establish the no-scene null. These identify
   noise but still need the signal/transfer witness for a detail-preserving
   claim.
3. **A validated generative prior or physical constraint.** A prior that makes
   the latent signal/noise decomposition identifiable can select between the
   two worlds, but the conclusion is model-conditional and must be validated on
   the claimed population.
4. **A calibrated intervention that breaks the common-filter gauge.** Known
   input patterns, phase, or another independently measured transfer can do
   this. Merely adding more commuting aperture PSFs cannot.

The practical capture is therefore a reference-bearing, repeated,
calibrated-target experiment: fixed geometry and illumination; a known
nonperiodic signal across the claimed band; independent reference and output
halves; matching dark/flat pattern maps; frozen tone and registration; and
separate post-render blur and sharpening challenges. The aperture ladder may
be included as a strong falsification arm, but it is not the reference that
licenses the claim.

## 5. Program consequence

**KILL the claim that the three-point aperture ordering alone unblocks the
detail axis.** Retain the capture as a cheap screen and model check. Do not use
its successful ordering to admit a detail statistic, validate a single
render's high-frequency provenance, or promote the resampling-kernel repair.
Those decisions still require an identifying observation or assumption of the
class in Section 4.

This does not shrink the claim and does not retract the aperture physics. It
separates a true intervention label from a sufficient truth reference.
