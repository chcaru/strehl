### Counting rules

**INFERRED BY READING.**

- An axis **loses `(d)`** only when it currently carries literal `(d)` and the
  plan names no required comparator arm for that axis.
- An axis is **scoreable now** only when its population, statistic, bar,
  commissioned checker, admissible frozen inputs, candidate output/product, and
  all other prerequisites exist. “Defined,” “capture-unblocked,” and
  “commissioned” are individually insufficient.
- A **material governance addition** is a scorecard-authored cross-axis
  prerequisite, arm-eligibility rule, track restriction, or publication-verdict
  rule that can change `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNMEASURED`, `DEFINITION BLOCKED`,
  `DISCLOSURE MISS`, or the claim's scope.
- Exact bars, confidence reductions, population floors, plants, and other
  within-axis completions of plan placeholders are delegated scorecard work.
  They remain scorecard additions where marked, but are not silently promoted
  into the governance-invention count.
