### Blocker `(c)` and stabilisation

`(c)` is genuinely present. The already-frozen input-only audit found the bright
block at `0.064031 px`, but the second-light block at **`0.107703 px`** against
the hard `0.10 px` limit; an independent estimator read **`0.105202 px`** and
made the same decision
(`docs/research/M-PHASE0-SPLIT2.md:1-13,69-83`). The report's reopening rule is
explicit: a new stabilisation-off capture, or two independently passing light
blocks, is required (`:143-153`).

This is not a metadata-only suspicion. Frame-to-frame non-rigid scene motion leaks
scene structure into `O-R` in exactly the band called “noise,” while averaging can
also blur that structure. The direction need not be uniformly adverse, so it cannot
be corrected as a simple variance surcharge. The required second arm has already
failed the registered gate.

### What `replace_with_matched_output` actually does

M140 defines it as identical hashed common TIFF inputs; native-size 16-bit linear
outputs; matched dimensions/crop/bit depth/ICC; tone-ramp and geometry/site-retention
passes; and no post-processing
(`docs/research/M140-readiness.md:98-116`). Those conditions are representation,
transfer, and geometry matching. M140 says this plainly: it is **“not matched
detail”** (`:114-116`).

That is not an adequate replacement for M2's **matched-information** condition.
Blur or denoising can satisfy every listed matched-output condition and win the noise
ratio by deleting information. The current scorecard independently says:

- the M1/M2/M4/M8 joint tuple must pass together and “Until then neither a
  noise-only nor a detail claim is valid” (`2026-09-sota-scorecard.md:144`);
- “a future replacement M1 must remain green” in M2's own control (`:640-644`);
  and
- M2's comparator computation itself still says modes must be within `98%` of a
  future M1 envelope (`:628-631`).

The phrase M140 imports comes from the proposal's **speed** protocol
(`docs/proposals/2026-08-sota-path.md:1975-1978`), not a definition of equal
information for noise. Therefore `replace_with_matched_output` is satisfiable
without M1 only as a file/transfer qualification; it is **not** sufficient for the
claim named by M2. A replacement could be a separately commissioned local
information-preservation/blur guard, but none is registered.

M2 also lacks a verdict-level aggregation over ROIs/cross-fits and an RGB reduction,
so identical per-ROI data can produce different pass/fail answers
(`docs/research/M133-blocker-audit.md:749-770`).

**Verdict: `NEEDS_NEW_CAPTURE`.** A new eligible two-light repeat unit is unavoidable
even after the matched-information and aggregation defects are repaired.

## Defects in the scorecard/readiness derivation

1. **M2's readiness row is wrong about `(c)`.** The body requires both named
   32-frame light blocks, and one already fails the frozen geometry gate. Removing
   `(a)` and buying comparators cannot repair the input.
2. **M3's omission of `(c)` is unsupported.** Its body requires 500/50 qualifying
   edges and 400-px reach, while the current-position table says full-reach sites
   are absent. Existing capture sufficiency has not been demonstrated.
3. **The claim that rule 4 alone propagates `(a)` to M2/M3/M4/M11 is false on the
   literal current document.** M2/M4's joint tuple, M2/M3/M4's own controls, M3's
   explicit future-M1 clause, and M11's complete-quality qualification all create
   independent M1 dependencies (`2026-09-sota-scorecard.md:144-145,585-587,
   640-644,687-689,723-724,1089-1090`).
4. **The prompt's M140 field description is not what the artifact contains.**
   M2 has `s2_action: "replace_with_matched_output"` and
   `s3_action: "remove"`; `replace_with_matched_output` is the narrowed-rule S2
   treatment, not the rule-removed S3 treatment
   (`experiments/M140-readiness/readiness.json:364-368`).
5. **M140's M2 substitute changes “matched information” into matched file
   representation.** It explicitly says “not matched detail,” and its source phrase
   comes from the speed plan. It cannot prevent a blurred/denoised comparator from
   winning M2.
6. **The blocker arithmetic ignores executable-instrument defects:** M2 aggregation
   and channel semantics, M3's paired-p99 definition and absent checker, M4's
   undefined denominator/population, and M11's live-arrival contract.
7. **M4's capture language is internally unsafe.** It calls protected potential
   holdouts commissioning inputs without separately freezing scored flat inputs, and
   “bee background” does not identify a directory.
8. **Buying licences is not itself blocker `(d)` clearance.** Valid stable-product
   common-input outputs, transfer witnesses, and strehl's own TIFF adapter/identity
   boundary still have to exist.
