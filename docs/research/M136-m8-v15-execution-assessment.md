# M136 — M8-O v1.5 execution assessment

Date: 2026-09-04

**RULING:** The exact section-7 clamped/Crop/development-flat protocol can be
made executable without any owner capture, but its number would be diagnostic
commissioning evidence and cannot move the scorecard. The first **scoreable**
M8-O v1.5 number requires a separate common-merge shipping-default campaign
and a prospective ruling on which live holdout units remain admissible. The
current source does not prove that new photography is mandatory, but it also
does not license an owner-free scored render. There is no scoreable M8-O
movement executable now.

This assessment does not repeat or amend the already-landed M8-O v1.5 bar. It
answers only what execution would require and what the resulting number could
license.

## 3. What the development number could license

After every commissioning and provenance gate passes:

- `V=0` for every flat and aggregate would license that the exact private
  CE83/Crop/clamped/CA-off candidate clears the registered M8-O v1.5
  development census;
- any aggregate `V>=1` would be a real FAIL for that candidate under the
  registered predicate; and
- an `UNMEASURED` outcome would license no numerical conclusion.

Even a zero could not license:

- a shipping-default M8-O pass;
- an M8 axis pass;
- the landed M172 implementation, ordinary Catmull-Rom, `cover=scale`, CA-on,
  or any unmeasured configuration;
- quality, detail retention, or a product-default change;
- use of the three flats as holdout evidence; or
- any SOTA claim.

The full M8 decision is conjunctive. The same scored candidate must have
integer-zero M8-R, integer-zero M8-O v1.5, and integer-zero identity results.
The admitted shipping-default M8-R result remains
`63,191,176 / 6,131,678,976 = 1.030569%`, so a development M8-O zero cannot
change the current M8 `FAIL`.

## 4. What a scoreable v1.5 campaign requires

A scoreable campaign must not inherit the CE83 configuration and call it the
shipping default. It must use the scorecard's common-merge track, the sole
publication track: one frozen converter produces the common linear ProPhoto
TIFF inputs, and Strehl must consume those exact TIFF bytes through an adapter
or prove bit identity at its internal pre-merge linear-frame boundary. The
product-native RAW track may be published as product evidence, but it cannot
populate or alter the M8 publication gate.

The scoreable campaign requires:

1. a numbered scorecard revision that resolves the holdout-admission conflict
   and registers the scored population before any scored render;
2. one exact scored commit and the shipping defaults at that commit, with no
   experimental quality flag or per-subject override;
3. one frozen common-input TIFF manifest and an adapter or internal-boundary
   identity proof showing that the instrumented Strehl process consumed those
   exact bytes;
4. an instrumentation-only trace path that is output-identical to the
   uninstrumented product;
5. a checker and independent verifier for the shipping default's Scale output
   lattice, coordinate mapping, eligibility, denominator, arithmetic, and
   serialization, while retaining the separate Crop/reference identity
   control;
6. the same commissioned v1.5 predicate, exact `q=1/131070`, unchanged
   denominator rule, and zero floor;
7. end-to-end same-byte binding from source to compiler, executable to loader,
   common-input TIFF to adapter, trace/final TIFF/metadata to both checkers, and
   checker source to interpreter;
8. directional commissioning on the development-only flat stacks before any
   holdout output is read;
9. a pre-target freeze of the converter, common inputs, source, build, binary,
   settings, population, instruments, dependencies, and all hashes;
10. one uninterrupted run over every admitted scored quality capture; and
11. independent recomputation and exact per-capture plus aggregate publication
    of M8-R, M8-O v1.5, identity, rail disclosures, denominators, and every
    violating coordinate.

N6 still prohibits adopting a different resampling kernel as a route to an M8
pass while the detail blocker is open. The direct scoreable route is therefore
to measure the existing shipping default honestly, not to make the section-7
candidate the default because its diagnostic is favorable.

## 5. Owner-capture ruling

The scorecard was sealed by post-registration v1.1 renders of the registered
bee quality unit. It then changed the M8 instrument and replaced M8-O v1.4 with
v1.5. Its governing revision rule therefore applies: a later instrument, bar,
eligibility, comparator, capture, or aggregation change requires a numbered
revision and a fresh holdout capture; existing score outputs become
development-only
(`experiments/SCORECARD-BASELINE/out/RUN-MANIFEST.json`,
`RENDER-MANIFEST.json`, and `MEASUREMENTS.json`).

That rule does not define whether “fresh” means newly photographed or an
existing unit that remains unspent. The live scorecard affirmatively classifies
twelve rows as `HOLDOUT — QUALITY`, explicitly says the three fabric arms
remain sealed, and calls the battery ISO-100 arm protected. M136 has no
authority to erase those classifications by inference.

The same source record contains contrary evidence that must be adjudicated
before any scored render:

- the 576-frame bee unit was rendered and measured after the scorecard
  registration under the scorecard baseline
  (`experiments/SCORECARD-BASELINE/out/RENDER-MANIFEST.json` and
  `MEASUREMENTS.json`);
- the three fabric f/5.6, f/8, and f/11 arms have committed spectral/focus-map
  outcomes, and header evidence places them in the same capture sequence as
  the explicitly burned f/14 and f/18 arms
  (`experiments/M129-fabric-spectral-ladder/out/ladder.json`,
  `docs/research/M-PHASE0-LCA.md`, and
  `experiments/M141-capture-inventory/capture-stacks.csv`);
- seven top-level subjects have pre-registration masters, visual verdicts, and
  metric outcomes (`experiments/DELIVER-V6/BANK-33.md`, `docs/STATUS.md`, and
  `docs/research/V-ADJ13.md`), while the scorecard says pre-existing unrelated
  masters are not score outputs but does not say plainly whether that exception
  also overrides the later session-burn rule; and
- the battery ISO-100 row is labelled holdout, but shares a subject/session
  family with live control rows
  (`experiments/M141-capture-inventory/capture-stacks.csv`).

**RULING:** New owner photography is **not yet proved necessary**, because the
authoritative live table still labels twelve quality rows as holdout. It is
also **not yet proved avoidable**, because the bee render and the unresolved
session/outcome conflicts prevent M136 from certifying those rows as the fresh
population required after the later M8 changes.

Before a score run, the scorecard owner must make one prospective, explicit
ruling:

1. identify which existing units remain fresh and register them as the scored
   M8 population, in which case no new owner capture is needed; or
2. rule that the existing units are spent and register the required replacement
   capture or population, in which case owner shutter time is required.

Whichever route is selected, the admitted inputs must be hashed before any
render and protected from output, metric, visual, tuning, or code-acceptance
reads until the scored run.

## 6. What a scoreable number could license

For the frozen shipping-default commit and newly registered population:

- per-capture and aggregate `V_O=0` would pass the M8-O v1.5 component;
- any aggregate `V_O>=1` would fail that component; and
- an incomplete provenance, population, commissioning, or same-byte gate
  would leave it `UNMEASURED`.

That number would move M8-O v1.5 from `UNMEASURED` to a real component verdict;
the component may be reported even if another M8 conjunct fails. It would not
erase the historical v1.4 figure, change M8-R, or pass the M8 axis without
same-run zero M8-R and identity results. While M1 remains absent, even a
complete M8 pass licenses only the internal no-invention invariant, not a
detail or full SOTA claim.
